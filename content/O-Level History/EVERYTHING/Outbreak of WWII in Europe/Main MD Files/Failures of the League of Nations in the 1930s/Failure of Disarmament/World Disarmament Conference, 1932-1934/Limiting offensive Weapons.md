**LimConference** -> tried to control destructive power of offensive weapons
- produced resolutions to ban bombing of civilian populations + chemical warfare + limit size of artillery + tonnage of tanks

Leaders could not agree on a few matters
- difficult to get countries to agree on what offensive and defensive weapons were

- resolutions passed => not effective in ensuring => how limitations on weapons would be achieved. 
	- Bombing of civilians was to be banned => all attempts to agree to ban planes capable of bombing were unsuccessful. proposal to ban chemical weapons also unsuccessful

Individual countries => used conference to raise own problems
- Italy wanted navy same size as France
