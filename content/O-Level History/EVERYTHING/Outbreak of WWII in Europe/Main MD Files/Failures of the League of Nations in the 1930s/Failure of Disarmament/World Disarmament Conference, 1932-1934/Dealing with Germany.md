Biggest issue => what to do with Germany
- most countries shared view that terms set out in TOV should be fairer to Germany
	- terms limiting size of Germany's army => perceived to be unfair
	- there was disagreement to how fair arrangement should be achieved

Option 1: Every country disarm to a level that was similar to that Germany was forced to do so. 
Option 2: allow Germany to rearm to a level closer to that of other powers

-> reluctance to allow either option
Experience of disarmament efforts in 1920s => proved most countries would not disarm

France => wary of Germany => due to its painful experience in WW1 => pressure the league against taking up option 2
- demanded that:
	- either France continued to have an army greater in size compared to Germany, Britain, US
	- or Britain and US guarantee to fight alongside France if war between France and Germany broke out

Disagreements continued
- US did not want to commit themselves to guaranteeing France's future security
- at the same time => Germany accused France and other powers of Hypocrisy

July 1932 => Germany => put forward proposal => for other powers to disarm down to Germany's extent. 
-> conference failed to agree on the principle of 'equality' => Germans walked out + conference broke up
