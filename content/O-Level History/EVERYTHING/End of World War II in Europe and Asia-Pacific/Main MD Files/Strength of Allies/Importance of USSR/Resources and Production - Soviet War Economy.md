Industrial Prod. -> key factor in WWII. 

To ensure prod. kept up with high demand -> Soviets -> enacted Harsh measures
- Adults in USSR (male and female) -> got no food -> unless -> contributed to war effort. 
	- note: half workforce was female
- Forced labour used
- Secret police kept close control of population. 
___
Extraordinary results:
- USSR produced small range but reliable weapons -> huge quantities
	- Equalled + surpassed German war prod. -> 1942
	- 1943 -> $2\times$ tanks + $1.5\times$ aircraft as Germans
- Germans -> captured much of USSR -> agricultural + industrial capacity
	- Shifted major industries to unaffected areas
		- forced 25M workers -> migrate east -> which would contribute to increased war prod. in USSR.
