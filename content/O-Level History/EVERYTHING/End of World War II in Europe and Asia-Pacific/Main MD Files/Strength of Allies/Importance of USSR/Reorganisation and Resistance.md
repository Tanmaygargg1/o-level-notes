German advance -> halted by Russian winter -> late 1941
- Stalin + military leaders -> used this time -> reform Red Army completely. 
	- Copied many tactics + ideas -> used by Germans + added some of their own. 

1. Created specialist tank armies + air force
2. Officers -> given greater freedom + independence. Stalin banned CP officials -> from interfering with military decisions
3. Radio communications -> adopted. 
	- radios placed in all tanks and aircraft (supplied mainly by Br. and US)
4. Effective new weapons developed + produced in huge numbers
5. Harsh disciplined used
	- Stalin issued *order 227* -> soviet army ordered to fight or die trying for every bit of Russian soil

- Here -> soviets held them in fierce battle
