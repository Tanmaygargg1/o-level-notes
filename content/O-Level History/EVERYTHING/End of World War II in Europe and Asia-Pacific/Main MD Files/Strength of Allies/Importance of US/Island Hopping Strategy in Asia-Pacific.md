1937-1941 -> Japan -> built up large empire in Asia + Pacific. 

Even after US victory -> In battle of midway -> US + allies -> faced potentially lengthy + costly campaign against Japanese forces -> which had dug themselves into island strongholds.

Allies -> came up with strategy -> Island Hopping.
- involved using air attacks + submarines -> isolate Japanese-held areas -> rather than try to capture them
- Allies would then conquer the islands -> which were poorly defended.
	- then use them to further cut off ships -> that supplied well defended islands. 
- Idea -> was to cut off some areas from their supplies of food, ammunition, and other essentials.

This approach -> proved to be successful.
- allowed allies -> incur fewer casualties 
- come within striking distance of Japan itself -> in just a few years. 

(if allies had tried to conquer every Japanese stronghold -> it would have taken much longer)
