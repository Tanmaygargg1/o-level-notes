After battle of Midway 
- US + allies -> gradually -> drove Japanese back across Pacific from the territories they had taken.
	- used strategy known as island-hopping

US island hopping strategy -> led to several key victories -> 1942-1945
- such as in battle to regain Philippines -> which continued to weaken Japanese military.

Japans resources -> severely overstretched -> was not able to defend entire empire. 
- Japanese fought fanatically -> for each island -> in Pacific + each territory in China and Burma.

Soon -> war reached pivotal stage. 
- 1945 -> US raids on Japanese soil -> such as bombing of Tokyo.

April 1945 -> Br. and US forces -> took island of Okinawa
- Allies -> had to kill/capture -> all 100000 Japanese soldiers -> defending the island as none of them would surrender. 
Allied ships -> also badly damaged -> by Japanese kamikaze -> suicide bombers -> crashed their planes deliberately into them.
- attacks were used as last resort by Japanese. 
Although Japan was being pushed back -> came at great cost for Allies.


May 1945 -> US -> confident -> of winning war eventually.

US troops -> would have to beat Japanese -> island by island -> and then invade Japan itself.
Alternative: 
- international team of scientists in US -> working on what was known as the Manhattan project -> had perfected the worlds first atomic bomb

Truman -> had to deliberate whether or not to drop the bomb on Japanese. 
- also thought about whether or not he should warn the Japanese or arrange demonstration in unpopulated area.

- Decided to use bombs without warning
- accepted military advice -> dropped 2 bombs in quick succession.
- was to convince Japanese -> that US -> had large stockpile of weapons, which it did not

6 Aug 1945 -> American Bomber -> Enola Gay -> dropped 5 ton atomic bomb -> over Hiroshima.
9 Aug 1945 -> second atomic bomb -> over Nagasaki

-> both caused appalling damage + horrific casualties.
- reduce both cities to ruins
- also left legacy of cancer and other radiation related diseases among survivors

|                  | Hiroshima | Nagasaki |
| ---------------- | --------- | -------- |
| Total Population | 225 000   | 195 000  |
| Dead             | 66 000    | 39 000   |
| Injured          | 69 000    | 25 000   |
| Total casualties | 135 000   | 64 000   |

Japanese emperor -> Hirohito + most of his govt -> told army to surrender after Nagasaki bomb
- some military leaders disagreed -> attempted to overthrow emperor + continue war instead.
-> proved to many people on allied side -> that decision to use bombs was right.
-> Emperor's will prevailed -> Japan surrendered -> 14 Aug 1945
