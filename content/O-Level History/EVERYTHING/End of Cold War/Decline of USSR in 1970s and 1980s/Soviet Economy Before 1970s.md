Soviet economy -> based on idea -> state was in best position to make decisions about economy
- thus state decided which areas of economy should receive immediate investment and be developed
- controlled resources like oil, coal, energy. 
- also controlled production, prices, wages, and all other vital aspects of economy


For much of USSR existence -> command economy seemed to be achieving its main aims.
in 1920s -> USSR was relatively backward -> mainly rural economy
- most of population lived in poverty and squalor
- education was rare, and there were high rates of death from disease and alcoholism

1928-1940: USSR transformed into modern industrial power with impressive growth rates.
By 1940s: USSR was modern economy with industries, infrastructure like roads, railways, dams, and power stations and a developing public education system
- modernisation was tremendous achievement

As we have seen -> USSR -> also able to defeat NG in WWII -> despite immense loss of life + damage to property.
in 1950s + 1960s -> it saw continued economic growth.
- soviets had guaranteed jobs, good healthcare, place to live, free education to university level
