After impressive growth rates in 1950s and 1960s -> Soviet economy began to slow down.
- USSR began to experience steady long term decline in growth

Late 1970s -> industrial output had dropped from double digit percentage growth to a 3-4% decline.
similarly -> output growth for Soviet agriculture dropped from 4.8% per cent a year in 1950s to 1.8% in 1970s.

Future soviet leader Mikhail Gorbachev -> later called this period "Era of Stagnation"

* all other economies increased from 1970 to 1990 (US, West Germany. Japan) and USSR declined
