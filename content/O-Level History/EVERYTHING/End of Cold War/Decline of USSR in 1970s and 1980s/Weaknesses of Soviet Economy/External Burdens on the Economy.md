- Mujahideen -> saw themselves as fighting against the communist regime
- make matters worse for USSR -> they knew the territory + had plenty of experience in guerrilla warfare + were supplied with weapons and money by US and Saudi Arabia


USSR invested far too much -> in its military power.
- particularly -> nuclear weapons program + war in Afghanistan were very expensive
- USSR never revealed figures -> est. 30-50% of Soviet resources when to military
