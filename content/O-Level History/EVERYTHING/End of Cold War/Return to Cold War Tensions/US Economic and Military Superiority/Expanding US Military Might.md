- booming econ -> gave Reagan + allies -> confidence + money -> to champion US values of capitalism and democracy
- Reagan believed in taking tough time with USSR wherever possible + increasing US defence spending.
- he supported anti communist forces in Afghanistan and Nicaragua.
- he increased US defence budget massively.
	- first 2 weeks in office -> increased defence budget by 32.6bil
	- new weapon systems such as B1 nuclear bomber were introduced


- 1982 -> gave the go ahead for the strategic defence initiative (SDI, known as Star Wars Programme) 
- was multi bil $ project to create system that could use satellites and lasers to destroy missiles before they hit their targets.
- this system could change nature of nuclear war

- SDI did not develop into viable weapon
- however it put pressure on Soviets -> who were never sure whether weapon would become reality.
- USSR already finding it increasingly diff -> to keep up with US, given its stretched resources. 
- Renewed arms spending would ruin it


- Reagan had huge impact on development of cold war
- his charisma -> made him popular + allowed him to advance tough stance of US against USSR -> which was supported by many WE leaders./ 
- he was also willing to spend huge amts of weapons => leaving US with 4 trillion of debt by 1980s
