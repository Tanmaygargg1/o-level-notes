- Reagan -> radical ideas for US domestic policy. 
- like his close ally -> British PM Margaret Thatcher -> believed that ppl + businesses -> needed to be liberated from govt regulation
- both believed that govt regulations -> stifled trade and innovation in businesses + held back economic expansion. 
- they emphasized that govt should cut taxes. 
- this would give ppl an incentive to work harder + start their own businesses since govt -> would not be taken their hard earned wealth

- Both advocated -> that govts should spend less + limit their involvement in ppls life (such as through welfare or state healthcare) -> as far as possible
- believed that ppl and free market -> better than govts at providing solutions to problems

- other important European leaders -> supported Reagan -> although not to the same degree as Thatcher. 
- in West Germany -> e.g. -> Chancellor Helmut Kohl introduced measures to free businesses from regulations

- Liberation policies -> contributed to economic boom in US and WE -> even if they were not necessarily the main cause
