- Jan 1990 -> Gorbachev visited Baltic state of Lithuania -> part of USSR
- leaders made clear that they wanted independence 
- they did not want to be part of USSR
- Gorbachev -> uncompromising
- would not allow this
- March -> Lithuania declared independence nonetheless. 
- through it all -> Russian Pres. Boris Yeltsin emerged as key figure


Change in Soviet policy from Brezhnev Doctrine to Sinatra Doctrine -> saw shift from centralised Moscow control to freedom for EE states to choose their own govt
