During Cold War -> US and USSR never took fatal step of fighting direct war with each other.
- however both sides + whole world lived in constant fear of this possibility -> esp. one that involved nuclear weapons.

Thus -> by 1960s -> 2 sides exploring ways to reduce tensions. 
- led to *detente*  (easing of tensions)
- improve relations + even cooperate in areas like arms reduction and trade. 
	- key mover of detente: US pres. Richard Nixon

**Symbol of Detente**
-> during this period: joint space venture known as *Apollo Soyuz test project was launched*
- Jul 1975 -> millions of ppl around the globe witnesses on TV -> historic moment when US Apollo spacecraft docked with Soviet Soyuz capsule
- mission commanders of respective spaceships exchanged -> first international handshake in space
	- moment widely regarded as first symbol of detente between 2 superpowers

**Limits of Detente**
although relations improved during detente period -> there were too many areas of disagreement between US and USSR + too much pressure exerted on both countries.
- internal politics rivalries in US led several pres. -> esp. Jimmy Carter and Ronald Reagan -> to adopt tougher approaches toward USSR

20 years after start of detente -> cold war ended and USSR ceased to exist.
