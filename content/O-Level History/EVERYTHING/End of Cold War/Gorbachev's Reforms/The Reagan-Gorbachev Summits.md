US pres. Reagan -> watched developments in USSR -> with great interest


- was shocked to discover that soviets had almost launched nuclear strike against US in 1983
- malfunction in soviets early warning system made them think the US had fired missiles at USSR. 
- Reagan keen keen to advance nuclear disarmament + tamp down strategic arms race. 
- these priorities led Reagan to make overtures to soviet leaders and to eventually engage Gorbachev in negotiations with shared commitment to nuclear abolition


1985-1988 -> Reagan and Gorbachev had 5 summit meetings
end of these summits -> reached agreements to further reduce nuclear weapons
Reagan declared -> he no longer thought USSR was an "evil empire" bc of changes Gorbachev was making
