in face of challenges -> USSR's leaders decided -> change necessary
1985 -> USSR elected 54 year old Mikhail Gorbachev -> to be leader -> following death of Konstantin Chernenko -> who led USSR for less than 14 months after succeeding Yuri Andropov in early 1984


Gorbachev -> aware -> terrible prob facing USSR:
- declining econ
- war in Afghanistan
- disillusionment of Soviet ppl
- challenge of US under Reagan

Despite this -> he believed USSR could be transformed with new ideas and new thinking.
___
Gorbachev believed -> comm rule should make life better for ppl
- while travelling in 1970s -> he had seen Western Europe had higher standards of living than USSR. 
- also upset him -> that rather than loyalty/trust toward govt -> many soviets felt only resentment at way it controlled their lives

He did not intend to dismantle comm in USSR and EE.
- but did not want to reform it radically

-> described ideas and policies as New thinking
