- Gorbachev -> called for open debate on govt policy + honestly in facing up to all problems
- Glasnost not detailed set of policies -> but it did mean radical change

e.g. -> would allow open political debate by Comm Party members -> who disagreed with govt.
would also allow criticism of govt in media

___
Backfired -> released much resentment against comm govt.
Glasnost also exposed shortcomings of govt officials -> such as inefficiency / corruption.
- led to decreasing confidence in party

soviets also further exposed to aspects of outside world -> esp. better quality of life in US and WE countries -> through popular Western Culture


Result -> Positive picture of soviet life that govt previously presented to public -> fell apart.
- many in soviet bloc -> felt deceived + lost confidence in vision + rule of comm govts.
- hence instead of rallying the people behind reforms of 'openness' -> glasnost had unintentionally -> created platform which unleashed criticism of communist rule -> eventually bought abt disintegration of USSR
