* Nixon stepped down sa US pres in 1974
* successor, Gerald Ford -> continued policy of detente
* Ford succeeded by Jimmy Carter in 1977 -> who after USSR's invasion of Afghanistan in 1979 -> abandoned detente + took much more aggressive approach toward USSR 
* same time -> Brezhnev began to think USSR was not gaining much from detente either


Both sides began to criticise each other actions again + accused other of breaking promises
* carter blamed USSR for not settling disputes peacefully. 
	* according to carter -> USSR making things worse -> by suppling arms to allies in war zones such as *angola and ethopia*
* Brezhnev countered that US was doing pretty much same in Central and South America + South Africa

Also blamed each other of trying to get around various nuclear arms limitation treaties.

final nail in coffin of detente -> was the soviet invasion of Afghanistan in 1979. 

when carter heard about invasion -> described it as "the most serious threat to peace since 2nd world war"

in some ways => detente was like plaster on scars caused by the cold war rivalry.
only matter of time -> before plaster fell off.
behind apparent good relations during detente period -> some major forces at work.
