late 1960 + early 1970 -> US pres Richard Nixon and Soviet Leader Leonid Brezhnev -> seemed willing to consider possibility of having less hostile r.s.

**Why Detente**
both men -> concerned abt risk of direct conflict + financial cost of rivalry.
- wanted to invest more in their own economies + welfare of ppl. 
- this combination of risk and cost which drove detente

at same time -> Detente presented by both sides in positive light + as act of goodwill
some historians view detente as motivated more by superpowers desire to achieve their aims in less costly + risky ways
