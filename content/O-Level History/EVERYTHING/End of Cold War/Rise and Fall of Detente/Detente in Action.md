During detente period -> US and USSR -> became open to cooperating with each other + with other less friendly countries

* began visiting each other's countries and meeting to discuss important issues -> like nuclear weapons, conflicts around world, trade, technology. 
	* meetings known as *summit meetings* -> as they involved leaders of countries. 

**Key Summit Meetings During 1970s**


| Date          | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Feb 1972**  | Nixon Became First US President to visit China since it turned comm 1949<br><br>continuation of progress made in 1971 -> when US table tennis team -> became first group of Americans allowed to enter China -> since comm takeover in 1949                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **May 1972**  | Nixon Visited Brezhnov in Mascow -> sign a document called *basic principles*<br>superpowers agreed to prevent dangerous exacerbation in their relations -> by doing utmost to avoid military confrontations -> to prevent outbreak of nuclear war + to exersise restraint in mutual relations<br><br>both leaders also signed strategic arms limitation treaty (SALT I), limiting each sides production of nuclear weapons |
| **June 1973** | Brezhnev visited US to sign agreement on prevention of nuclear war -> in which both sides agreed to do all they could to avoid nuclear war                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **June 1974** | Nixon went to Moscow again -> discuss ways to strengthen collaboration in many areas - esp in economic aspects + further disarmament and possible SALT II                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
