25th June 1950 -> North Korea invaded South Korea.
- Truman -> immediately sent advisors + supplies + warships -> to waters around Korea. 
- At same time -> he put enormous pressure -> on United Nations security council -> to condemn North Korea’s actions + to call on them to withdraw their troops. 

Truman -> would have prob intervened -> in Korea -> even without UN support -> but he obtained it as circumstances favoured him. 
Like US -> USSR had power to veto any decision made by UN. 
- however it was boycotting UN then -> as it was protesting UN’s refusal to grant membership to communist China after -> Chinese Civil War. 

Thus USSR -> was not present -> to use its veto -> when resolutions on situation in Korea were passed. 
As US -> was biggest contributor to UN budget -> it was in a position to influence the UN decision. 
UN -> was now committed -> to using member forces to drive North Korean troops -> out of South Korea. 
US was also largest contributor of troops to UN forces -> which were under the command of US general -> General Douglas MacArthur

UN forces -> included troops from other countries -> Turkey, Philippines, Thailand, India -> they fought alongside thousands of south Korean troops. 
while some countries sent combat troops, others provided medical services or transportation.
