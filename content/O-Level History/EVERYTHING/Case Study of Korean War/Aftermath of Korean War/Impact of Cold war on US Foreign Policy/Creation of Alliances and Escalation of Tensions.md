- Korean war -> greatly affected US policies + attitudes -> particularly in Asia
- most significantly -> US -> was freshly determined -> to pursue its policy of containment in Asia. 
- South Korea, Japan, Taiwan -> became central to US policy in Asia. 
- US -> stationed troops + significant air power + naval ships -> in South Korea and in Japan
___
- US -> also extended its network of alliances -> beyond NATO. 
- 1954 -> sets up a network of anti communist alliances -> around world:
	- Southeast Asia Treaty Organisation (SEATO) in SEA. 
	- Central Treaty Organisation (CENTO) in Central Asia + Middle East. 
- US gave money, advice, arms -> to these allies. 
- In return -> leaders of these countries -> suppressed communist influence at home.
___
- USSR saw-> these alliances as aggressive. 
- Accused US -> of trying to encircle communist world. 
- To counter NATO -> USSR set up *Warsaw Treaty Organisation* -> better known as Warsaw Pact -> 1955
- This included USSR + all other communist Eastern European countries except Yugoslavia
