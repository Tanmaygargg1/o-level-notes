- result of Korean war -> US intensified military power -> to enforce policy of containment by military means if necessary
- Before 1950 -> US -> had been scaling down its forces. 
- It had been fighting cold war primarily thru political activity + media -> but by 1951 -> US armed forces had doubled in size compared to the previous year. 


- US’s NATO -> allies -> took a similar approach. 
- European military power grew dramatically
- When korean war began -> NATO countries had only 14 army decisions + spent approx. 5.5% of their Gross Domestic Product (GDP) on the military.
- By the time war ended -> NATO -> had 15 divisions -> stationed in West Germany alone + NATO countries were spending more than 12% of their GDP on defence.
