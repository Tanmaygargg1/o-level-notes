- May 1948 -> 80% of eligible population -> took part in national election -> in *area south of 38th Parallel*
- US backed *Syngman Rhee* for position of president
	- as Rhee had campaigned for Korean independance -> during Japanese occupation. 
	- However he had not lived in Korea for some time. 
	- US favoured Rhee -> primarily because he was deeply anti communist, fluent English speaker, and graduate from prestigious universities in US

After gaining Majority of votes in election -> Rhee became president of newly founded *Republic of Korea (or South Korea)* -> Aug 1948. 
Furthermore -> US forces -> were to withdraw from South Korea by June 1949.

Over years -> Rhee -> grew increasingly authoritarian -> arresting + even killing opponents -> including suspected communists. 
- despite disagreements with Rhee’s policies => US continued to support Rhee and his govt -> due to their anti communist stance -> so as to prevent spread of communist influence from North. 

-> similar to Kim -> Rhee also believed that Korea should be unified.
