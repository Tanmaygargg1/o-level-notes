**Following WWII -> important developments took place in korea**
- Towns and villages -> locals formed political committees -> to maintain order + replace Japanese govt + Korean officials who had served Japanese

Lot of bitterness left over from years -> when country were occupied by Japanese.
in many areas -> people sought to take revenge against those who had collaborated with Japanese. Would have been very difficult to unite country

US and USSR -> met several times from 1946 to 1947 -> to determine way of administering Korea after war.
- although agreed that it should be unified under a provisional govt -> they differed on how to unite and administer it -> as both were wary of each other. 

1947 -> United Nations -> called for free elections to be held in korea -> to establish govt for a *unified korea*
