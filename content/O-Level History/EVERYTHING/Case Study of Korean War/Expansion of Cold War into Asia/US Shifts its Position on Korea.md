- By April 1950 -> US defensive perimeter -> was effectively expanding.
- US -> was changing position to oppose any attempt to spread communism in Asia
- US state department -> drew up -> top secret report -> known as *National Security Council Paper No. 68 (NSC-68)* 
- Set out a more assertive + aggressive US foreign policy -> to deal with communist threat. 
___
- Report -> prove decisive for Korea -> as situation was becoming more dangerous -> with rising tensions and clashes -> between troops across the border.
- US policy before -> was simply -> let North and South Korea -> fight it out -> if war broke out between them. 
- With drafting of NSC-68 -> Truman would have been inclined to intervene if war broke out
- he could not risk -> possibility of victory by North Korea -> which would be triumph for communism + humiliation for US.
