Following end of WWII -> Chinese civil war resumed -> as *Mao Zedong's* Chinese Communist Party (CCP) -> fought against *Chiang Kai-Shek's* GMD -> for control over China
- CCP was victorious -> China became communist state -> October 1949
	- US was concerned about implications of China turning communist

1946-1949 -> US -> pumped 2billion -> aid -> into China -> mainly to support GMD nationalist under Chiang. 

By Dec 1949 -> GMD govt -> defeated. 
- they established themselves on island of *Formosa(Taiwan)* -> which is off Chinese mainland.
- With victory of CCP -> there was now massive new communist state on map

US -> fearful -> that communism -> might start spreading -> throughout Asia.
Hence -> refused to accept legitimacy -> of CCP govt -> continued to back GMD govt in Taiwan

Naturally -> Soviets -> delighted -> with communist victory -> in China
- they had support Mao and his movement -> during Chinese civil war
- now they had powerful new ally against US

-> Feb 1950 -> 2 communist powers -> signed *Sino Soviet treaty of friendship, alliance, and mutual assistance 
- came a month after Acheson's announcement of US defensive perimeter

Sino Soviet treaty -> set out various agreements 
- about land and transport in East Asia
- also involved cumulative loan of 300 million from USSR -> to help China rebuild after years of war
