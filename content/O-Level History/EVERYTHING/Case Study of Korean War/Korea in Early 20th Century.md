- for much of its history -> Korean Peninsula -> controlled by neighbouring countries -> like China and Japan -> partly because of its strategic position in East Asia

Japan -> was rising power in Asia -> in late 19th and early 20th centuries -> aggressively extending its control over the region.
- 1905 -> Korea became a *protectorate* of Japan
- 1910 -> Japan *annexed* Korea
___
Japanese -> ruled Korea -> with iron fist.
- Police + military -> had total power -> Koreans had very few rights.

-> there was great deal of resentment towards Japanese.
- though it was difficult + dangerous to oppose Japanese rule -> some Koreans resisted

Despite crushing resistance movements brutally -> Japanese continued to fight against them through 1920s and 1930s. 
- still it proved impossible to undermine Japanese control of Korea
___
Resistance to Japanese rule -> intensified with outbreak of WWII. 
- some resistance movements -> had support of communists in China and USSR. 
- resistance groups -> were courageous + committed -> but still failed -> to make much of an impact on Japanese rule. 

With differing aims and ambitions for an *independent Korea* -> different groups were deeply divided. 
___
One resistance group -> led by *Kim II Sung* -> particularly important. 
- Kim was deeply committed communist -> who had been trained in political and military thinking in the USSR. 
- His forces -> fought tenacious guerrilla war -> against the Japanese -> until they were forced to flee to communist controlled areas -> in China -> 1939
- this struggle -> proved to be important later -> helped Kim gain support of North Koreans after war
___
As WWII neared end in 1945 -> Japanese were facing defeat -> by allies.
leaders of allies -> met at [[Potsdam Conference, July-Aug 1945]] 
- agreed that Korea should eventually be given full independance. 
- did not specify what form this independance would take
___
Allies agreed -> that once Japanese defeated -> Korea would be temporarily occupied -> until arrangements for new govt could be put in place .
US forces -> would govern southern part of Korea 
Soviet troops -> govern northern part of Korea.
dividing line would be the *38th parallel*
