Warsaw pact -> strengthened -> USSR -> effectively gave USSR -> command of all armies of the satellite states.
Inevitably -> tensions increased -> because Europe now divided into 2 armed camps.

Next 35 years -> each side -> put much energy into plans -> to stop a possible invasion by the other side. 
- e.g. -> 1000s of US troops, tanks, aircrafts, weapons -> stationed in bases across Europe -> particularly west Germany
- Return -> USSR and other Warsaw pact members -> trained their military forces -> and stationed troops in each member's territory in case NATO attacked.

Warsaw Pact -> was significant -> also because it entrenched Soviet rule -> in Eastern Europe.
Soviet troops, rather than respective govt -> were ones who dealt with protests + uprisings in Eastern Europe.
- e.g. 4th Nov 1956 -> nation wide revolt -> in Hungary -> was forcefully quelled by Soviet tanks and troops.
- 1000s were killed + injured + nearly 1/4 million Hungarians -> fled its shores. 
- Similar protests -> in East Germany, Czechoslovakia, Romania -> crushed in same way by Soviet Foprces.
