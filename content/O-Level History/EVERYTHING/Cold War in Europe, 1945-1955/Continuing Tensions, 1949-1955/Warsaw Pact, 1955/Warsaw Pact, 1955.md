1955 -> West Germany -> invited to join NATO -> along with its rearming, it proved to be too much for USSR.

Stalin died in 1953 -> but East-West tensions remained.
USSR did not believe that West Germany joining NATO -> was anything but a threat.

In response -> USSR created -> Warsaw pact in -> May 1955.
- all communist states of Eastern Europe, *except Yugoslavia* -> joined.
- all members agreed that if one was threatened, they would help to defend it
