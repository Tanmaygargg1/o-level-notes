>[!note]
>tensions between 2 countries not just about differing ideology
>US and USSR -> had history of suspicion + mistrust going back to 1917 > when Russia became communist

- USSR communist vision -> aligned to *communist manifesto* -> called for worldwide communist revolutions -> to overthrow capitalist states. 
	- US + western European democratic countries ->  alarmed -> feared communism would spread in their countries + cause similar revolutions

- At PPC 1919 -> decided that Germany would not be broken up. [[Paris Peace Conference and League of Nations Main Note]] 
	- 1 reason -> Br + US -> worried -> about possibility of new communist state in Russia + its consequences. 
		- though Germany could be barrier to communism -> highlighting their suspicion toward Russia.

- 1918-1921 => bitter civil war -> fought in Russia -> between communists and their opponents -> known as whites. 
	- US + Br troops -> intervened on side of whites -> hoping to prevent communist victory

- 1920s: *Red scare* in US.
	- US govt -> arrested / imprisoned many people -> usually immigrants from Russia -> accusing them of being communist
	- most were innocent

- 1930s: Br + Fr -> had [[Policy of Appeasement in the 1930s]] -> toward Hitler
	- (to some extent) Policy was driven by fear -> that USSR was trying to spread communism by force. 
	- Br hoped -> Hitler's Germany -> would oppose communism
	- to USSR -> appeasement -> threatened its security -> seemed to imply that Hitler was allowed to take over Eastern Europe.

- USSR signed [[Nazi Soviet Non-Aggression Pact, 1939]] with Germany. 
	- promising not to attack each other. 
	- such pact -> surprised US + world -> because earlier in year -> USSR was in discussion -> with Britain and France -> with regard to formation of an alliance against Hitler. 
	- nothing came out of that discussion HAHA
