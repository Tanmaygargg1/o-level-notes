>[!note]
>atmosphere at Potsdam Conference very diff from atmosphere of compromise during previous conferences. 

Stalin -> still leader for USSR -> continued to push for his demands
Leaders of both US and Br -> changed. 
- US now led by Harry Truman, as Pres Roosevelt had died in April 1945

- Truman -> did not have same r.s. with Stalin -> that Roosevelt had -> as he assumed power after war time alliance -> between USSR and US -> had broken down.
- was concerned about potential threat -> USSR posed -> to security of Western Europe 
- was thus determined -> to get touch + challenge Stalin -> on the big issues. 

In Britain -> Churchill -> lost general election in July 19456 -> replaced by Clement Attlee -> halfway through conference.
___
Both Truman + Attlee -> young + more inexperienced -> in foreign affairs -> compared to predecessors.
