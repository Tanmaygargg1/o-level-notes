Truman -> wanted to use -> US's mighty economic power -> to prevent spread of communism.
- ordered US secretary of state -> George Marshall -> to come up with an economic aid plan. 
- June 1947: Marshall plan -> was announced to world. 

US -> would put 13 billion -> into helping Europe's shattered economies recover.
- Starting April 1948: food, machinery, animals and countless other items -> were shipped to democratic countries in Europe. 
- Truman -> wanted people to get back to work + make money. 
- wanted to make them feel good abt. capitalism -> so they would not be attracted to communism.

Marshall plan -> widely seen -> by US's allies -> as incredibly generous gesture ->were extremely grateful.
- however important to remember that US -> looking after its own interests.
___
First -> US -> needed to recover -> so that its industries -> had market -> to export their goods to. 

Second -> largest amts. of financial aid from Marshall Plan-> went to US's closest political allies -> Britain and France.

Third -> countries which did not have democratic, capitalist systems -> did not receive such financial support. 
- e.g. Italy did not receive any aid -> until non communist govt took power in 1948.
___
Marshall Plan -> key development -> in Cold war.

In economic terms -> it provided massive boost to Western Europe + brought about speedy recovery for countries.

Britain + France benefitted -> and so did war ravaged West Germany -> to the extent that west Germany enjoyed an 'economic miracle' by early 1950s. 
- helped to bind the US and its allies closer against communism
