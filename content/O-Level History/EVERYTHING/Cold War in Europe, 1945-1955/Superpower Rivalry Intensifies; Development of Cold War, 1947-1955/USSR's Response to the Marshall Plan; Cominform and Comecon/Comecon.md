Comecon set up => in response to Marshall Plan.
- Stalin wanted to make sure satellite states -> were free from any kind of control / influence from the US.

Idea was that Comecon -> members would trade mostly with one another rather than the west. 

Satellite states -> promised benefits -> similar to Marshal plan.
Reality -> Comecon -> favoured -> USSR far more than any of its other members.
- e.g. Poland was forced to sell its coal to the USSR -> at 1/10 the price -> it could have sold on the open market.
