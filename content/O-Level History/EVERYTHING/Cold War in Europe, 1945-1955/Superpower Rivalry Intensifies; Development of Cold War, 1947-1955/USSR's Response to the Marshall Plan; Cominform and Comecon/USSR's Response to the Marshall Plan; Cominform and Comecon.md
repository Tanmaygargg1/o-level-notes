Stalin -> appalled + angered by Marshall plan.
- seemed to him -> that US was tying Western Europe to it -> in same way the USSR had controlled Eastern Europe. 
But to Stalin -> difference was US did not need defensive buffer zone -> in way that USSR did.

He was concerned -> that domination of Western Europe -> was first step for US to advance into Eastern Europe + threaten Soviet control there.
Addition -> Stalin wondered:
- if Truman's involvement in Germanys quick recovery was due to latter's intention to use Germany against USSR

Stalin -> could do little about -> what was happening in Western Europe -> but he tightened grip on Eastern European states + ordered them to have nothing to do with Marshall Plan.
- then he turned them into -> *satellite states* -> which were forced to stay close to the USSR.
- did this by creating 2 new organisation -> *communist information Bureau (Cominform)* and *Council for Mutual Economic Assistance (Comecon)*
