Members in Cominform -> included leaders of communist parties in Eastern Europe.
Set up in 1947 -> Cominform -> published propaganda -> that would unite communist states -> in Europe -> and ensured that satellite states implement a soviet style communism -> and trade mainly with other Cominform countries. 

Stalin used Cominform -> to tighten his control over the satellite states + ensure that US could not gain any political influence in them.

At Cominform meetings -> Stalin told Eastern European states -> which policies they should follow.
- included rejecting financial support -> from Marshall Plan + cutting off trade and any other kind of contact with countries -> that did not belong to Cominform.

There was no real debate within Cominform.
- only state which argued with Stalin was Yugoslavia -> led by Marshal Tito. 
- Yugoslavia -> was expelled from Cominform in 1948 -> and there was bitter, long lasting feud between him and Stalin
