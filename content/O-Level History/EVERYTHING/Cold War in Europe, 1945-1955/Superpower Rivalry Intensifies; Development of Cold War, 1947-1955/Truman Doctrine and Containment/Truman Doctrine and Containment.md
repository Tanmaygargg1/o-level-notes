1946-1947: relations between US and USSR -> steadily worsened.
- Truman continued to take tough line against Stalin
- One of the documents that influenced his perception -> was *the long telegram*. 
eventual result was an announcement in March 1947 -> became known as the *Truman Doctrine*

- Truman -> announced in a speech -> that US -> would provide money + military equipment + advice -> to democratic countries perceived to be threatened by communist influences. 
- Truman doctrine -> was beginning of *policy of containment* - attempt to stop the spread of communism. 
- Was introduced as a result of events in Turkey and Greece
