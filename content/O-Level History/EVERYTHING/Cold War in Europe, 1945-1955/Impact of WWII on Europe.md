### Europe in Ruins
By time Germany surrendered in May 1945 -> much of Europe -> shattered + devastated war zone. 
- Around 36 million Europeans -> killed
- Much of infrastructure of roads, railways, bridges, canals -> destroyed by fighting or bombing
- No Gas, electricity, or running water in many areas
- Disease + Malnutrition -> widespread -> were desperate shortages of food and medicine
- Homelessness was huge problem -> over 50 million people -> lost homes / had been forced to leave homes
___
### New Political Landscape
- war and events that followed -> led to fundamental shifts in balance of power in international politics. 
- factors continue to shape development of cold war

##### Decline of Western European powers
1939 -> 3 great European powers; Br., Fr., Germany.

- By end of war -> even though Britain was never invaded/defeated -> it had exhausted resources of its people, industries, and empire during war
	- became virtually bankrupt

- 1940: France -> occupied by Germany. 
	- liberated in 1944 -> but was no longer the great power it had once been

- By 1945 -> Germany lay in ruins after defeat in WWII

-> thus in post war Europe -> Britain, France, Germany -> would no longer be dominant players on the world stage

##### Emergence of Superpowers
- Both US and USSR -> emerged from war as superpowers
- Due to geographical size + population + economic output + military strength
	- they continued to possess these points after war
