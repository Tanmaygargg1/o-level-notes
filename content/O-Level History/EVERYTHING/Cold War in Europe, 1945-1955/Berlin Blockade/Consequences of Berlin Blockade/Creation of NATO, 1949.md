at height of berlin blockade -> Western powers -> met in Washington -> and signed agreement to work together.
- resulted in *North Atlantic Treaty Organisation (NATO)* -> in April 1949
___
There would be many parts to treaty. 
- Most important -> was that all NATO members -> agreed to go to war if any member was attacked
- Truman wanted to make it clear -> to Stalin -> that US would intervene in Western Europe if necessary

NATO countries -> would provide US forces -> with secure bases in Europe. 
US allies such as Britain -> were happy -> to have US in Western Europe -> as it guaranteed protection from a possible Soviet attack.

Not surprisingly -> USSR -> did not see alliance in same way. 
- Soviets -> saw it as a threat -> by the Western powers -> that split the world into 2 military blocs
