1944 -> US -> drew up *Morgenthau Plan* -> involved breaking up Germany + dismantling its industries -> so that it would never be strong enough to wage war again.
- implementation of 4 zones of occupation in Germany -> took reference from that plan

- However with increasing tensions between East and West from 1945 onwards -> Truman began to see -> Germany -> as possible future ally against USSR. 
- Br. + Fr. -> were also keen -> for Germany -> to start rebuilding its economy -> as controlling their zones was a drain on resources. 
- Early 1948 -> US, Fr, Br -> combined their zones -> into single economic unit -> known as *Trizone*
- new currency was also introduced. 

To Stalin -> seemed that US -> was rebuilding his old enemy. 
- he could not stop allies -> but thought he could make a point by forcing them out of Berlin
- June 1948 -> he carried out Berlin blockade -> during which the USSR cut rail + road links -> from allied zones to west Berlin. 
- also disrupted water and power supplies.
- seemed like clever scheme
- if allies stayed in Berlin -> people there would suffer

But Truman -> not prepared to be driven out of Berlin
- to counter blockade -> he ordered Berlin Airlift. 
- For almost year -> US and allies -> supplied West Berlin with -> coal + food + medicine by air. 
- While western media -> praised courage + endurance of air crews -> Soviet media criticised the constant flights as unsafe. 
- Stalin eventually gave up -> lifted the blockade in -> May 1949
