education system => already had strong emphasis on national pride + importance of Japanese traditions. 
=> also stressed Shinto Beliefs, loyalty to the emperor, and the community over the individual. 
Govt => continued to strengthen their control of Japan thru education

1937 => Ministry of education => published document => *Fundamentals of our national policy* => used it as a basis for Japans education system.
- document was distributed to officials and educators => was intended to guide teachers in calculating Japanese traditional ethics in their students. 
___
Japanese curriculum => sought to instil sense of patriotism in young Japanese and was centred on ultranationalist and militarist ideals.
- also contained large amounts of physical exercise, military style drilling and technical lessons designed to prepare youths for war
