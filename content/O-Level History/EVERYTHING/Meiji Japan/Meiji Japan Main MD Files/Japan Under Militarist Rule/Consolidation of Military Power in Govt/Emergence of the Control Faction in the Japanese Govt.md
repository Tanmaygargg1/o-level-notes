while military was getting stronger => it was not united.
- was divided between imperial way of faction and control faction
- feud between 2 factions was bitter. 

Feb 26 Incident 1936 => Young officers from imperial way of faction led a military coup
- attempted to assassinate key govt leaders + demanded new govt
- part of their call for Showa restoration, which aimed to restore glory to emperor + defending empire + improving life for ordinary people

Coup failed => emperor condemned their actions
- 19 leading figures => in the imperial way faction were arrested, tried in secret military court and executed

Failure of the coup => caused imperial way of faction to lose its influence within army
- gave control faction opportunity to seize control of military + restore its discipline
Military had so much power influence over cabinet => was able to appoint active general as Minister for war -> who in the post would have been non military person. 
- with strong grip on govt => Control faction began to prepare for war => by modernising Japanese military
