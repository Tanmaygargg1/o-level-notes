GD => hit Japanese farming and farmers hard
1932 => many farmer were heavily in debt
- struggling to pay their rent and taxes
- and feed their families
-> govt included range of measures to help farmers pay off their debts.
govt => increased public works in countryside to provide jobs for people

1932 => Ministry of Agriculture => launched campaign for economic revitalisation.
- campaign was a mix of propaganda and practical proposals => aimed to strengthen countryside

Campaign => stressed need to revive spirit of cooperation in rural areas. 
-> also encouraged farmers to utilise scientific farming methods => instead of traditional ones
- farmers received guidance on how to better manage crops and finances on their farms. 
- measures helped them to increase their income => and many farmers signed up for the campaign. 
- government also presented success stories to motivate more farmers to take part

Conditions did not improve significantly => govt was more focused on industrialisation
