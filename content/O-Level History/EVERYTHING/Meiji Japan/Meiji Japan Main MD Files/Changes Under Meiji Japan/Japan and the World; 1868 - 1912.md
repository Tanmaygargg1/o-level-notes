* Under Meiji restoration -> Japans economy -> expanded -> as it engaged in trade with outside world.

* Some Japanese leaders -> Believed that Japan should become a more important player in international politics - Esp. in Asia.
* They thought their country -> should build an empire like Britain
* Many politicians -> government officials -> and military leaders -> believed that it was important for japan to control lands -> with natural resources -> in order to grow and strengthen its economy.

* This determination to become an important force on the world stage -> became clear in 1894-1895 -> when japan went to war with china over Korea.
-> it was a stunning victory for japan as china's forces - were no match for the modern and well trained Japanese army and navy.

With its victory -> Japan increased its influence and control in Korea. 
China -> also ceded Taiwan and Liaodang Peninsula in Manchuria to Japan.
-> this alarmed another great power - Russia - because Japan was now too close to Russia's borders.
1904-1905 -> war broke out between Russia and japan. 
-> spectacular victory for japan -> was the first time an Asian power defeated a major European power. 
Japan replaced Russia as the key power in Manchuria. 
1910 -> Japan annexed Korea completely and became a major power in the region.
