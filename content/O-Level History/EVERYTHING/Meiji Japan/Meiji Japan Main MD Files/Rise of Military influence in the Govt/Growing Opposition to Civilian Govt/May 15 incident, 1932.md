15 May 1932 -> group of naval officers -> from League of Blood and other activists -> assassinated PM inukai Tsuyoshi.
* Inukai had angered some of the extremists by opposing the actions of the military in Manchuria. 
* he refused to give official govt acceptance of the state of Manchukuo when it was announced in February 1932.

the day they killed Inukai -> attackers also attacked the Mitsubishi Bank, the Seiyukai Headquarters, and several power stations - and tried to assassinate several other government officials as well.
* it was an attempted coup d etat
* they had hoped that the people would rise up to support them and overthrow Japans democratic govt.

* people did not rise up. 
* league of blood activists were arrested and put on trial.
* however, military commanders did not condemn the rebellion and even issued a statement - which effectively excused the officers for their actions and demanded political reform in Japan.

___
although military did not spell it out precisely -> they wanted control of govt.
* after talks with military leaders, Seiyukai party was prevented from forming a new cabinet even though it had the majority share of representatives in the diet.
* 26 may 1932 -> emperor appointed a navy admiral as Japans new PM. 
* his cabinet had 15 members - of whom just 5 were elected civilian party representatives. 
* remaining 10 were military officers and govt officials. 
* with majority of the cabinet made up of military officers, Japan now came under militarist rule.
