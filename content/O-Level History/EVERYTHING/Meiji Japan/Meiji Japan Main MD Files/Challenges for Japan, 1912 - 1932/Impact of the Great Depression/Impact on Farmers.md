As Great depression hit - Japans exports fell.
* affected the income of people - which resulted in lower demand for farmers produce
* -> caused income of farmers to fall by average 43% between 1929 and 1931
* Tenant farmers - tried to get landowners to reduce their rent -> but they disagreed
* landowners - even tried to evict tenant farmers - who could not pay rent but many resisted
* tenant farmers -> organised themselves into unions. 
	* they held protest meetings and took landowners to court. sometimes disputes turned violent.
