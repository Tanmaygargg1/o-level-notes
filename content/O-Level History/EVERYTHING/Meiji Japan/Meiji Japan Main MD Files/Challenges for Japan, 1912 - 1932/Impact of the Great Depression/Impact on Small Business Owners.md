* owners of small businesses -> shops, restaurants, and small factories -> suffered
* they struggled to attract customers as many Japanese felt the effects of the crisis
* 1930 -> there were twice as many closures of small businesses as an average year. 
* Many of these business owners - joined protest movements - such as that led by the imperial middle class federated alliance
* alliance - claimed that political parties were looking after landowners, banks and zaibatsu - while letting small business owners go out of business.
