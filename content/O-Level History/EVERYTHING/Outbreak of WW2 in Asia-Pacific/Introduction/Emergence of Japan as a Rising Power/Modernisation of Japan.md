- Avoid the same fate -> Japan began programme of modernisation in 1868 -> called Meiji restoration
	- Introduced new Meiji constitution -> provided for a limited form of democracy -> with emperor still dominant. 
	- Japans industries -> also underwent rapid expansion.
	- Modernisation quickly transformed Japan into important power in Asia by early 1900s.

Japan - Fought + won wars against China (1894-1895)  and Russia (1904-1905)
-> with its newly acquired military might
- annexed Korea + controlled areas of Chinese region of Manchuria. 
- Developed close alliance -> with Britain -> whose territories it guarded during WW1. 

1920s => Japan -> well respected on international stage.
-> full member of LON + took part in the process of disarmament after WW1
___
1921 -> Japan signed Washington naval treaty -> with Britain, France, and US.
- all promised to reduce building of warships.

-> Japan had to reduce their naval strength more than US and Britain.
- many Japanese -> saw this an unfair treaty -> fuelled their desire to establish themselves as equals of the West.

-> Japan also signed trade deals -> with Britain and US -> expanded its trade with the rest of Asia.
