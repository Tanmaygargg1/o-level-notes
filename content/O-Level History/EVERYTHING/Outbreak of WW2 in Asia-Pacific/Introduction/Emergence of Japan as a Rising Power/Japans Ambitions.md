Quickly developing nation -> Japan wanted to be seen -> equal with Western powers that had empires.

In Japan:
- fierce + aggressive form of nationalism (described as **ultranationalism**) -> was promoted

Ultranationalistic beliefs -> popular amongst Japanese
- public opinion -> strongly approved of conquest of Korea in 1910 + supported idea of Japan expanding its empire
- Beliefs -> driven by natural limitations that Japan faced. 
- Most of Japan -> covered by high mountains
	- there was little farm land to grow food to feed rapidly growing population

Japan -> did not have raw materials like iron ore and coal.
-> 1920s -> Japan depended on importing raw materials from china and food for its growing population.
- Result -> many ultranationalists argued -> Japan had to expand to get access to raw materials that a modern nation needed.
