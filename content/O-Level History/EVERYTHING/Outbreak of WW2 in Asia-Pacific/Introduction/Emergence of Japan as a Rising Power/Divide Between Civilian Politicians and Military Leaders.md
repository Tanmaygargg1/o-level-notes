Early to mid 1920s -> more moderate civil politicians -> who preferred to advance Japanese interest by peaceful means -> were in control of govt policy
- Tried to expand Japanese influence -> through trade + international cooperation 
	- Instead of conquest of territories. 
- they also cut military budget

___
Cut in budget -> angered military leaders -> felt it showed a lack of respect for military
- Believed that other countries could not be trusted as the latter might go back on the trade if the deals suited them

Extreme military leaders -> believed war between Japan and Western powers -> esp. US -> was inevitable + Japan needed resources of a big empire to fight this war.
