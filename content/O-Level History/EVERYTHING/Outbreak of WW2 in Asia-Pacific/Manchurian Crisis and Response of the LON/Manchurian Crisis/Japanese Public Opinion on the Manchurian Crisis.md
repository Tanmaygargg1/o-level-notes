1931 -> idea that influence + control -> should be extended over Manchuria -> had gained popularity in Japan.
- Manchuria -> rich in coal + iron -> and was seen as perfect solution to Japans lack of natural resources.
- Japans foreign minister (at that time) -> Shidehara Kijuro -> who was civilian politician -> with moderate views -> shared this view. 
- Shidehara Kijuro -> was good example of how entrenched the belief -> that Japan needed to expand was. 

As Foreign minister -> Shidehara Kijuro -> had taken part in the negotiations to limit arms + was a firm supported of LON.
- even he believed -> that it was right + justifiable -> for Japan to expand into Manchuria. 
- There were many who were far more aggressive -> than Shidehara. 
- Among them was Ishiwara Kanji -> colonel in Kwantung Army. 
	- Ishiwara -> was a hard-line ultranationalist -> and he believed Japan should rule Manchuria.
