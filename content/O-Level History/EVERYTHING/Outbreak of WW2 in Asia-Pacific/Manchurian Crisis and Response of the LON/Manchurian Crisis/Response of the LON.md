When Japan invaded Manchuria -> China -> appealed to LON -> for international support.
Oct 1931 -> Leagues council -> passed resolution -> by 13 votes to 1 -> calling on Japan to withdraw its troops.
- only vote against resolution -> cast by Japan.
Japanese -> claimed -> they were not invading China -> but acting in defence -> to keep peace -> since China was in state of anarchy.

For LON -> this was serious test (assessment on whether it could solve international dispute and force aggressor state to back down)

Sept 1932 -> full year after the invasion -> League -> presented Lytton report on incident.
- Conclusion was clear -> Japan had acted unlawfully + Manchuria should be returned to Chinese.

Feb 1933 -> Instead of withdrawing from Manchuria -> Japanese announced -> they intended to occupy more of China.
- Maintained -> this was necessary -> to defend their territories.

24th Feb -> Lytton report -> approved by 42 votes to 1 in -> Leagues general assembly.
- Only Japan voted against it.
- Japan rejected Lytton report -> resigned from League on 27th March.
___
Leagues economic sanctions -> powerless-> without support of US -> Japans main trading partner.
Britain -> did not sanction Japan -> seemed more interested in maintaining -> good relationship with Japan.
League -> could not ban arms sale to Japan -> because member countries could not agree on that.
- they were worried that Japan -> would retaliate + escalate the situation

Britain + France -> would not risk their navies or armies in war -> to stop Japan.
Only US and USSR -> would have had resources to remove Japanese from Manchuria by force -> but they were not League Members.
- USSR even sold railway line in northern Manchuria to Japanese.
