Manchurian Crisis -> had major consequences. 
- first -> it tarnished prestige of the League.
	- demonstrated that league -> was helpless in face of aggressor nations -> like Japan.
	- also undermined confidence of league officials -> to tackle future crises.
- second -> encouraged Japan -> to further expand into Asia -> in following years -> as Manchuria's location placed it in a strong position for expansion into China.
- Third -> Japan now had access to raw materials + resources -> available in Manchuria -> needed for its growing industries. 

Manchurian Crisis -> impacted Japanese politics.
- Annexation of Manchuria -> extremely popular in Japan. 
- increased the prominence + influence of ultranationalists and military -> resulted in weakening of democratic politics in Japan.
- Military -> continued to challenge civilian politicians authority -> and gained more positions of power and influence -> in govt by 1932. 

From this point -> Japans politics -> increasingly driven by ideas -> which promoted expansion.
