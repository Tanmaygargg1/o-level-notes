1937 invasion of China -> prompted US govt -> to take progressively harder line -> on Japan.
President Roosevelt -> was worried -> about Japans growing ambitions + wanted to keep its expansion in check.
- thus -> he announced series of financial + economic measures ->designed to halt Japans aggressive foreign policy.

Initial sanctions -> proved to be ineffective -> Japan subsequently occupied French Indochina -> 1940.
Response > embargo was placed on raw materials -> like steel and iron -> Oct 1940.
After failure of negotiations -> between US and Indochina and Japan + refusal of Japan -> to withdraw from China and Indochina -> oil embargo -> imposed ->  August 1941
Roosevelt -> resurrected a law from WW1. 
- Trading with the Enemy Act -> which seized Japanese assets -> in US -> making it unable to buy essential goods.

US -> policy -> was attempt to strangle Japan financially. 
- most serious impact -> Roosevelt's measures -> would cut off almost 90% of Japans oil supplies.
- For modern economy -> this was damaging. 
	- A modern army, air force, navy ->this was crippling; ships planes and tanks -> useless without fuel. 

US ->demanded Japanese troops ->withdraw from China + and promise not to threat any territories belonging to US / its allies.
