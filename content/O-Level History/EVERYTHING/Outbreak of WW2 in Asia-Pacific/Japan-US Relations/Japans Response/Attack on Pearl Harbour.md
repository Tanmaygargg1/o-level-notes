Even with increased output => of Manchuria + Korea + all its other territories -> Japan could not challenge power of US outright.
Sept 1940 -> Japan signed Tripartite Pact -> which made it an ally of Germany and Italy. 
- pact was meant to be insurance against US intervention.

Japanese commanders -> also decided -> they needed to deliver quick knockout blow to US -> from which latter would take time to recover.
-> in that time -> Japan would be able to conquer new territories + build up its economic resources + match US power. 

Admiral Yamamoto Isoroku -> devised pre-emptive skill -> against US.
Strategy -> targeted powerful US pacific fleet base -> at pearl harbour -> which protected US + other western interests in Asia.
___
Morning of 7th Dec -> 300 planes -> took off from Japanese aircraft carriers -> launch attack on Pearl harbour.
- attack caused widespread destruction 
- Japan failed to destroy American Repair shops + fuel oil tanks + other crucial naval installations.

All 3 US pacific fleet's aircraft carriers -> escaped unscathed -> as they were not present at Pearl harbour.
-> with this attack -> US declared war on Japan -> triggering outbreak of WW2 in Asia-Pacific.
