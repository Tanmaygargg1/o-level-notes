1940 -> Japan -. launched programme called Greater East Asia Co-Prosperity Sphere.
- aimed primarily at people in various Asian territories -> that Japan controlled.

Emphasis -> was on goal of ridding Asia -> of US and other western power influence 
- message was that: people of Asia -> should unite behind Japan -> which would lead campaign to drive foreigners out of Asia + Japan would protect people of Asia and rule them fairly.

Reality -> Greater East Asia Co Prosperity Sphere -> was more of programme for Japan -> to use massive resources of its empire in Asia -> to prepare for war.

In this respect -> Japan was initially successful.
- E.g.: production of coal in Manchuria -> rose from 12.8 to 60 million tons.
- production of iron from 68k to 870k tons.
- production of aluminium from 17k to 407k tons
- production of cotton from 247k to 597k tons
- production of liquid fuel from 0 to 1million tons
