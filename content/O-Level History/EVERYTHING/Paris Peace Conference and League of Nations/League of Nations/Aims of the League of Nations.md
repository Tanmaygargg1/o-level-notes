Key aim was to resolve international disputes without war
-> reflected in the Covenant signed by all members

Wilsons vision for league => world govt and police force

- all major countries would join the league => bind themselves to its covenant and disarm
- if they had dispute with another country => they would take it back to the league and accept its decisions
- League members => promised to protect one another if attacked

-> arrangement was called *collective security*
If any member broke the covenant and went to war illegally => other members would impose economic sanctions
as last resort => league could take military action against aggressor nation
