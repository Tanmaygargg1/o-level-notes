Not all leaders and major powers => convinced by Wilson's vision for league of nations

Lloyd George => wanted simpler organization => that met only in emergencies
Body like this already existed => *conference of Ambassadors*

Clemenceau => was also skeptical about the league
- wanted his country to be free and act independently

#### Ability to Achieve disarmament
By joining league => members were expected to disarm
- many countries feared that they would not be able to protect their territories + interests if they did so

-> led to reluctance among members to disarm (worsened in 1930s)

#### Ability to Stop Aggressor countries
Member countries => concerned about impact of economic sanction on their economies -> therefore reluctant to stop trading with aggressor countries.

Although league could take military action against aggressor country as last resort => difficult to do so -> league did not have an army of its own


#### Membership of the League
march 1920 => year after debate => US congress refused to ratify TOV + allow US to join the league
USSR => also not included in league as it was communist
-> 2 of the worlds greatest powers (US and USSR) were not part of the league

Germany => also not allowed to join due to its role in causing WW1

Without US in league => economic sanctions were difficult to enforce
US => could continue to supply weapons and goods to aggressor countries => even if league imposed military or economic sanctions.

Meant that aggressor countries > would not be as badly affected => could continue to trade with US which had worlds largest economy.

US also had one of the most powerful militaries.
Absence of US from the league => difficult to take strong action against aggressor countries => if League decided to do so as a last resort
