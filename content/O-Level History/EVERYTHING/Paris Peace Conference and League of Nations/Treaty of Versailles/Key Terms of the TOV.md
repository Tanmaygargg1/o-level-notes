Germany had to accept all terms of the treaty, including territorial changes.
___
1. War guilt clause
Under article 231 of treaty of Versailles => Germany had to accept total blame for starting the war

2. Reparations
Germany had to pay 6.6 billion pounds => reparations to the allies for war damage.
-> figure was debated for some time and only set in 1921

Economic rights in China, Morocco, Egypt => fortified
Saar coalfield to France for 15 years and huge quantities of raw materials given to allies.

3. Territory Changes
Germany's land => redistributed amongst allied powers
All overseas colonies in Africa and Asia were lost
Alsace Lorraine and Saar coal region were given to France.
Land between Germany and East Prussia => given out to form Polish corridor => to give Poland access to sea
lost 10% of land
lost 12.5% of population
=> forbidden to make an alliance with Austria to form Anschluss.

Former German colonies => became mandates controlled by LON => meant they effectively came under French or British control

4. Armed forces (Demilitarization)
Germany had to be reduced to well before pre war levels
- Army was limited to 100 000 men
- conscription was banned; soldiers had be volunteers
- no armored vehicles, submarines, aircrafts were allowed
- Navy could only have 6 battleships, with a size limited to 15000 men
- Border area Between Germany and France => Rhineland => demilitarized => no German forces or fortifications allowed there. 

5. Self determination
People who previously belonged to Austria Hungary => given right to govern themselves.

6. League of Nations
-> set up as an international body -> to help countries resolve disputes + if necessary enforce decisions without war. 
Germany was not allowed to join
