**Wilsons Aims at Paris Peace Conference**

1. 14 points and the league of nations
Wilson => believed that US would restore world to civilization + Nations should cooperate to achieve world peace
Jan 1918  > he outlined his 14 points in a speech to help accomplish this
One of the most important points for Wilson => setting up of an international body called league of nations. 
Wilson => sought to create an association of nations => in which disputes between countries could be resolved without war

2. Germany
Felt Germany should be punished => but was not in favor of destroying it completely
-> main aim was to strengthen democracy in Germany => so its people would not lets its leaders start another war
wanted to replace its old leaders who had brought Germany into war => with ones who would be more committed to democracy and maintaining peaceful relations with others states

3. Self Determination
Also believed in the idea of self determination => or that Countries should rule themselves instead of being ruled by others
Wanted different people of eastern Europe (Poles, Czechs, Slovaks) => to rule themselves rather than be part of the Austro-Hungarian Empire
___
#### Concerns About Wilsons Ideas
-> Lloyd George and Clemenceau found many of his ideas worrying
felt idea of self determination was naïve and impractical
Clemenceau also concerned that Wilson would be too soft on Germany
