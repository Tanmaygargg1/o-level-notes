tough politician => with a reputation of being uncompromising
-> he had seen his country invaded twice by Germans => determined to not let it happen again

France => suffered enormous damage in WW1
Large stretches of northern France had been invaded + there were 1.7 million deaths with many more wounded or missing

of big 3 => France had largest proportion of deaths compared to population => just over 4%, compared to 2% for Britain and 0.1% for US

Clemenceau => also aware of public opinion in France to punish Germany for the losses it had suffered

while French public wanted to avenge the past => Clemenceau and other French politicians => worried about the possible future threat posed by Germany
-> wanted to ensure that Germany would not be able to wage war against France again.
Determined that Germany should be able to pay for damage that had been caused in northern France by invading German armies.

some concerns:
- Germany had much larger and younger population (65 mil) compared to France (40mil)
	- Germany could use its people to fight a future war
- There had been no fighting in German territories => meaning their industrial regions were not damaged
	- allow Germany's economy to recover faster than France's


Clemenceau => also wanted Germany to be broken into a collection of smaller states => but was aware that Wilson and Lloyd George would not agree. 
Nevertheless => he had to show awareness of the public opinion in France => to protect France against another future German Invasion
-> demanded a treaty that would weaken Germany as much as possible
