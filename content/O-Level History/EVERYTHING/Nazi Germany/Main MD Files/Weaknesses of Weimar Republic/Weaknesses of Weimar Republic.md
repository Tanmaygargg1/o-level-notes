#### Weimar Constitution
as with any democracy => new republic needed a constitution. 
Even though constitution was approved by majority in the Reichstag => there were a number of representatives who voted against it.
-> early worrying sign

New constitution => introduced significant changes.
* All Germans over the age of 20 could vote => with women being included for the first time
* Party representation in Reichstag => would be determined by proportional representation

| Strengths of Weimar Govt                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Weaknesses of Weimar Govt                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| - proportional representation => meant that any party taking part in the elections => would gain seats in the Reichstag => equal to the number of votes that it received. <br>=> example: if party received 20% of the votes, it would gain 20% of seats in the Reichstag<br>-> allowed most views and interests being represented in the reichstag. <br><br>- Laws => had to be approved by the Reichstag<br>=> chancellor could not just push them through<br>-> this prevented the abuse of power. <br> | - hard for one party to gain a majority. <br>=> even parties opposed to democracy => like German communist party => could gain seats in the Reichstag. <br>-> governments ended up as coalitions=> which made it hard for the Reichstag to pass laws. <br>-> led to governments that were weak and often short lived. <br><br>- Article 48 => was a double edged sword. <br>=> it could be used to protect + preserve democracy -> but presidents with different attitudes towards democracy could misuse + exploit it. <br>-> could be a threat to the constitution. |
President => Ebert => wanted to bring about measures and reforms which would benefit millions of Germans => but he could not do so due to internal and external problems (with reference to the republic.
