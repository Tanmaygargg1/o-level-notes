From Poor workers and farmers to wealthy Landowning aristocrats => right wing groups came from all classes of society
Many of them => were former soldiers => including Freikorps who had fought the Spartacists in 1919.

March 1920 => Wolfgang Kapp => led 5000 Freikorps into Berlin in a rebellion known as Kapp Putsch
Kapp => planned to overthrow Weimar Republic + return Germany to a more authoritarian system => similar to that in the time of the Kaiser

Weimar Govt => ordered German Army => to attack Freikorps and put down the rebellion.
Army => refused to act against Freikorps => seemed as though Ebert's govt was doomed

However => Germanys 12 million industrial workers and trade unions => came to the rescue
=> declared a general strike
Country suddenly had no transport, power or water.
At the same time => all civil servants and government officials => showed support for Ebert and refused to cooperate with Kapp

After a few days => Kapp realized he could not succeed and he fled the country
-> hunted down and died awaiting trial

Seemed like => Weimar Republic had some support and power after all
-> However besides Kapp => no other rebels were punished.
Courts => set many rebels free. 
Political violence from right wing => continued after Kapp Putsch => but they were rarely punished
