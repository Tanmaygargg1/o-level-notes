One of the first challenges that Weimar republic faced => announcement of the terms of the TOV in may 1919.
German people => not told that if war carried on => Germany would have eventually been invaded by its enemies
=> vast majority though -> German army had ended the war undefeated and even celebrated return of the troops

Without knowing truth => German people were expecting a negotiated treaty => in which they would be treated with respect as equals.
=> were shocked and angry when the terms were announced.
-> called the treaty a 'diktat' or a dictated peace forced upon Germans.
Ebert himself => very reluctant to sign treaty => but he had no choice but to do so on 28th June 1919

Treaty => undermined Weimar republic + poisoned attitudes of many Germans towards it => in such a way that it eventually brought the Republic down.
Minds of many Germans => Ebert and Weimar politicians => were forever to blame for the treaty
=> were often called 'November criminals'
Most Germans=> also chose to believe in the Dolchstoss Theory > or that German army had been stabbed in the back by the socialist and liberal politicians => that agreed to the armistice in November 1918.
-> as unpopularity grew => extremist ideas, violence, voices => took root in the country
