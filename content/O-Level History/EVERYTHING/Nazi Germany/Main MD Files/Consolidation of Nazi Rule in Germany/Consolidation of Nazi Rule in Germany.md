Various challenges Hitler faced when he first became chancellor:
* Socialist SPD and communist KPD => made it clear they would oppose anything Hitler tried to do. 
* Hitler did not have majority of seats in Reichstag => which he needed to carry out his plans
* Von Papen => planned to control Hitler and use Nazi Party => and would even try to replace Hitler if he could. 
	* Powerful Landowners and business owners supported Von Papen
* Von Schleicher and most other army commanders => did not think much of Hitler => were loyal to Hindenburg rather than Hitler. 
* Most of the German People had far more loyalty and respect for Hindenburg than Hitler.
