Local authorities => were suspicious of the new party => sent army intelligence officer => Adolf Hitler to investigate
=> when Hitler spied on the DAP => he found that he agreed with their views and criticism of the WG. 
Sept 1919 => he joined DAP

=> he soon rose through party ranks
* he was an excellent speaker => began to build up a personal following
* Argued that the party would get more support if it developed more nationalist policies
* Feb 1920 => Hitler and Drexler => changed the name of the party to National socialist German workers party => Known as Nazi party
* Same time => they set of Nazi's main Belief in the 25 point program

1. Abolition of the TOV
2. Anschluss between Germany and Austria
3. Only "true" Germans => would be allowed to live in Germany. Jews in particular were to be excluded
4. Nationalization of large industries and businesses
5. Generous provisions for old age pensioner
6. Creation of strong central govt in Germany
