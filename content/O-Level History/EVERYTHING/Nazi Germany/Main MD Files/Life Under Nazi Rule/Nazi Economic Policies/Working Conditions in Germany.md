Propaganda => praised workers in Germany + tried to associate them with Hitler.
Hitler => fulfilled his promise => to lower unemployment => helped to ensure Nazi popularity among industrial workers.
=> also tried to win their loyalty through many various initiatives.
* schemes such as *strength through joy* => gave them cheap theatre and cinema tickets, organized courses, trips and sports events, and even offered discounted cruises on luxury liners
	* 1939 => 50 million Germans had been on a KdF holiday
* *Beauty of Labor Movement* => improved working conditions in factories. 
	* introduced features not seen before => washing facilities and low cost canteens. 
* state scheme was introduced => to help every worker buy the Volkswagen beetle. 
	* no worker ever received one => war halted car production in 1939
___
Workers lost their main political party => SDP
=> lost their trade unions => for many workers this was a source of bitter resentment
* all workers had to join DAP => run by Robert Ley
	* DAF kept strict control of workers
		* they were not allowed to strike for better pay and conditions => and sometimes they were prevented from moving to better paying jobs. 

Wages => remained comparatively low => although prices of goods were also strictly controlled
late 1930 => workers were complaining that standard of living => was still lower than it had been before the depression
