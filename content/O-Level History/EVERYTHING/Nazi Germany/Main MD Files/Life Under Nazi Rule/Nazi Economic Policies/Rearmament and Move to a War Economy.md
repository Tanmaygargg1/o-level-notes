1935 => he reintroduced conscription for German army
1936 => he announced a 4 year plan => under the control of Goering => aimed to get the German economy ready for war within 4 years. 
Priority => had to be given to rearmament => and Germany had to be made as self sufficient as possible -> in food and industrial production ->  policy known as autarky
Goering => proceeded to manage the economy through a series of regulations => which controlled most aspects of food, prices and raw materials => setting new targets that private industries had to match. 
4 year plan => one of the very few clear policy documents that Hitler ever wrote.

Conscription => reduced unemployment. 
Need for weapons, uniforms, equipment => created jobs in coal mines, steel mills and textile mills.
Engineers and Designers => also gained new opportunities => when Hitler decreed that Germany would have a world class air force.

Besides bringing about economic recovery => these measures boosted Hitler's popularity => they renewed national pride.
 Germans => began to feel that Germany had finally emerged from its humiliation in WW1 and TOV.
	 -> was now able to put itself on equal footing with other great powers.
