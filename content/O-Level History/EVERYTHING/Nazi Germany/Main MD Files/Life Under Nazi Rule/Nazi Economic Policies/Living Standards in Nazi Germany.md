Big companies => benefitted the most from Nazi rule
-> no longer had to worry about strikes and trade unions
Companies like => Siemens, Mercedes, Volkswagen => made massive profits from govt contracts.
as companies flourished => so did their managers.
		-> their incomes rose much faster than those of their workers

**Farmers**
During great depression => Many farmers => borrowed money to survive => and were in debt => esp. with banks.
Introduction of *Reich Entailed Farm Law* => made it impossible to take land owned by farmers in debt and force them out
=> saved many farmers from losing farms
However => farms were not owned by state => and many farmers still remained poor.

**Middle Classes**
Many middle class Germans => pleased at the crushing of communists.
Owners of businesses => connected to rearmament => benefitted from govt contracts.
Many small businesses => found it difficult to do well in prevailing economic conditions
