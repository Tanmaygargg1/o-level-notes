Germany => very successful => early stages of war => and captured last amounts of territory in eastern Europe.
Many Jews => lived in these captured territories.
1941 onwards => Jews => used as slave labor + forced to live in ghettos => then systematically killed in death camps.
Estimated 6 million Jews were killed by 1945.

Apart from Hitler and SS => being responsible for death camps => others were to bear responsibility as well.
Genocide => would not have been possible => without cooperation of German civil service + police forces in Germany and occupied lands.

Other groups -> either cooperated or did nothing to oppose final solution => including army and owners and managers of major industries.
Many historians => also argue that German people => should shoulder some responsibility => as many took part in some aspect of the holocaust => by closing their eyes to the full reality of what was happening.
