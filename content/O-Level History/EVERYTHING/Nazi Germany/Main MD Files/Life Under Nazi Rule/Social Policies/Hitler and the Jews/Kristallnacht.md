November 1938 => young Jew => killed German diplomat in Paris.
=> Nazis used this as an opportunity => launched a violent attack on Jews.
Plain clothes SS troopers => given addresses of Jewish businesses + issued with pickaxes and hammers.
=> ran riot => smashing up Jewish shops and workplaces.
91 Jews => murdered.
Hundred of synagogues => burnt.
20 000 Jews => taken to concentration camps. 
Thousands left the country.

=> event became known as Kristallnacht => or night of broken glass.
Many Germans => watched it unfold => with alarm and concern.
-> Nazi controlled press presented Kristallnacht => as a spontaneous reaction of ordinary Germans against Jews.
Most Germans => did not believe this.
Hardly anyone protested => few who did were killed.
