Under Weimar Republic => Women were given right to vote + equality in areas such as marriage and civic matters. 

Hitler => had a very traditional view on the role of women
=> believed that Women could best serve their nation > by fulfilling their roles as good mothers and spouses.

Under Nazi rule => women were excluded => from politics and academics. 
Women were not allowed to vote in elections => and were discouraged from pursuing university studies
Many Young German girls => received vigorous training => From league of German girls => in domestic tasks.
Employment opportunities for women => limited => discrimination against women applicants was encouraged. 

Later wars of 1942-1945 => Nazi Arm industries => did require more women to cope with increasing demand for war supplies
* still their Policies towards women => remained conflicted between their traditional view of women as mother, and their actual need for them to support Nazi war machine
