Goebbels => kept strict control over what Germans could read hear and watch
No books, paintings or other art forms could be produced without Nazi approval.
-> there was also heavy censorship and newspapers -> could not print anti Nazi ideas.

- all films had to carry pro Nazi messages
	- newsreels shown along with films => also proclaimed Nazi achievements
Government => made cheap radio available => so all Germans could buy one and listen to Nazi propaganda when aired
* harsh penalties for anyone who listened to unauthorized stations

All these restrictions => enforced strictly.
Gestapo agents and informers => monitored anyone thought to be producing or consuming banned works. 
Police, SD, Gestapo => would arrest any offenders + take them to concentration camps.
