* following presidential election -> main candidates all argued for an end to the war -> sign that Americans were anxious to get out

* Nov 1968 -> Richard Nixon elected president. 
* 1969-1973 -> he and his national security advisor -> Henry Kissinger -> worked to end US involvement in Vietnam

**Vietnamisation**
* Nixon began *policy of Vietnamisation* -> handing responsibility for the war to SV forces and withdrawing US troops.
* April 1969 - end of 1971 -> abt 400000 US troops left Vietnam
* Jan 1973 -> all parties signed *Paris peace accords* -> "end war and restore peace in Vietnam"

* with Paris peace accords ending direct US military intervention in Vietnam -> Nixon described it as "Peace with Honour"
* Nixon promised continuing financial aid + military support to Vietnam -> but US congress refused to allow it

**Watergate and Political Fallout**
* Nixon -> in big political trouble over *Watergate scandal*
* 1972 -> he authorised illegal break-in at Watergate complex to place listening devices in offices of politicians who opposed him
	* result -> was *impeached in 1973*
* these political developments -> made it diff. for Nixon to gain support congress when it comes to Vietnam war
* Congress also believed -> US should not continue involvement in war -> esp. given the corruption of SV regime

**Final Withdrawal**
* by 29 March 1973 -> last US forces had left Vietnam
* Ho died in 1969 -> did not get to see US forces leave and his country reunified a few years later. 
* even with withdrawal of US forces -> new US pres. -> Gerald Ford -> wanted to continue providing financial aid to SV
* request made in Jan 1975 + rejected by congress -> signalling lack of support for Vietnam war
