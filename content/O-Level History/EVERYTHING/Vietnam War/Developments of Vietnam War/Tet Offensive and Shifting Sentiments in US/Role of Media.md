* r.s. between US govt and media was good -> government wanted to win domestic support for the Vietnam war
* Journalists accredited by MACV -> would get access to war zones, interviews, regular reports.*
* in return -> they were expected to not reveal any information which would help the enemy

* by 1968 -> tone of reporting on Vietnam was beginning to change. 
* Tet offensive seemed to be main reason. 
* during Tet offensive -> journalists abandoned their restraint. 
* Tet offensive convinced many journalists -> US could not win war
* Walter Cronkite of CBS news on American TV station - was one such journalist.
* he publicly criticised US war effort in Vietnam on national TV. 
* some believed that this swung public opinion on US involvement in Vietnam war
