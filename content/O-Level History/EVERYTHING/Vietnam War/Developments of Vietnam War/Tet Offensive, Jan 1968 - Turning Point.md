**Before Offensive**
- 1965 -- 1967: official view in US was that -> war was going well

since fight against Viet Cong insurgency had no clear territorial goal to capture 
- US and SV forces used "body count" -> means of measuring success. 
* goal -> kill as many Viet Cong soldiers as possible -> wear the enemy down

**Tet Offensive**
- This confidence shattered -> early in 1968
- during Vietnamese New Year (Tet) holiday -> Viet Cong fighters attacked more than 100 cities +  other military targets.
- leading up to attack -> Viet Cong -> Used Ho Chi Minh trail -> move war material + fighters from NV to SV

this attack known as Tet Offensive
___
* Tet offensive -> involved ard 80000 Viet Cong + NV troops
* particularly bitter fighting over northern part of SV 
* offensive -> costly for communists -> Viet Cong badly weakened
	* lost ard 10000 experienced fighters
* ppl of SV did not rise up and join Viet Cong as hoped

* despite that -> TO turned out to be turning point in war -> raised hard questions in US
	* 1st -> US had nearly 500000 troops in Vietnam + was spending 20 billion a year on war. 
		* so why had communists been able to launch major offensive that took US forces completely by surprise
	* 2nd -> US and SV forces quickly retook towns captured in offensive. 
		* however in order to do so -> they required large amts of artillery and air power. 
		* many civilians killed + ancient city of Hue destroyed
