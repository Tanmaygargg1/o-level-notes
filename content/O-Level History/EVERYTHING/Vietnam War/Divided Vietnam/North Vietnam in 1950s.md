**Population Movement After Geneva**

Immediately after Geneva Conference -> Ho worked quickly -> consolidate his hold on North Vietnam.
- in early months of his regime -> thousands fled from North Vietnam -> fear of communist rule
- at same time -> many Viet Minh soldiers + supporters in South Vietnam -> fled northwards.
- Strengthened regime -> got rid of potential opponents.

**Support for Ho and Communist Regime**

Ho + communists -> had much support in North Vietnam:
- Ho ->  recognised as great leader -> who had fought Japanese invaders + French colonial rulers.
- he had introduced sweeping land reforms
	- which ensured that land was confiscated from larger landowners and;
	- distributed to peasant farmers.
- during redistribution policy -> many landlords -> placed on *show trials*

* Land reform -> was popular policy among peasant farmers.
	- however these reforms also led to decline in agricultural output
	* combined with decline in imported foodstuff -> led to famines in NV

**Harsh Repression / Opposition**
to maintain stability -> regime was ruthless ->  in dealing with opponents + potential opponents.
- lot of violent resistance -> from large landowners during land redistribution -> led to 1000s of opponents being executed. 

->  combination of popular support + harsh treatment of opponents -> meant North Vietnam was overall politically stable
