By end of [[Geneva Conference, 1954]] -> Diem was PM of South Vietnam.

- was fierce anti communist + Vietnamese patriot
	- aimed to modernise Vietnamese econ + society
		- strengthen country against interference by foreign powers

Early in presidency -> Diem implemented some modernisation programmes.
- construction of universities
- expansion of agriculture
- attempts to transformed communities in countryside
	- aims of creating national identity + localised democracy.

Despite attempts => Diem's regime -> proved to be increasingly unpopular -> as 1950s progressed -> due to:
- use of authoritarian methods
- regime frequently imprisoned + sometimes executed opponents.
