VW -> devastating consequences for American Society + confidence of US in managing international relations
domestic self doubt -> created international decline in American prestige.

| Impact of Vietnam War on US Foreign Policy | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Bankrupt Ideology**                      | Campaign against communist -> portrayed by US as a moral crusade. <br><br>However Vietnam war -> demonstrated that ideology of West was not morally superior. <br><br>atrocities committed by US soldiers + use of chemical weapons -> had damaged its reputation                                                                                                        |
| **Failure of Containment**                 | US failed to enact containment in SEA despite military strength. <br><br>as feared by proponents of the domino theory, after fall of SV -> Laos, Cambodia also turned communist                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Weakening of US Presidency**             | US congress -> traditionally supported the presidents in their conduct of foreign policy<br><br>however towards end of VW -> congress exercised greater oversight of presidential actions. <br><br>for instance -> Congress forbade US military aid to Cambodia in 1970. <br><br>in 1973 -> it introduced *war powers act* -> limiting ability of president to go to war |

**Impact on SEA**
Within SEA -> inability of US to win Vietnam war + contain communism in Vietnam -> stoked fears of spread of communism.
* fear contributed to formation of *association of southeast Asian nations (ASEAN)* in 1967 amid the Vietnam war
* ASEAN also declared SEA as zone of peace, friendship, neutrality in 1971 -> with goal of keeping ASEAN free from the interference of any outside powers
