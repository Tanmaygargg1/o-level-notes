**Impact of Vietnam on US Policy**

- failure greatly affected future US policies towards communist states
- after war -> US tried to improve relations with communist world

**Detente with USSR**
- US sought greater understanding with USSR in this period -> known as Detente (reduction of tension)
- 1972 -> US and USSR signed *Anti Ballistic Missile Treaty* to reduce nuclear weapons
	- this was result of series of successful bilateral negotiations known as *strategic arms limitation talks*

- in 1975 -> 2 parties met again in Helsinki, Finland -> to discuss borders and human rights

* at same time -> US ended its block on the issue of China's representation at UN and Nixon made visits to China to improve diplomatic relations
