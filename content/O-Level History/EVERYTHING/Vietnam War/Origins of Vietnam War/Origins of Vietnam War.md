**French Colonial Rule**
In 18th and 19th centuries -> European powers -> established rule and influence in SEA.
- Vietnam was part of the French ruled area known as *Indochina* -> which was conquered and controlled by the French empire progressively -> between 1862 and 1904. 
- local forms of govt replaced with French Systems of governance led by French officials -> under French rule

While French colonial rule -> in Indochina -> introduced Western Education and Healthcare -> their rule was considered resource exploitation 
- heavy local taxes
- colonial monopoly on the trade of salt, opium, rice, alcohol.

-> thus several attempts to rebel and overthrow French rule from 1890s to the 940s. 
- however none succeeded. 
___
**WWII and Japanese Occupation**
- WWII began in 1939 -> France defeated by Germany in 1940
- Japan -> ally of Germany -> wanted access to French Indochina -> resources and strategic position
- Thus Japanese forces invaded ->  northern region of French Indochina in Sept 1940 -> then came to armistice and agreement with the French Indochina colonial govt.
- French colonial govt -> aligned with Vichy France.
	- Vichy France -> was the new govt set up after defeat of France in 1940 -> was aligned to Nazi Germany. 
	- Vichy France -> allowed Japanese to station troops in Indochina.
French Colonial govt -> was often reduced to a *puppet* of the Japanese during this period.
___
French colonial rule + subsequent Japanese rule in French Indochina ->  both harsh. 
- result -> various groups emerged -> resist French + Japanese rule
- most important of these resistance groups ->  Viet Minh, led by Ho Chi Minh 
	- short for<span style="background:rgba(240, 200, 0, 0.2)"> League for the Independence of Vietnam was a nationalist</span> movement ->  aimed to achieve independence.
	- it was an umbrella movement made up of 2 main groups:
		- non communists ->  simply wanted to drive Japanese out of Vietnam in favour of self rule 
		- communists ->  wanted to establish an independent communist state in Vietnam

During WWII -> Viet Minh -> received money, weapons, supplies from:
- US 
- USSR
- Guomindang (GMD) govt in China
-> Even though GMD govt in China -> was anti communist -> they support Viet Minh against common enemy that was Japan
