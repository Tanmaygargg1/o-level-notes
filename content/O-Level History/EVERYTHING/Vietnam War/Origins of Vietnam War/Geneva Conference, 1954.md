After defeat at Dien Bien Phu, French govt and public lost interest in retaining Vietnam as colony -> cost of conflict outweighed benefits

My 1954 -> international conference -> attended by:
-  France, Viet Minh, USSR, China and US, and Britain -> held in Geneva (Switzerland)

Viet Minh demanded:
- recognition of Democratic Republic of Vietnam and the removal of French Troops

After weeks of talks:
-  Vietnam -> divided into 2 -> along *17th Parallel* 
	- NV: comm: Led by Ho Chi Minh
	- SV: Non com: Led by PM Ngo Dinh Diem

- Part of the agreement -> was that elections would be held by 1956 -> to establish a single government for a unified Vietnam.

**Key Outcome**
Agreements brought peace but not stability. 
- neither side was happy with the partition -> but the only realistic alternative was further fighting
- with peace agreement and withdrawal of the French by 1956 -> Viet Minh disbanded
