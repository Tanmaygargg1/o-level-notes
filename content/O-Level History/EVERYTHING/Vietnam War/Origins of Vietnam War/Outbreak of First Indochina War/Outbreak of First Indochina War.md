**French Return and Resistance**

After WWII ended (1945) -> French + Allied Forces -> moved to reoccupy Vietnam + accept surrender of Japanese soldiers in Vietnam.

- French came back wanting to rule Vietnam again, but faced serious resistance.
- to the Viet Minh -> they had not fought the Japanese only to hand power back to French. 

- Determined to take control of whole country -> Viet Minh entered city of Hanoi on 2 Sept 1945 + declared Vietnamese Independence.

Over course of 1946 -> tensions between French | Viet Minh intensified.
- first Indochina War broke out on 16 Dec 1946
	- Viet Minh used underground warfare tactics
	- they retreated to the mountains and forests + carried out surprise raids against the French. 
___
**Chinese Involvement**
Other opponents to French rule than Viet Minh
- initially anti-communist GMD govt in China -> did not want a pro-communist Viet Minh to take power. 
- however after defeat of GMD at hands of Chinese communists -> China continued to support Viet Minh.

**US Involvement + Domino Theory**
US -> it was opposed to imperialism and communism

after WWII -> had to make the choice between:
- supporting the colonial ambitions of France 
- supporting the largely communist Viet Minh. 

Hoping to win the sympathies of anti colonial US -> Ho cleverly kept quiet about wanting a communist Vietnam.
- instead portrayed it as a struggle against French colonial rule.

however possibility of winning US support vanished when communists took power in China

Against backdrop of cold war -> US believed that Ho and Viet Minh were controlled by USSR. 
- therefore it withdrew its support for Viet Minh + supported the French -> providing weapons and supplies in their fight against Viet Minh.
