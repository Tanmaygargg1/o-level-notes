 As war dragged on to the early 1950s -> the French attempted to lure Viet Minh out into open battle -> against strongly defended bases -> where French superiority in firepower could be used. 
- one such French Base -> was Dien Bien Phu.

In 1954 -> large well armed force of French troops -> was thoroughly defeated by the Viet Minh.
Viet Minh was able to cut off the French army's supply lines, depriving them of food + medical supplies and ammunition.
