**Gulf of Tonkin Incident (2 Aug 1964)**
* NV petrol boats -> attacked USS Maddox (a US destroyer) -> in Gulf of  Tonkin.
* By this point, NV and SV -> already in a de facto since state of conflict.

**US Response**
* Gulf of Tonkin incident -> led to US congress to take decisive action.
* passed *Gulf of Tonkin resolution* -> giving Johnson power to take all necessary measures -> prevent further aggression and achieve peace and security.
* marked beginning of direct US military intervention

**Escalation to Full Scale War**
* instead of only deploying advisors, US sent troops to fight in Vietnam. 
* US allies such as Australia, Br. New Zealand, SK -> also sent soldiers
* phase of direct American Involvement in war -> called Vietnam war, or 2nd Indochina war
