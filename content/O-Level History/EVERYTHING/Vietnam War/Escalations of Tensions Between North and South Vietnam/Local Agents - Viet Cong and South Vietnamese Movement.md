local conflict continued to escalate in early 1960s.

**Viet Cong Escalates Conflict**
* supplied by  NV -> Viet Cong stepped up to guerrilla war against South Vietnamese movement

* used Ho Chi Minh trail -> send reinforcements + supplies to guerrilla fighters
	* 40,000 NV army troops between 1961-1963

fighters -> attacked:	
* SV govt forces, officials, buildings
* Viet Cong chose to avoid well defended SV positions in urban areas
* focused on making countryside unsafe for govt forces. 
* a attacked US Air Force supply bases.

**SV's Response: Strategic Hamlet Programme**
* SV responded with policy called *Strategy Hamlet Programme* 
	* peasants relocated (sometimes forcefully) -> to fortified villages away from control + influence of Viet Cong
	* Strategic hamlets -> also support Diem's vision of transforming countryside 
	* removing civilians -> allow superior firepower of US Air Force + SV army -> destroy Viet Cong without civilians being caught in crossfire.
