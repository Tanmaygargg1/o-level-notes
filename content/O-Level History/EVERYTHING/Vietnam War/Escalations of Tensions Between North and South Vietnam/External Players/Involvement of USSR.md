**Cautious Start in early 1960s About Getting Involved in Vietnam**

* Soviets had supported HO's struggles against French in 1950s + had provided support for formation of Viet Cong
* however not active in Asia in early 1960s
	* because of the *Sino Soviet Split* (mid 1950s onwards)
		* caused r.s. between USSR and China to deteriorate -> thus 2 comm powers less likely to cooperate
		* Sino Soviet split also meant -> NV had to play balancing act between USSR and China's interests -> gain aid and support from both countries.

**Increased Interest (Mid-Late 1960s)**

mid 1960s -> Soviets began to show more interest in Vietnam.
* Due to Sino Soviet Split -> USSR also in contest with China over leadership of communist world
* showing supp for another communist state which was standing up against US -> important in asserting USSR's leadership.
* soviets steadily provided more and more arms + other essential supplies to NV. 
* late 1960s -> USSR provided more supp to NV than China -> in form of aircraft, air defences, surface to air missiles
