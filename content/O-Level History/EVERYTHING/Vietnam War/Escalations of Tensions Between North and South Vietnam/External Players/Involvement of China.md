**China's Strategic Concerns (Post-1949**

Aft comm victory in China in 1949 -> Mao saw US -> as greatest threat
* concerned abt possible invasion by US allies, backed by US forces. 

Mao believed -> if Vietnam became non communist country that was allied with US: -> China potentially vulnerable to attack from SK, Vietnam and GMD govt in Taiwan.

**Buffer Zone Strategy**

thus Mao -> eager to support any state / group that created anti US buffer in Vietnam -> esp. if it was comm one

Another reason Mao wanted to help NV -> show support for another comm state.
+Found bond of loyalty to Ho.

**Internal Political Reasons**

Mao also believed -> involvement in Vietnam war -> help unite Chinese people behind is leadership. 
- had done same in Korean War -> had strengthened his position, esp. when Chinese forces pushed US troops back in Korea.
