##### How does Growth of MNCs drive Globalisation
**Point:** MNCs drive globalisation by promoting interconnectedness of the world through their operations and services
**Examples:** 
Nutella: (operations in multiple countries, production of Nutella requires ingredients from multiple countries) [[Extra Examples for Globalisation#Nutella]]

Starbucks 

Iphone?

**Explanation:** 
- through their massive supply chains, MNCs are able to connect the world and make countries more economically independent. 
- When MNCs set up operations in other countries, the hiring of local manpower / workforce further increase the *interdependence* [[Essays on Globalisation#^a57531]] between countries
