1. Classify the sources into those which agree and do not agree with the given statement

2. Focus on the sources which agree with the statement
- Use PEEL for each source and explain how each source agrees with the statement

3. Next, look at the sources which contradict with the statement
- Use PEEL for each source and explain how each source disagrees with the statement
- Explanation in NOT REPHRASING OF EVIDENCE
- Explanation requires an inference & elaboration of the quoted evidence (like the format for inference content: CIEE)

*Summary: 2 agree paragraphs, and 2 disagree paragraphs, total of 4 paragraphs*
*This will only give you a maximum of 8/10marks*

#### What to do for bonus of 2 Marks:
**Assess reliability**
Source use for this CANNOT BE THE SOURCE USED IN RELIABILITY SKILL QUESTION
- Only way to assess for reliability for this question is <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">provenance</mark> 

**Use Contextual Knowledge**
Not recommended as you have to provide FACTUAL INFO related specifically to the issue)

**Provide a Balanced Conclusion**
Conclude that given statement has 2 perspectives & provide resolution to the issue
- basically you have to state why both sides have merit, and why both are valid statements. 
- for the disagree paragraphs, in this paragraph you need to include how you can SOLVE, or solutions that will help address the issue to improve the situation
