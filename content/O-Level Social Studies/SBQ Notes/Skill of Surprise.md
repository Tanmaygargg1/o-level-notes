**Splitting VS combining paragraphs**
- Can combine content and cross reference paragraphs
	- start with the stand of your cross reference
	- can only combine if the stand of content is the same as stand of cross reference
	- if you want to do diff. stand (e.g. content is surprised, cross reference not surprised as it contradicts…) then SPLIT your paragraphs
___
CR Para
- surprised - due to contradiction
- not surprised - due to support
___
I think you can include some reliability as well, before you state surprised or not surprised. Usefulness is not required.
- reliable -> surprised that such a reliable source can say ""
- vice versa
