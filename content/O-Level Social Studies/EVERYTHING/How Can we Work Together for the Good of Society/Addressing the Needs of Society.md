Focuses on improving well being of different groups of people by responding to issues and challenges they face.
(outcome)
____
- government set up structures, policies, national programs in a targeted manner
- Despite efforts of community groups and the govt, there could be gaps in addressing specific needs of groups of people
	- to bridge these gaps => individuals can initiate their own efforts of tap on the resources and programs provided by the govt or community groups
	- can also create new community groups to further attend to specific needs of society that has not been addressed
