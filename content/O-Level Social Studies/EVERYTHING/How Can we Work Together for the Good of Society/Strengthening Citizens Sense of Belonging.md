#SocialStudiesExamples Persevering memories of Dakota Crescent => seen as Singapore's heritage
- govt announced Dakota Crescent would be redeveloped as part of renewal plans
- Residents would be able to relocate to another are
- announcement was made later that some of the buildings and a playground in Dakota Crescent would be retained and repurposed for civic and community uses

**Efforts by Community Groups**
- 2 people and private media company came together to form an informal community group for project called Between 2 Homes. 
- produced multimedia website to document the rich heritage and community of Dakota Crescent => and it showed the impact of the relocation on residents
- also conducted educational workshops in schools => discuss how plans for redevelopment and relocation can be improved. 

**Efforts by Govt**
Our Singapore Fund => launched by SG govt.
- supports ground up projects that build national identity or meet social and community needs. 
- Successful applicants => may receive funding of up to 80% of costs, subject to maximum 20 000 per project

**Efforts by Individuals**
Various citizens joined Between 2 Homes initiative
- conducted interviews with residents of Dakota Crescent and documented their stories

- volunteers became more aware of needs of Dakota Residents. 
- to care for these residents => some volunteers formed Cassia Resettlement team => focus on helping residents overcome challenges before, during and after relocations
- included weekly house visits and potluck parties for residents

-> group of passionate Singaporeans came together in an informal group => efforts help to bring sense of belonging as citizens develop attachement to history, people and places in the country.
-> more concerned about shaping country's future as well as they show care and take actions to help others.
