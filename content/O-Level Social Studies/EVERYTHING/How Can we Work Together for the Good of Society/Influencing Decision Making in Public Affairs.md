Citizens + govt can work together to make decisions for the good of society through engagement platforms such as Reaching Everyone for Active Citizenry @ home (REACH) and citizens jury for war on diabetes.
#SocialStudiesExamples 
- REACH -> seeks to enhance citizens view on public policies, listen to their concerns and issues, consolidates and shares feed back with relevant govt agencies. 
- Ministry of Health launched nationwide effort, War on Diabetes to help manage and prevent diabetes. 
	- Organised first ever Citizens Jury to develop community based and community driven recommendations to combat the problem of the disease. 
		- campaign to drink more water => installed more water coolers. 
		- Diabetes awareness campaign and curriculum for children
