Many helping hands-> individuals, families, community groups, govt -> work together -> address issues -> related to socio-economic diversity + meet needs of society

- some individuals -> may not have resources -> take care of themselves or family members
	- Community groups / govt -> step in to support them

Govt -> provides targeted assistance -> to individuals and families -> who are unable to provide for themselves -> and where support from community groups is inadequate.
- takes form of -> subsidies, financial assistance + skills training to increase employability
___
#### Subsidised social services and targeted assistance -> provided by state

##### Subsidies for essential services
###### Healthcare
- Singapore's healthcare financing system -> emphasises -> individual responsibility + affordable healthcare for all
	- govt -> adopts multiple tiers -> of protection -> ensure accessibly + affordable healthcare -> to all Singaporeans.
	- While Singaporeans save for their own or their dependents' healthcare needs -> thru CPF Medisave account -> govt provides subsidies -> to reduce medical costs -> for Singaporeans. 

###### Preschool
- Preschool subsidies -> ensure all children have access to quality education -> for good start in life -> regardless of family background
	- While Parents pay for preschool fees -> govt subsidies across all eligible income tiers -> have increased
	- From Jan 2020 -> lower income families -> earning <3000 per month -> pay $3 a month at preschool centres -> under Anchor Operator Scheme (funded by govt)

###### Housing
- Ensure affordable housing opportunities for Singaporeans
	- While Singaporeans pay for their home purchase in cash -> or with their CPF savings -> govt has put in place various housing grants and rental housing schemes -> ensure housing opportunities kept affordable for Singaporeans.
____
#### Social and Community Assistance to provide targeted support -> for lower income Singaporeans
- long term assistance -> provided for persons -> permanently unable to work -> due to old age, illness, disability -> and have little to no income, savings, family support
- short to medium term financial support -> provided -> help families + individuals -> tide over difficult times and regain stability

##### Vouchers -> provide more support -> for lower to middle income Singaporeans Households
- Some areas of support -> provided by permanent GST voucher scheme:
	- cash pay-outs in August every year -> support lower income Singaporeans -> with immediate needs
	- Quarterly U save rebates -> help lower and middle income HDB households -> offset utility bills

##### Opportunities for Upskilling and Training
- Programs and initiatives -> under SkillsFuture Singapore -> give students + working adults -> access to training + courses -> continually upskill + equip -> with industry relevant skills
- Workfare skills support scheme -> provides training allowance + cash awards -> encourage low wage Singaporeans -> to upgrade skills
____
Multiple layers of assistance -> provided -> ensure -> needs of Singaporeans from Diverse Socio economic backgrounds -> supported

-> with opportunities provided for education and training -> Singaporeans -> able to ensure -> own economic well being -> through employment


Low income groups / vulnerable and those with special needs -> govt provides additional targeted support -> protect their needs + enable them to experience social mobility
- complements individual, family, community efforts -> to help meet needs of society
