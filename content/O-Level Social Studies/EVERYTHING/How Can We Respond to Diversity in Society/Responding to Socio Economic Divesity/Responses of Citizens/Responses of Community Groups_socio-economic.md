>[!example]
>Daughter of Tomorrow (DOT)
>- formal community group -> conducts programs -> help women aged 20-60 -> upgrade skills + find jobs. 
>- Most women -> came from low-income families / live in subsidised rental flats
>
>DOT's financial literacy program
>- 4 weekly workshops -> followed by 7 months with support group
>- women learn good money management skills + progress towards financial goals
> #SocialStudiesExamples 

>[!example]
>Keeping Hope Alive (KHA)
>
>- informal group -> volunteers offer time / skills -> help less privileged -> in Singapore. 
> 
> Every sunday:
> - 50-60 volunteers -> conduct door to door visits -> to 1-2 rental flats-> look out for residents -> who are in need + take action to meet needs. 
> - include cleaning homes, fixing / replacing appliances, providing them with haircuts
>
> - share common goal -> of improving Quality of life -> of low income families in Singapore
