- immigrants are expected -> to adopt beliefs + practices of majority group -> in host country
- Over time -> identities of assimilated groups -> expected to become identical to those of majority group. 
	- E.g. Denmark, Germany, France
___
All French citizens + immigrants -> have to abide by the principle of *laicite* -> means state secularism
- refers to the belief of setting clear separation of religious affairs from governance and not favouring one religion over the other.

-> France believes: ideal of harmony -> in society is achieved -> when every individual puts aside his/her connections to race, ethnicity, religion
-> acknowledges existence of divers cultural identities
- believes French national identity should be prioritised over others.
