- Minority groups + immigrants -> retain unique identities while forging common grounds with majority group in host country
	- e.g. Canada, New Zealand, Singapore


Singapore -> believes socio-cultural identities -> complement Singapore national identity
- Singapore identity -> anchored in: 
	- Shared values of upholding law and order
	- respecting racial and religious harmony
	- commitment to working together for better future
- Differences in practice, customs, traditions of diff groups: 
	- recognised + respected
	- equality is promoted -> without granting special rights to any racial, ethnic, religious groups
