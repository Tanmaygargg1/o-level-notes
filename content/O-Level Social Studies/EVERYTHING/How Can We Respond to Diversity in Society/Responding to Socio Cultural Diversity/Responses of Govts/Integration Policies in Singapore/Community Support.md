2009 -> national integration council -> launched **community integration fund**
-> support organisations in implementing projects to encourage interactions between Singaporeans, immigrants, foreigners.
- allows them to deepen mutual understanding, build relationships, and develop stronger connection to Singapore.
___
#SocialStudiesExamples 
>[!example]
>Community events + activities -> organised by peoples association
>- include house visits, block parties, festive celebrations 
>	- so that new Singaporean citizens + PR -> get to know neighbourhood better and make new friends in community

- facilitate meaningful interactions between the 3 groups
- provides opportunities -> for them to experience diff. ethnic culture and religions of Singapore + learn about diff. aspects of living here
- Singaporeans can also befriend foreigners -> can learn more about local history, culture, ways of life to integrate into Singapore society
