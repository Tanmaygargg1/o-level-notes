>Welcome In My Backyard (WIMBY)
>- raise awareness about lives of migrant workers. 
>- also ams to promote interactions between migrant workers and local community
>
>During Relocation of migrant workers into HDB estates -> WIMBY volunteers: 
>- engaged singaporeans -> to write notes to welcome migrant workers into neighbourhoods. 
>  - Notes were translated in their native languages of migrant workers -> convey appreciation for their contribution to Singapore. 
>    
>WIMBY: 
> - organised several initiatives -> encourage interaction -> between Singaporeans and migrant workers.
> - one such initiative -> partnering schools in their Value in action programmes -> address issues facing immigrant workers. 

- collective effort -> required -> maintain harmonious interactions in diverse society.
- initiatives + proactive responses -> of individuals + community groups -> help shape attitudes + actions of general public
	- also help to develop -> deeper understanding -> between diff. socio cultural groups + spirit of mutual understanding and mutual accommodation and trust
