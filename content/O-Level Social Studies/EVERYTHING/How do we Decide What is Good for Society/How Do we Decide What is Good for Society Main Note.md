### Principles Shaping Governance
Governance -> can be defined as exercise of authority and use of resources to manage problems and affairs in society

Although there are systems and procedures => to guide the govt => it is also necessary that govt makes decisions shaped by principles. 

Principles shaping governance:
- having good leadership
- stake for everyone, opportunities for all
- anticipating change and staying relevant
- practicing meritocracy
