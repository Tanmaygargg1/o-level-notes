Providing stake for everyone and opportunities for all => entails giving citizens a say in decision making.
- when citizens feel like they have stake in SGs future => they develop greater sense of belonging and become more willing to be involved in working towards good of society
- Opportunities are thus created to encourage Singaporeans to share their views on Policies and decision making

#SocialStudiesExamples 
Singapore Together Movement
- Singaporeans are encouraged to partner govt and one another => to shape and act on their shared future in areas they care about
	- can be done through partnership opportunities or govt support for citizen led initiatives.

Emerging stronger conservation (ESC) series => part of Singapore Together movement
ESC => ran sessions. 
- involved many Singaporeans from different backgrounds. Languages and experiences => who expressed their hopes => for how Singapore can emerge stronger from COVID 19 pandemic.
