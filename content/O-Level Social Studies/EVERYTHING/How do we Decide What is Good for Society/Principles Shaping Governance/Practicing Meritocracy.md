#SocialStudiesExamples 
Eg: students in govt and specialized schools => offered awards based on their character, leadership, conduct learning attitudes and academic performance.

1. Edusave award for achievement, good leadership and services (EAGLES)
	-> for up to 10% of students from each school who have demonstrated leadership qualities, services to community and schools, excellence in non academic activities and good conduct

2. Edusave good progress award
	-> for students who are within top 10% of schools level and course => in terms of improvement in academic performance and have demonstrated good conduct

3. Edusave scholarship
	-> for students who are within top 10% of their schools level and course => in terms of academic performance and have demonstrated good conduct
