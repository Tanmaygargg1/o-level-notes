2 types of needs:
- Thing that people depend on for survival
- Things that people depend on for their well being

People => often share similar basic needs for survival
-> but they generally have different needs when it comes to their well being
-> people also have their interests in addition to their needs.

*What are Basic Needs*
People in society => often agree on their basic needs for survival, such as need for water, food and shelter.

*Needs for Well Being*
-> generally vary among individuals
Eg: An individual may see job as a need => as job provides him or her with an income for greater well being
-> another individual who has more wealth may not see having a job as a need

*What are Interests*
-> refer to things that are directly or indirectly related to fulfilment of needs.
Everyone who sees having a job as a need => could have an interest in owning a form of private transport for daily commute.
___
Situations when ones interests can be another persons need.
=> excersise can be an interest of healthy senior citizen, whereas it is a need for senior citizen who has been instructed by doctor to exercise daily.

For govt => to carry out its roles and functions effectively => govt has to identify needs and interests of society as a whole => and not just those of individuals.
-> govt has to look after all citizens not just some

hard to decide what is good for society when society is diverse
- would be even less agreement on what is considered good for society, because needs and interests of people can vary to a greater degree

2008 orchard road carpark controversy
- plan was to create more recreational areas for public to enjoy by converting portion of Orchard roads open air carpark into a green public space
	- however business owners were concerned that it would lead to lesser customers => especially people who drove there. 
	- Govt and public people => argued that it would enhance shopping experience, promoting foot traffic and create a more vibrant atmosphere. prioritized long term sustainability and attractiveness of the orchard area
