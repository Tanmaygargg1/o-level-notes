Although individuals may agree to certain needs and interests of society => it may still be challenging to decide what is good for society due to differing priorities.
=> challenge becomes greater when there are limited resources
___
#SocialStudiesExamples 
Singapore => land needed for various purposes => such as education, housing, business and recreation. 
Given limited area and land => crucial that land use is optimized => so that current and future needs of people are met
Elderly living in area => who have medical needs would like hospital to be built
However citizens in same area with school going children would prefer school to be built
