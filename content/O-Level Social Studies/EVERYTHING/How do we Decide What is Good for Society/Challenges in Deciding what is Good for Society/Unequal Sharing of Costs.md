-> refer to varying impact that decision has on different groups of people.
=> while decision may benefit society in general, it can have a negative impact on certain groups of people

#SocialStudiesExamples 
Building an expressway would improve traffic flow for commuters. 
However => this may come at a cost for those living near expressways who may be affected by the noise generated by the traffic.

When decision leads to an unequal sharing of costs => not everyone will agree that a decision is a good one
-> other hand: residents who are negatively impacted will say that it is not a good decision
___
#SocialStudiesExamples 
NIMBY syndrome (not in my backyard)
-> foreign dormitories being built near residential areas to give foreign workers better living conditions.
-> good intensions because it gives more respect to foreign workers and prevents them from living in poor conditions.
-> but people who stay near feels it might affect safety and cleanliness of area
-> some times, recreational facilities like basketball courts were sacrificed to build the dormitories
