___

Government => set of institutions by which society is ruled
Many countries adopt *democracy*
- Means rule of the people => which refers to the ability of citizens to participate in political decision making
(there are different types of democracy)

1. Representative Democracy
- citizens elect representatives to form a government to make decisions on their behalf. 
- Government in representative democracy =>does not have unlimited power to make decisions / carry out policies. 
	- it is required to act according to country's constitution

-> in representative democracy => essential that elections are free and fair
-> free election is one in which all citizens *are able to vote for the candidate of their choice*
-> fair election is one in which all votes have equal power + counted accurately

- Eligible citizens can vote. Voting should not be difficult for any particular group of people
- citizens are free from intimidation and have access to reliable information
- citizens can stand for election as candidates. no candidates are unfairly prevented from running for office
- voting is free from fraud. Every eligible voter only votes once and ballots are counted accurately.
