SG govt => 3 main functions:
- Make and pass laws
- implement and enforce laws
- interpret and apply laws
___
These function => carried out by 3 branches of govt.

Legislature => makes and passes laws
Executive => implements and enforces laws. 
Judiciary => interprets and applies laws.

Authority and power of these branches => derived from constitution => often referred to as supreme law.
