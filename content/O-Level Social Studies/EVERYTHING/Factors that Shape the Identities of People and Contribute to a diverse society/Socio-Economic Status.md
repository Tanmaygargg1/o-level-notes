Socio Economic status refers to an economic situation shared by a group of people in society. Measured based on combination of social and economic factors such as income, education, occupation and housing #SocialStudiesDefinitions 
___
Individuals or households income is one indicator of this
- helps govt identify people who may require additional assistance
- govt can provide more targeted support to those who need it more

Families with lower income => may face challenges => in providing the resources to support their children's development
- ensure children have a good start in life => SG govt introduced KidSTART program => as added support for children 6 and below. #SocialStudiesExamples
