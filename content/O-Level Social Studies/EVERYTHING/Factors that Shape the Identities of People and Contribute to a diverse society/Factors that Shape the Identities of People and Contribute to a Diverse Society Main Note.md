___
Diversity => refers to differences and variety.
- society is socio-culturally diverse => when people are of different races and ethnicities, religions and nationalities.
- Society is socio-economically diverse when people have different socio-economic backgrounds due to different levels of education, income and wealth

Diverse groups of people in society => there will likely be different beliefs, values, practices and views. 
- such differences can sometimes bring people closer together or lead to tensions and conflicts. 
- hence important for us to consider how we can respond to these situations.
