Social Mobility: individuals movement across socio economic positions is known as social mobility. #SocialStudiesDefinitions 
(movement can be upward or downward)

socio economic status may influence persons life experiences, it is not the only aspect of a persons identity and does not define him/her
___
Example: #SocialStudiesExamples 
- Mr Faaqih Hilmy Gozan's family faced financial difficulties
- when he was in JC => daily lunch was packet of white rice with soy sauce or chilli and sometimes an egg
- older brother died => worked many jobs to help out with families finances. 
	- made about $500 every month => gave most of it to his mother to finance household bills and siblings education
- Sponsorship helped him to eventually graduate with degree in civil engineering. 
	- he is now an engineer with local construction company, job that offers financial stability to his family.
