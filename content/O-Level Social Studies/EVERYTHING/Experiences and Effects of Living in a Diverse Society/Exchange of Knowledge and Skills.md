Diverse society like SG -> brings together people with different backgrounds, qualifications and experiences to share their knowledge and skills. 
-> outcome: creation of new ideas, methods and products. 

Example: #SocialStudiesExamples 
**Thermo Fisher Scientific**
- one of the largest medical device makers, with R&D center in Singapore
- Multinational company with over 2000 employees in Singapore and 80 000 employees worldwide.
- Singaporeans are trained in leadership skills and collaborate with employees of different cultures to sharpen their skills
- Collaborating with A* STAR to support their R&D in genetics and cancer research. Production of high quality equipment such as PPE and PCR test kids during Covid-19
