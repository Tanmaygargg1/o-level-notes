in Diverse society like SG => there are many opportunities => for people from different socio-cultural and socia-economic backgrounds => to interact with one another.

- differences in cultural backgrounds can sometimes lead to conflicting views on values, beliefs, practices and perspectives. 
	- This can then cause misunderstandings and unhappiness. 
		- This can then lead to negative sentiments, causing people to jump to conclusions about others

inaccurate and insensitive judgements and comments => often made based on ignorance and wrong assumptions
___
#### Stereotypes
Definition: generalized beliefs about a particular group of people are known as stereotypes #SocialStudiesDefinitions 

Example: I guess you can use any stereotype you know of: Indians are smelly, Malays are stupid etc. 
___
#### Prejudice
Definition: refers to attitudes and feelings towards other groups => that are not based on reason or fact. can come in the form of stereotyping, assumptions, generalization, beliefs or feelings about a group #SocialStudiesDefinitions 
- negative prejudice => can take form of insensitive and hurtful remarks or discriminatory actions
- Prejudice => can also take the form of *xenophobia* => which is directed against people of a specific nationality or from a particular country

Example of Prejudice: #SocialStudiesDefinitions 
- former PR sent his luxury car to car workshop => he and his son had to travel by MRT
- he posted a photograph of his son on the MRT on social media => captioned "daddy where is your care and who are all these poor people"
- later put up a post on social media of his son in his repaired car
- people who saw his posts were outraged. tried to apologize but they were deemed insincere
- result of intense public criticism => he was fired. 
- later left Singapore with his family. 
___
#### Discrimination
Prejudice can lead to discrimination
- when a person is prejudiced against specific group of people, he or she may act on his prejudiced beliefs and negative feelings by hurting or excluding that group. known as discrimination. #SocialStudiesDefinitions 
- Feelings of prejudice and acts of discrimination => because of someones race or ethnic group => known as racism. #SocialStudiesDefinitions Racism can be displayed by an individual, community or institution. 

Example of Discrimination: #SocialStudiesExamples 
- nurse had been on good terms with neighbors for 6 years
- changed in 2021 during COVID 19 pandemic => neighbors learnt that nurse and his wife were frontline workers
- neighbors harassed them => called them a virus before proceeding to spray daughters face with disinfectant, and poured disinfectant at their doorway. parents were also verbally abused
- Neighbors charged and pleaded guilty to their actions. 
___
### Impact of Stereotypes, Prejudice, Discrimination
- resentment and strained relationships among groups. 

Episodes of prejudice and discriminations, both through words and actions => can cause hurt and unnecessary suffering. 
Critical to promote open-mindedness, sensitivity towards feeling of others, respect and mutual understanding among various groups in society
