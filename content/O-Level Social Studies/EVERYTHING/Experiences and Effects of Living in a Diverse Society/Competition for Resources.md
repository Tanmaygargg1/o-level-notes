Inflow of foreigners into Singapore => meant to meet Singapore's shortage of manpower due to its low fertility rate and ageing population.

| Employment Pass                                                                                        | S-Pass                                                                               | Work Permit                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Foreign professionals, managers, executives earning at least 4.5k a month with suitable qualifications | Mid level skilled workers earning at least 2.5k a month who meet assessment criteria | Semi skilled migrant workers in the construction, manufacturing, marine shipyard, process or services sector or as domestic helpers |
___
#### Concerns of Singaporeans
- heightened competition with foreigners over limited resources
	- include Job opportunities, and infrastructure such as public housing and transportation.

Competition (real or perceived) => led to tensions, unhappiness and negative experiences among groups of people in the country.

Example: #SocialStudiesExamples 
- 2020 => survey conducted by REACH => govt feedback unit => founded that S'poreans generally agreed it was important for SG to be a regional hub => as it provides good opportunities for Sporeans. 
- also means remaining open to foreigners

#### Addressing concerns about foreigners in Singapore

| Stricter Workforce Control                                                                                                                                                         | Fair Consideration Framework                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| tightened criteria for EP (employment pass)<br><br>Raised the minimum qualifying salary => for S pass I.e a foreigner needs to earn a higher min salary before they can get a pass | Employers => must give fair consideration => to Singaporeans based on merit. <br><br>Employers => who carry out unfair hiring practices => that favor foreigners => will be dealt with<br><br>Employers submitting EP and S pass applications => need to advertise job vacancies on MyCareersFuture to facilitate job searched by Singaporeans and PRs |
Address concerns about availability of healthcare resources and public housing and increasingly congested public transport system => govt has made plans to improve amenities, urban spaces and transport services for Singaporeans. 

Example: LTA master plan 2040 => aimed at improving connectivity for people between their homes, communities and workplaces. #SocialStudiesExamples
