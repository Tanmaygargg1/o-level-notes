#### Interactions in a Diverse Society and Common Space
Societies become increasingly diverse => relationships among people can become more complex.
-> people have different beliefs, values, practices and views.

Interactions among diverse individuals and groups of people -> lead to exchange and appreciation of cultures, knowledge, skills. 
-> interactions may also create tensions => people negotiate differences.

Preserve harmony => must build society in which people can engage with each other => in spirit of trust, respect and mutual accommodation
___
 *common space* refers to a physical location and/or social space where different groups of people interact, cooperate or collaborate on projects or activities. #SocialStudiesDefinitions 

Allow people to:
- forge ties and establish mutual understanding between people of different races, ethnicities, religions and socio-economic statuses. 
- Create shared experiences and memories

Example of Common space #SocialStudiesExamples: void deck in public housing estates. 
-> open and sheltered areas on ground floor of most blocks of flats build by HDB. 
-> provide opportunities for residents to meet, interact and bond. 

Example of Common space: schools and their programs. (they mix, study and play together)
OBS => outward bound Singapore => engage in activities to improve community and environment. 
-> develop ruggedness, resilience and cohesion among students from diff. backgrounds. 
-> opportunity for students to create shared experiences and memories.

Example of Common space: social and cultural events, programs where people can engage and develop relationships with one another

#### Experiences and Effects of Living in a Diverse Society
Peoples attitudes towards interaction is important as well.
attitude of *openness* or being open minded helps promote harmonious interactions in a diverse society. 
-> open mindedness: more willing to actively listen, respectfully engage and appreciate differences between 1 another.
