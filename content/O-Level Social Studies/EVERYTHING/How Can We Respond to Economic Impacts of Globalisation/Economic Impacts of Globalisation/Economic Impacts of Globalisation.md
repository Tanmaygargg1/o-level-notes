[[Employment Opportunities and Challenges Experienced by Individuals]]
___
* those with better education and sought after skills -> may get better employment opp. contrast -> those with less education and limited skills risk being replaced by cheaper labour from other countries or by automation and AI

- individuals living in well connected locations (e.g. global cities or urban areas) -> have more opp. less accessibly locations (e.g. rural areas with less developed infrastructure) -> tend to have fewer econ. opp. as they are not well connected to global flows of goods, money people, info

- priorities of employers and govt in promoting skills + career development for local workforce, or seeking to reduce costs -> lead to diff. impacts on individuals and groups
