to large degree -> individuals experience econ impacts of globalisation -> as citizens / residents of their country
* when there is economic growth in country -> more econ opp. such as employment -> available to individuals in that country
* econ decline -> job losses and affect individuals earnings

___
### Employment Opp for Individuals
globalisation -> create employment opp + new ways of working for individuals.
* when country is open to global econ -> businesses can reach larger market -> resulting in higher demand for goods + services from country. 
	* creates more jobs + provides livelihood for individuals in country

* presence of MNCs and increased investments like FDIs -> encourage use of more advanced tech -> lead to creation of jobs involving higher skills and wages.

* advancements in digital tech + transportation -> also enabled individuals to enjoy increasing mobility beyond country's borders. 
	* means that individuals -> may find jobs overseas -> better match their skills and job aspirations

___
### Employment Challenges Experienced by Individuals
* MNCs -> may relocate operations to other countries -> save costs / access expertise available elsewhere. Individuals who are employed in these operations may lose their jobs. 

* advancements in tech -> may cause some types of jobs -> to be automated as machines are increasingly used to perform work. individuals working in these type of jobs -> lose jobs

* with greater movement of labour as individuals relocate to work in other countries -> local workers in those countries -> face greater competition from foreign labour in job market
