Identity=> refers to how you perceive yourself and how you believe others perceive you.

People from same country => have a sense of national identity
Comes from a shared belief of connectedness to one another + belonging to the country

When citizens share common spaces, practices, experiences => they develop a sense of belonging and emotional attachment to the country.

Example:
* Singaporeans consider => hawker centers as an important common space that represents Singapore's multicultural heritage.
	* 2019 => many Singaporeans, including those living abroad -> pledged their support for countries nomination of its hawker culture => to UNECO's representative list of intangible cultural heritage of humanity => which was recognized in December 2020
* Singlish => represents harmony in Singapore. Was born from generations of different races living and working together harmoniously.
	* Rojak Language > represents melting pot of races, religions, cultures, that helped shape Singapore into what it is today. 
	* it is the glue that helps Singaporeans connect easily.
	* when travelling overseas => it is easy to spot another Singaporean.
	* Key word; Familiarity.
