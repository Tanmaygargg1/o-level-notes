Citizens => enjoy rights as members of that country.
___
Definition: Rights refer to freedom and privileges that citizens in a country have. 
#SocialStudiesDefinitions
___
Rights of citizens=> usually laid out in the constitution => protected by law and should be respected by everyone in the country
___
**Article number/rights:  

9 -> liberty of person - no person should be detained unlawfully

10 -> slavery and forced labor prohibited - no person be made a slave

11 -> protection against retrospective criminal laws and repeated trials - no person can be punished for doing something which was not an offense when he did it

12 -> equal protection - all persons are equal before law and entitled to equal protection under law

13 -> freedom of movement - subject to certain legal exceptions -> every citizen of SG has right to move freely throughout SG + right to live in any part of singapore

14 -> freedom of speech, assembly, association – subject to certain restrictions -> every citizen of SG has the right to free speech and expression; assembly peacefully and without arms + form associations. 

15 -> freedom of religion - every person has the right to practice one's religion. 

16 -> rights in respect of education - no citizen of SG -> discriminated against on grounds of religion, race, descent, place of birth -> when it comes to the administration of any educational institution by a public authority.**
