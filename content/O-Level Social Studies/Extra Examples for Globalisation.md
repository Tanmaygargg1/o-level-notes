## Nutella
#SocialStudiesExamples 

**How many countries is Nutella exported to?**
- 110 countries between March 2023 and Feb 2024

**Nutella Headquarters, and Name of Company:**
- is based in Italy
- Ferraro international

**What are the ingredients of Nutella and where are the ingredients sources from:**
Turkey - hazelnuts for its trademark flavour 
cocoa -  harvested from Nigeria. 
Sugar - is shipped from Brazil.
palm oil and vanilla - supplied by Malaysia and France.

Factories in Europe, Russia, North America, and South America.

**World Nutella Day, 5th Feb, and why is it a good example of globalisation:**
- fact that we can get Nutella at almost every part of the world shows that borders are becoming less defined and the world is becoming very *interconnected* [[Essays on Globalisation#^bb55c0]]

**In the Case study of Nutella and Ferraro International S.A, Ferraro international is an example of a multi national corporation. What do you think is the definition of an MNC**
- Company that has business operations in at least one country other than its home country
