### Uses of Esters

**Fragrances and Flavours**
- Many esters have sweet, fruity smells.
- Used in **perfumes, deodorants, and flavourings** for foods and drinks.  
    _Example:_ Ethyl acetate smells like pears, isoamyl acetate smells like bananas.

**Solvents**
- Esters like **ethyl acetate** and **butyl acetate** are good solvents.
- Used in **paints, varnishes, and nail polish removers** because they dissolve other substances well.

**Plasticizers**
- Some esters are added to plastics to make them more flexible

**Pharmaceuticals**
- Esters are used in medicines, either as active ingredients or to improve taste and absorption.
 
**Manufacture of Synthetic Materials**
- Esters are involved in making **polymers** like polyester fibres and plastics.
