General formula of ester with 1 ester linkage is: $C_nH_{2n+1}COOC_mH_{2m+1}$

$C_nH{2n+1}CO-$ is derived form the carboxylic acid which has reacted to form the ester -> hence n = 0, 1,2,3 and so on. 
$-OC_mH_{2m+1}$ is derived from the alcohol -> which has reacted to form the ester. hence, m = 1, 2,3 so on.
