when carboxylic acid is warmed with alcohol in the presence of a few drops of concentrated sulfuric acid as a catalyst -> an ester and water are formed.

![[Pasted image 20250607141853.png]]
- during the formation of ethyl ethanoate -> the $-OH$ group from ethanoic acid is replaced by the $CH_3CH_2-O-$  group from ethanol.

![[Pasted image 20250607141943.png]]

since an ester is formed in this reaction -> it can be referred to as an *esterification reaction*
reaction can also be known as *condensation reaction* -> because 2 molecules react to form a single molecule(ethyl ethanoate) with the loss of a small molecule (water)

although alcohol has the hydroxyl ($-OH-$) functional group -> this group does not dissociate to form $-OH-$ ions in water.
- hence alcohol is not a base and esterification is not an acid-base reaction
___
>[!note]
>opposite of esterification is hydrolysis
