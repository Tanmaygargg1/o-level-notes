- have higher melting and boiling points compared to the alcohols and hydrocarbons with the *Same number of carbons*
- carboxylic acids show a gradual trend in boiling points.
	- <span style="background:#fff88f">boiling points increase as the molecular size increases. </span>

| Carboxylic Acid | Boiling Point $/\degree C$ |
| --------------- | -------------------------- |
| methanoic acid  | 101                        |
| ethanoic acid   | 118                        |
| propanoic acid  | 141                        |
