Ethanoic acid ($CH_3COOH$) is a *colourless liquid at r.t.p* and has the *smell of vinegar* 
- vinegar is a dilute solution $abt 4%) of ethanoic acid
-> used in food as preservative and

-> it is formed by oxidation of ethanol.
___
### Oxidation of Ethanol by Atmospheric Oxygen
Vinegar, dilute solution of ethanoic acid -> produced in industries by oxidation of a solution of ethanol/
ethanol reacts with *atmospheric oxygen* in presence of 1 type of bacteria that is usually present in air to become ethanoic acid.

### Oxidation of Ethanol by Acidified Potassium Manganate(VII)
in lab -> ethanoic acid prepared by heating a mixture of ethanol and acidified potassium manganate(VII) i.e. a mixture of potassium manganate(VII) solution and dilute sulfuric acid
- redox reaction takes place [[Oxidation and Reduction Main Note]]
![[Pasted image 20250607141143.png|400]]
Potassium manganate(VII) acts as *oxidising agent*. it is *reduced*
changes from *purple to colourless*

ethanol acts as *reducing agent*. it is *oxidised* to ethanoic acid.
