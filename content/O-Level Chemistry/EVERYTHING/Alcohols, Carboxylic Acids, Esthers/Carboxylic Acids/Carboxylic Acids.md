### General Formula
General formula of carboxylic acids with *1 carboxyl group* is $C_nH_{2n+1}COOH$
where n is number of carbon atoms in each molecule <span style="background:#d3f8b6">minus 1</span>.

>hydrogen atom in the carboxyl group can form a hydrogen ion in aqueous solution. hence members of the series are *monobasic acids* [[Acids]]. 
>however they are *weak acids* [[Strong and Weak Acids]] because of partial dissociation in water producing a low concentration of hydrogen ions.
