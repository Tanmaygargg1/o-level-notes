##### Alcohols Exhibit General Trend in Physical Properties
- solubility of alcohol decreases down the homologous series as the carbon chain increases in length
- melting and boiling points of alcohols increase down the homologous series. as the molecular size increases, the intermolecular forces of attraction increases. 


| Alcohol  | Physical State | Solubility in Water | Boiling Point / $\degree C$ |
| -------- | -------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------- |

| Ethanol  | Liquid         | Very Soluble        | 78                          |
| Propanol | Liquid         | Soluble             | 97                          |
| Butanol  | Liquid         | Slightly Soluble    | 118                         |
