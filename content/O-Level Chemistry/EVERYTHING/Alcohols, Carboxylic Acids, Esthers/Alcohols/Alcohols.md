### Formula and Names of Alcohols
>[!definition]
>Alcohols are organic compounds with the hydroxyl ($-OH$) functional group
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

all alcohols contain carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen atoms.

##### General Formula of Alcohols
- general formula of alcohols with 1 hydroxyl ($-OH$) group is $C_nH_{2n+1}OH$, where n is the number of carbon atoms in each molecule

##### Name and Formulae of Alcohols
- name ends with suffix 'ol'

first 4 members of alcohol homologous series 
- hydroxyl group is attached to the *terminal carbon atom*

>[!note]

#### Isomerism
- other than straight chain isomer of butanol shown above -> 3 other alcohols have same formula $C_4H_9OH$ 
- full structural formula of the isomers can be obtained by shifting the position of the hydroxyl group or by shifting the alkyl groups.
