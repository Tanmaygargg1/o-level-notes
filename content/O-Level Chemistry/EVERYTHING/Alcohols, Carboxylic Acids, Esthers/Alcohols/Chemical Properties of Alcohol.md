### Combustion
Complete combustion of alcohol produces carbon dioxide and water.\

<span style="background:#fff88f">Ethanol burns very easily with a pale yellow flame to form carbon dioxide and water vapour. </span>
- exothermic reaction - produces lot of heat (that is why it is considered an alternate fuel)
Ethanol can also undergo incomplete combustion if there is limited volume of oxygen. 

>[!example]
>methanol burns in oxygen as follows:
>methanol + oxygen -> carbon dioxide + water
>$2CH_3OH(l) + 3O_2(g) \rightarrow 2CO_2(g) + 4H_2O(g)$

___
### Oxidation
- Most alcohols undergo oxidation -> when heated with oxidising agent -> such as acidified potassium manganate(VII)
- for alcohols with hydroxyl ($-OH$) group bonded to the terminal carbon atom -> <span style="background:#d3f8b6">carboxylic acid</span> will be obtained upon oxidation

Acidified potassium manganate(VII) changes colour from *purple to colourless*
