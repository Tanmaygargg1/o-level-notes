### Forming Polyamides
when monomers of dicarboxylic acid and diamine are combined, polyamides are formed. 
in polymerisation -> water is eliminated. 
water is formed from the $-OH$ of the carboxylic group and the $-H$ from the amine group.
monomers are linked together by amide linkages. 

hence in repeat unit -> we see functional group (image below) in the middle -> while dicarboxylic end will have the $-OH$ removed from the $-COOH$ group and the diamine end will have the $-H$ removed from the $NH_2$ group.

### Forming Polyesters
when monomers are dicarboxylic acid and diol are combined -> polyesters are formed. 
in polymerisation -> water is again eliminated. 
water is formed from the $-OH$ of the carboxylic group and the $-H$ from the hydroxyl group. 
monomers are linked together by ester linkages.

hence in repeat unit -> we see that functional group (image below) in the middle, while the dicarboxylic end will have $-OH$ removed from $-COOH$ group and the diol end will have the $-H$ removed from the $-OH$ group.
