>[!definition]
Molecule of element: Electrically neutral particle made up of 2 or more atoms of the same element, which are chemically combined through sharing of electrons
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

>[!definition]
>Molecule of Compound: Electrically neutral particle made up of 2 or more atoms of different elements which are chemically combined through sharing of electrons
#ChemistryDefinitions 

___
>[!definition]
>Valency refers to the number of electrons that must be lost, gained or shared in order for the atom to attain noble gas configuration.
#ChemistryDefinitions 

___
>[!definition]
>Covelant Bond is the strong electrostatic forces of attraction between 2 positively charged protons in the nuclei of the 2 atoms and the negatively charged electrons which are shared between 2 atoms.
#ChemistryDefinitions 

___
Covalent bond is formed between atoms only* -> not ions or molecules
occur between non metals.

Triple bond -> ≡ -> formed when total 6 valence electrons are shared.
Double bond -> = -> formed when total 4 valence electrons are shared
Single bond -> - -> formed when total 2 valence electrons are shared.

**Covalent Molecules**
- covalent molecules can be made from atoms of same or different elements

- Covalent molecules -> mostly simple molecules
	- means that they have a countable number of atoms in a fixed ratio

Some covalent substances -> exist as giant molecules which have uncountable number of atoms in each molecule.
