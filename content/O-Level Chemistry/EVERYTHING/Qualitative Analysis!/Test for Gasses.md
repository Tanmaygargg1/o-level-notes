# Test for Odourless Gasses

| Gas                    | Odor and Color         | Test                                 | Observation                                                                          |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Hydrogen, $H_2$        | Odorless and colorless | ![[Pasted image 20240825150232.png]] | Burning Splint is extinguished with a pop sound                                      |
| Oxygen, $O_2$          | Odorless and colorless | ![[Pasted image 20240825150245.png]] | Glowing splint is rekindled (catches fire again)                                     |
| Carbon dioxide, $CO_2$ | Odorless and colorless | ![[Pasted image 20240825150300.png]] | White precipitate is formed. <br><br>precipitate dissolves upon further bubbling<br> |
# Test for Pungent Gasses

| Gas                    | Odor and Color                        | Test                                 | Observation                                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chlorine $Cl_2$        | Yellow-green gas with a pungent smell | ![[Pasted image 20240825151013.png]] | Damp blue litmus paper turns red and is then bleached     |
| Sulfur Dioxide, $SO_4$ | Colorless gas with a pungent smell    | ![[Pasted image 20240825151028.png]] | Purple acidified potassium manganate(VII) turns colorless |
| Ammonia, $NH_3$        | Colorless gas with pungent smell      | ![[Pasted image 20240825151040.png]] | Damp red litmus paper turns blue                          |
