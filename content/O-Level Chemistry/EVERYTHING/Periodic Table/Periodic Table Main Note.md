#### Important Information:
Non metals with 7 valence electrons are generally poor conductors of electricity -> as they do not have free electrons to carry an electric charge

Elements with 7 valence electrons usually form diatomic molecules rather than monatomic in their natural state.

>[!sample]
>explain why no elements in group 18 are shown in the table depicting oxidation states
 All elements in table have a *possible oxidation state* which imply they *can be chemically bonded with another element* OR table does not show grp 18 elements as there are *non with oxidation state 0*
 >Group 18 elements are inert because they have stable electronic configuration with fully filled valence shell and thus will not chemically bond with other elements
