##### #1
1. When atom of metal -> react with atoms of an non-metal to form an ionic compound -> the metallic atoms lose electrons to the non metallic atoms
2. Energy is required -> to remove one mole of valence electrons -> from one mole of atoms of metal to form one mole of positive ions. This energy is known as **ionisation energy**
3. Size of atoms (and atomic radius) -> are also different from the size of ions (and **ionic radius**) formed by the atoms
