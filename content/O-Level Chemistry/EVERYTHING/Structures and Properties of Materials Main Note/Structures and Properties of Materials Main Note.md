**Differences between Elements and Compounds**
>[!definition]
>Element is pure substance that cannot be broken down into 2 or more simpler substances by chemical processes and electrolysis
#ChemistryDefinitions 

- Atoms - smallest particle of element that have chemical properties of that element
- Element - only made up of 1 type of atoms
*note element can burn to form a new compound - reacts with oxygen to form an oxide.*
Some elements exist as atoms, some elements exist as molecules*

Molecule: particle which is made up of 2 or more atoms chemically combined together
![[Pasted image 20240922203335.png|700]]
Non metals to left, metals to right. transition metals in the middle.
note* 7 diatomic elements are: F2, Cl2, Br2, I2, O2, N2, H2.
Diatomic molecules - 2 atoms chemically combined together
Polyatomic molecules - 3 or more atoms chemically combined together.
  
Periodic table:
- Group: vertical: elements have similar chemical properties. 
Group number = number of valence electrons
- Period: horizontal: number of electron shells. Gradually changes from metallic to non-metallic.

### Compounds
>[!definition]
>Pure Substance containing 2 or more different elements chemically combined in a fixed ratio. (fixed proportion by mass)
#ChemistryDefinitions 

- Compounds formed when atoms of 2 or more elements - react with each other 
	- During reaction - these atoms are chemically combined together -> results in formation of new particles such as molecules and ions that make up the compound

Formation of compounds - usually involves change in energy. (Exothermic or endothermic)

Compounds - can be separated int constituents by:
- Thermal decomposition - exposing compound to strong heat 
- Electrolysis - passing electric current through the compound 
- (only chemical not physical means)

Compounds do not have similar properties to constituent elements.
___
### Mixtures
>[!definition]
>Mixture is an impure substance that is made up of 2 or more elements or compounds that are not chemically combined
#ChemistryDefinitions 


Diff between mixture and compounds
- Mixtures - do not have substances in fixed ratio
- Mixtures can consist of multiple elements OR compounds

Mixtures - physically combined
-> can be separated using techniques such as:
- Paper chromatography
- Filtration
- Distillation 
- Little energy is required to separate mixtures 
- Physical means are used
Similar properties to constituent elements
