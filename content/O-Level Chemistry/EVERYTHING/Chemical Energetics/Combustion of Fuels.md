- Fuels -> substances that can burn easily in air to give out energy
- Examples of fuels -> fossil fuels such as coal (mainly made up of carbon), petroleum (crude oil), and natural gas (mainly made up of methane). other fuels are petrol and hydrogen gas. more about it in (link to chapter 21)

- Comparison of complete and incomplete combustion using combustion of methane.
![[Pasted image 20250326220657.png|400]]
___

| Type of Combustion     | Complete          | Incomplete            |
| ---------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------- |
| Limiting reactant      | fuel (methane)    | oxygen gas            |
| Excess reactant        | oxygen gas        | fuel(methane)         |
| Products formed        | $CO_2$ and $H_2O$ | $CO$, $H_2O$, C(soot) |
| Type of Energy change  | endothermic       | exothermic            |
| Amt of Energy released | larger            | lower                 |
>[!important]
>during combustion of carbon containing fuels, both CO and $CO_2$ will be formed
