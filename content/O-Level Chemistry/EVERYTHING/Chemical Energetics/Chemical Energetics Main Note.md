>[!understanding]
>System vs Surroundings
>System: the reactants ALONE e.g. NaOH and HCl
>Surroundings: everything else, e.g. beaker, water, thermometer, cup
>- surroundings is not directly involved in the chemical reaction while the system is

### Answer Techniques
**Explaining Why Reaction is Endo/Exo**
1. <span style="background:#d3f8b6">State that bond breaking is endo and bond making is exo</span>
2. State energy of products higher/lower than reactants
3. State whether energy taken in from surr to system for breaking bonds <span style="background:#b1ffff">in</span>  xx mol of xxx compound is more/less than energy released from system to surr. to <span style="background:#b1ffff">make new bonds</span> to form xxx mol of xxx compound
4. Net energy transfer from system to surr / surr to system
5. Positive/Negative enthalpy change. 
6. Thus Endo/Exo
___
>[!important]
>important to state direction of energy movement ; *taken in* or *given out*
