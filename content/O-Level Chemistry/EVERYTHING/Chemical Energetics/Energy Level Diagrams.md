## Exothermic Reactions - Energy Level Diagram
- Energy cannot be created or destroyed, and it can only be changed from 1 form to another and there is energy content in a substance. [[Energy]]
- As exothermic reaction -> gives heat to surroundings -> total energy of products ($E_p$) will be lower than the total energy of the reactants ($E_r$)
- Since $E_P < E_r$, $E_P - E_r = negative (-ve)$ 

Difference in energy level of reactant and products in exothermic reaction can be represented by energy level diagram
![[Pasted image 20250326211913.png|200]]
$$\Delta H = E_P - E_r = -ve$$ (from diagram $E_r > E_p$)
- In exothermic reaction -> products are *more energetically stable* because -> they have less energy than the reactants. Products are more stable at lower energy levels. Thus, many reactions which occurs spontaneously are exothermic reactions

## Endothermic Reactions - Energy Level Diagram
- Energy cannot be created or destroyed, and it can only be changed from 1 form to another and there is energy content in a substance. [[Energy]]
- As endothermic reaction absorbs heat from the surroundings -> total energy of the products ($E_P$) will be higher than the total energy of the reactants ($E_r$)

- Since $E_P > E_r$ 
$$\Delta H = total\ energy \ of\ products\ - \ total \ energy \ of \ reactants$$
$$ = $E_{P}- E_{r} = positive(+ve_)$$
- difference in energy level of reactants and products in endothermic reaction can be represented by energy level diagram
![[Pasted image 20250326212518.png|200]]
- In endothermic reaction -> products are *less energetically stable* -> because they have more energy than the reactants. Products are unstable at higher energy levels hence they will break down easily.
