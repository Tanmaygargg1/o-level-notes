###### Classification of Reactive Electrodes
- Electrodes made up of any metal more reactive than platinum . [[Reactivity Series Main Note]]

>[!note]
>- Concept of selective discharge of cations still applies -> as cations are reduced at the cathode. 
>- Concept of selective discharge of anions DOES NOT APPLY as anode -> which is reactive electrode -> will undergo oxidation instead of anions

>- The **cathode reaction** is **not affected** by whether the electrodes are inert or reactive.

>[!sample]
>the copper anode ionises -> Cu(s) -> Cu2+ + 2e-. this *replaces* the number of Cu2+ ions discharged at the cathode. The Cu2+ ions that get discharged at the cathode come mainly from the copper anode.
