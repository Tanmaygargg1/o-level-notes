- Device which uses chemical reactions to generate electricity. 
**Energy conversion:**
- chemical energy -> electrical energy
___
battery is made up of simple cells. chemical reactions occurring in simple cells produce electricity -> which is used to drive non spontaneous redox reactions  in electrolytic cells. 
thus battery is not found in the set up of simple cell.
___
###### Parts of Simple Cell
- bulb/voltmeter
- negative electrode (anode)
	- similar to negative terminal of battery
	- more reactive metal electrode
- positive electrode (cathode)
	- similar to positive terminal of battery
	- less reactive metal electrode
- connecting wires (connect bulb/voltmeter to anode and cathode)
- electrolyte (3 common types)
	- aqueous solution of compounds of grp 1 metals (salt solution of grp 1 metals)
	- aqueous solution of the compound of the less reactive metal which is used to make cathode (e.g. salt solution of less reactive metal)
	- dilute acids

Unlike electrolytic cell -> negative electrode in a simple cell is the anode, while the positive 
electrode is the cathode.
like electrolytic cell -> reduction takes place at cathode and oxidation takes place at anode.

**In order for simple cells to generate electricity, there must be a difference in reactivity of metals used to make anode and cathode**
- greater the *difference in reactivity between the 2 metal electrodes* -> the greater the potential difference (or voltage) produced in simple cell
- -> the greater the amt of electricity generated and the brighter the bulb

**Why is anode the more reactive metal electrode (negative electrode)**
- more reactive metal loses electrons more readily -> to form positive ions -> than less reactive metal
- thus more reactive metal electrode -> is anode -> as oxidation occurs at anode
- since negative charged electrons produced at anode -> it is the *negative* electrode

**Why is cathode less reactive metal electrode (positive electrode)**
- negatively charged electrons produced -> will flow from negative to positive. since electrons are produced at anode -> the anode is the negative electrode -> while cathode is positive electrode
- electrons will flow thru external circuit -> from the more reactive metal electrode (anode: site of oxidation) to the less reactive metal electrode -> where it will be involved in the reduction of cations. thus less reactive metal is the cathode as cathode is the site of reduction

**Concept of Selective discharge of cations still applies**
- as cations are reduced at cathode

**Concept of selective discharge of anions does not apply**
- as anode -> which is reactive electrode -> will undergo oxidation instead of anions

**Comparison of 3 simple cells which have diff electrolytes**
- potassium chloride solution
- aqueous silver nitrate
- dilute sulfuric acid
-> these cells used *magnesium* and *silver* electrodes
