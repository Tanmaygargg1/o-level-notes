- gaseous fossil fuel consisting mainly of *methane*
- natural gas is colourless and odourless. 


Methane burnt in *excess* oxygen -> chemical equation written as:
-> methane + oxygen -> carbon dioxide + water
-> $CH_4(g) + 2O_2(g) \rightarrow CO_2(g) + 2H_2O(g)$

natural gas considered the *cleanest* fossil fuel in terms of its impact on the environment.
___
before natural gas is transported to consumers -> it is usually liquified first.
- this conversion from gas to liquid reduces the volume of natural gas by 600 times.

unlike gaseous fuels - liquified natural gas (LNG) - does not burn too easily and is thus safer for consumers. 
*additive* like tetrahydrothiophene (THT) -> which has pungent smell -> uusally added to natural gas.
- smell alerts if gas leak occurs.
