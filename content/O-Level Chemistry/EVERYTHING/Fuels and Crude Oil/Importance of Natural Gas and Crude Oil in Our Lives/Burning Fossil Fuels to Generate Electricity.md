Fossil fuels -> important -> largest sources of energy for electricity generation

Appliances such as:
- air con
- water heaters
- refrigerators
-> consume a lot of electricity

Some modes of transport also powered by electricity, while others directly powered by fossil fuels.
____
**Fossil Fuels** -> formed from decaying plants and animals from millions of years ago.
- are mainly organic compounds that contain only carbon and hydrogen -> known as [[Hydrocarbons Main Note]]

When fossil fuels burnt in presence of oxygen -> main products formed -> are $CO_2$ and water. 
- this exothermic reaction -> known as **Combustion** [[Exothermic and Endothermic Reactions]]
- thermal energy generated from combustion of fossil fuels can be converted to other forms of energy. 

Crude oil + natural gas -> common forms of fossil fuels.
