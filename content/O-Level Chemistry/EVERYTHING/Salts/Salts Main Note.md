#### What is a salt
<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">since all Salts are ionic compounds, you need knowledge of common ions to write chemical equations: </mark>[[Common Substances]]
>[!definition]
>Salt is an ionic compound which is made by the reactions of acids with metals, carbonates, or bases, or alkalis. In these reactions, when 1 or more hydrogen ions of an acid is replaced by a metallic ion or an ammonium ion, salt is formed.
> #ChemistryDefinitions

## Deciding How a salt can be prepared
Factors to Consider:
* solubility of salt in water
* solubility of starting materials in water*

**If the salt is soluble > it is usually prepared from reaction of acids. if it is insoluble > it is usually prepared by [[Precipitation Method for Preparing Salts]]
