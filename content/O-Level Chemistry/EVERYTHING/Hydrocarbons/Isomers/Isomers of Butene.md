#### Straight Chain Isomers
other than diff. placements of alkyl groups, different placements of the functional group C=C also gives rise to isomerism

In butene -> the C=C bond can be placed at the side or in the centre -> giving rise to 2 straight chain isomers of butene -> but-1-ene and but-2-ene.

### butene and but-2-ene

| Organic Compound                            | Butene                                    | But-2ene                                  |

#### Similarity:
- both have same molecular formula, $C_4H_8$

#### Differences: 

| Organic Compound     | Butene                                                                                                                                                                                                 | But-2ene                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| structural formula   | $CH_3CH_{2}CHCH_{2}$                                                                                                                                                                                   | $CH_{3}CHCHCH_{3}$                                                                                                                                                                               |
| arrangement of atoms | - in this straight chain butene -> position of functional group, carbon-carbon double bond -> is between the 1st and 2nd carbon atoms in the carbon chain<br>![[Pasted image 20250602150009.png\|200]] | - in this straight chain butene -> position of functional group -> carbon carbon double bond -> is between 2nd and 3rd carbon atoms in carbon chain<br>![[Pasted image 20250602150026.png\|200]] |
>[!sample]
>are these compounds isomers? explain
>- Yes. 
>- both compounds have same molecular formula of $C_4H_8$
>- but they have different structural formula. the position of the carbon-carbon double bond is between 1st and 2nd carbon atoms in butene but this functional group is between 2nd and 3rd carbon atoms in but-2ene.
