>[!example]
>butane obtained from the fractional distillation of crude oil can be converted to a more useful chemical, such as methylpropane by using a catalytic process.
>
>methylpropane is a starting material for important petrochemical products. it is also used as a refrigerant to transfer heat from 1 area to another. 

>[!definition]
>Compounds that have same molecular formulae but different structural formulae are called isomers
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

Isomerism can occur -> when length of main carbon chain differs.
- *straight chain isomers* -> contain longest main carbon chains
- *branched chain isomers* -> contain shorter main carbon chains


- different structural properties -> thus properties will be different.

**a) Molecules of compounds with same molecular formula have: **
- same type of atoms
- same number of each type of atoms

**Molecules of compounds with different structural formula have diff. arrangement of atoms. Differences can be in terms of:**
- functional groups (or have functional grp vs no functional grp)
- position of functional group
- type of bond
- number of each type of bonds.

### Importance of Isomerism
- structure of a drug molecule is an important factor in determining its effectiveness when interacting with various biological molecules in the body. 
- thus knowledge of isomerism -> help pharmaceutical scientists in introducing drugs (medications) that are safer and more effective alternatives of existing drugs

#### Alkyl Groups
- alkyl groups is commonly found in branched chain alkanes and alkenes
- this group is obtained by removing a hydrogen atom from an alkane and therefore have the general molecular formula $C_nH_{2n+1}$
Examples:
- methyl: $CH_3$
- ethyl: $CH_3CH_2-$
- propyl: $CH_3CH_2CH_2-$
- butyl: $CH_3CH_2CH_2CH_2-$
