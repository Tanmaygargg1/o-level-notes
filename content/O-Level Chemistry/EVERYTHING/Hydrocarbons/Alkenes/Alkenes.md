>[!definition]
> Alkenes are a homologous series of unsaturated hydrocarbons that contain 1 or more carbon carbon double covalent bonds. 
> #ChemistryDefinitions 
- molecules of alkenes consist of only carbon and hydrogen atoms
- all 4 valence electrons of each carbon atom are involved in covalent bonding
- at least 1 of the carbon atoms will form a double covalent bond with another carbon atom in the molecules of all alkenes.
- alkenes are covalent substances with simple molecular structure. 

Carbon-Carbon double bond -> is a double covalent bond between 2 carbon atoms.

### Characteristics of members of alkenes
- same general formula,$C_nH_{2n}$
- same functional group, carbon-carbon double bond, hence have similar chemical properties
- each member of the series differs from the next by $-CH_2-$
- physical properties which show a steady gradual change as we go down the series due to an increase in size and mass of molecules.
	- increase in melting and boiling points
	- increase in density
	- increase in viscosity (only for substances in liquid state)
	- decrease in solubility in organic solvents such as benzene and tetrachloromethane ($CCl_4$)
	- for explanation: refer to [[Physical Properties of Alkanes]]
