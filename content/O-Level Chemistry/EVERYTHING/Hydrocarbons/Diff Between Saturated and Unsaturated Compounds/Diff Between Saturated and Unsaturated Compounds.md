### Saturated and Unsaturated Hydrocarbons
hexane is an alkane whereas hexene is an alkene.

Although both alkenes and alkanes are hydrocarbons -> their chemical properties are different.

Alkanes are *saturated hydrocarbons* -> as they only contain single covalent bonds between the carbon atoms. 
- in saturated hydrocarbons -> all carbon atoms are bonded to 4 other atoms

Alkenes are *unsaturated hydrocarbons* -> as they contain double covalent bonds between the carbon atoms. 
- in unsaturated hydrocarbons -> more atoms can be added across the carbon-carbon double bond.
___
### Test for Unsaturation
*aqueous bromine* is a reagent commonly used in laboratories -> to test for unsaturation

- brown solution which consists of bromine dissolved in water. 
![[Pasted image 20250531130219.png|400]]


| **Substance**   | Unsaturated Hydrocarbon <br>(alkene)                                                                                                                     | Saturated Hydrocarbon<br>(alkane)                                                                                            |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reagent**     | Aqueous bromine / bromine water/ bromine solution                                                                                                        | Aqueous bromine / bromine water/ bromine solution                                                                            |
| **Test**        | Add few drops of hydrocarbon to test tube containing aqueous bromine                                                                                     | Add few drops of hydrocarbon to test tube containing aqueous bromine                                                         |
| **Observation** | Red brown aqueous bromine is decolourised / turns colourless                                                                                             | red brown aqueous bromine remains unchanged.                                                                                 |
| **Explanation** | Ethene has a carbon-carbon double bond that will undergo addition reaction with red brown aqueous bromine to form colourless liquid of 1,2 dibromoethane | ethane does not have a carbon-carbon double bond thus it will not undergo addition reaction with red-brown aqueous bromine.  |
