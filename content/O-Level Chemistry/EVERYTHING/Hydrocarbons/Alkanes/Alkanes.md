#### Formulae and Names of Alkanes
>[!definition]
>Alkanes are a homologous series of saturated hydrocarbons that contain only carbon-carbon single bonds and carbon-hydrogen bonds
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

carbon-carbon single bond is a single covalent bond between 2 carbon atoms.

- all 4 valence electrons of each carbon atom are involved in covalent bonding. each carbon atom only form single bonds (4) with other atoms in molecules of alkanes. 
- alkanes are covalent substances with simple molecular structure. 
___
#### General Formula of Alkanes
general formula of alkanes is $C_nH_{2n + 2}$ 
- n is the number of carbon atoms in each molecule.

When there is 1 carbon atom in alkane molecule -> n = 1. 
So number of hydrogen atoms will be 2(1) + 2 = 4. 
Hence formula of alkane is $CH_4$
___
#### Naming Alkanes
Name of alkane ends with -ane-

| Name    | Number of Carbon Atoms | Molecular Formula |
| ------- | ---------------------- | ----------------- |
| Methane | 1                      | $CH_4$            |
| Ethane  | 2                      | $C_2H_6$          |
| Propane | 3                      | $C_3H_8$          |
| Butane  | 4                      | $C_4H_10$         |
>[!definition]
>each member of the alkane homologous series differs from the next by a $-CH_2-$ unit. E.g. ethane differs from methane by having an additional $-CH_2-$ unit.

## Characteristics
- same general formula (above)
- similar chemical properties (similar chemical reactions - combustion and substitution reactions)
- each member of the series differs from the next by $-CH_2-$
- physical properties which show a steady gradual change as we go down the series due to increase in size and mass of molecule
