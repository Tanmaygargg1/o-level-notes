### Strong Acids

>[!definition]
>The term strength refers to the extent of ionization of an acid molecule to produce hydrogen ions in aqueous solutions.
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

>[!definition]
>Substance which ionizes completely to produce a high concentration of hydrogen ions in aqueous solution.
> #ChemistryDefinitions 


Strong acids:
- Hydrochloric acid
- Nitric acid
- Sulfuric acid

HCl(aq) -> H+(aq) + Cl-(aq)

### Weak Acids

>[!definition]
>Substance which ionizes partially to produce a low concentration of hydrogen ions in aqueous solution.
> #ChemistryDefinitions 


Citric acid - found in citrus fruits - weak acid.
vinegar(ethanoic acid) -> weak acid.
Only some CH3COOH molecules will ionize in an aqueous solution.
CH3COOH CH3COO-(aq) + H+(aq)

Weak acids:
- Ethanoic acid
- Carbonic acid
- Phosphoric acid

Strength of acids and concentration of acids

Strength of acid - depends on its degree of ionization 

Concentration - related to the number of acid molecules present in a given volume of water.

Strong acid -> can be concentrated (large number of acid molecules in given volume) or dilute (small number of acid molecules in a given volume)

Weak acid can also be concentrated or dilute
