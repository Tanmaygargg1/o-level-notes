All acids produce hydrogen ions, H+ in aqueous solution.
=> when acids are dissolved in water -> acid molecules ionize or dissociate, to produce H+ ions.

H + ions are mainly responsible for the behavior of acids.

>[!definition]
>ionization is the process that acids undergo to produce hydrogen ions when dissolved in water.
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

>[!definition]
>an acid is a substance that produces hydrogen ions, H+ in aqueous solutions by ionization/dissociation in water.
> #ChemistryDefinitions 


**Importance of hydrogen ions to acids:**
- Hydrogen ions give acids their physical and chemical properties

**State the physical state of dilute acids and explain the importance of being in this physical state.** 
- Aqueous state. Acids will only produce hydrogen ions in aqueous solutions and these give acids their physical and chemical properties. 

<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Ionization of water: H+(aq) + OH-(aq)</mark>

### Properties of Acids

1. acids have sour taste
2. Acids produce ions when dissolved in water. These ions allow the resulting aqueous solution to conduct electricity. [[Properties of Ionic Compounds]]
3. Acids turn blue litmus paper red
4. Acids react with reactive metals

Good conductor of electricity:
-> molecules of acid - ionize in aqueous solutions to produce hydrogen ions and corresponding anions which are freely moving. 
-> these ions act as charge carriers and hence allow aqueous solution of acids to conduct electricity.
Refer to [[Ionic Bonding]] and [[Properties of Ionic Compounds]]

**Uses of acids**

*Uses of sulfuric acid:*

- Starting material for the production of fertilizers such as ammonium sulfate. 
- Concentrated sulfuric acid is used in the manufacture of detergents
- Dilute sulfuric acid is used in the car batteries to generate electricity.
