>[!definition]
>alkalis are substances which produce hydroxide ions in aqueous solution (ionizes in water)
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

Sodium oxide + water -> sodium hydroxide
Na2O(s) + H2O(l) -> 2NaOH(aq)

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Hydroxide ions are responsible for the chemical properties of alkalis </mark>

State the physical state of alkalis:
- Aqueous state. Alkalis will only produce hydroxide ions in aqueous solution and these ions give alkalis their properties. 

|                     |         |                                   |     |
| ------------------- | ------- | --------------------------------- | --- |
| Alkali              | Formula | Ions produced in aqueous solution |     |
| Sodium hydroxide    | NaOH    | Na+                               | OH- |
| Potassium hydroxide | KOH     | K+                                | OH- |
| Calcium hydroxide   | Ca(OH)2 | Ca2+                              | OH- |
| ammonia             | NH3     | NH4+                              | OH- |

Aqueous solution of ammonia -> some of the ammonia molecules react with water molecules to form ammonium ions and hydroxide ions. One ammonia molecule reacts with one water molecule to produce 1 ammonium ion and 1 hydroxide ion.

⇒ aqueous ammonia is not known as ammonium hydroxide even though it contains ammonium ion and hydroxide ion.

All group 1 metal hydroxides are alkalis

## Properties of Alkalis

1. Alkalis taste bitter    
2. Alkalis feel slippery and soapy
3. Alkalis dissolve in water to form solutions that conduct electricity
4. Alkalis turn red litmus paper blue
5. Alkalis react with acids.

#### Alkali + Acid -> salt + water (neutralization)
- Sodium hydroxide + dilute hydrochloric acid -> sodium chloride + water
- NaOH(aq) + HCl(aq) -> NaCl(aq) + H2O(l)


- <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Aqueous ammonia + dilute hydrochloric acid -> ammonium chloride (works for other acids too)</mark>
Chemical equation: NH3 + HCl -> NH4Cl
Ionic equation: H+(aq) + OH-(aq) -> H20(l)

In neutralization reaction - hydrogen ions from acid, H+, and the hydroxide ions from the alkali, OH-, react to form water.

|                                                                                                                              |     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| H+(aq) + OH- (aq) -> H2O(l)<br><br>This is the ionic equation for any neutralization reaction between an acid and an alkali. | **  |
#### Alkalis react with Ammonium Salts
<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Alkalis when heated with ammonium salts -> give off ammonia gas</mark>

Alkali + ammonium salt -> salt + water + ammonia gas
Naming of salt -> same as the ammonium salt 
KOH(aq) + NH4Cl(s) -> KCl(aq) + H2O(l) + NH3(g)


Ammonia gas - recognized by its characteristic pungent smell.
We can test for ammonia gas -> piece of damp red litmus paper. 
Ammonia gas turns damp red litmus paper blue.
refer to [[Test for Gasses]]
___
**When alkali is heated with ammonium salt:  
No effervescence is seen as ammonia gas is soluble in water. Ammonia gas produced in the reaction dissolves in the aqueous solution of the reaction mixture.**
