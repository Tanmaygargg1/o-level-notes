>[!definition]
>strength of alkali is the extent of ionization of alkali to produce hydroxide ions in aqueous solutions
> #ChemistryDefinitions 


Bases - can be strong or weak, depending on their degree of ionization. 
=> strong soluble bases (alkalis) such as sodium hydroxide (NaOH) -> fully ionized when dissolved in water.

Ammonia(NH3) -> weak alkali since it is only partially ionized in water.
NH3(g) + H2O(l) NH4+(aq) + OH-(aq)
___
>[!definition]
>strong alkali is a substance which ionizes completely in water to produce a high concentration of hydroxide ions in aqueous solution.
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

>[!definition]
>weak alkali is a substance that ionizes partially in water to produce a low concentration of hydroxide ions in aqueous solution.
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

Strong alkalis: (complete ionization)
Potassium hydroxide(KOH), rubidium hydroxide(RbOH), calcium hydroxide(Ca(OH)2), barium hydroxide(Ba(OH)2)

Weak alkalis: (partial ionization)
- Aqueous ammonia (ammonia solution)[(NH3)(aq)] 
- Methylamine (CH3NH2)
