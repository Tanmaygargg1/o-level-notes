>[!definition]
>a compound of oxygen and a metal. This compound reacts with acids to form salt 
>and water.
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Oxides of most metals are basic oxides.</mark>
___
**Physical properties**
Since basic oxides are metal oxides, basic oxides are bases. 
⇒ basic oxides and bases have similar physical properties. They are ionic compounds with giant ionic lattice structures. 

- High melting and boiling points, solids at R.T.P
- Only some basic compounds are soluble in water. Few oxides - such as sodium oxide and potassium oxide - dissolve readily in water to form alkalis. Most others are insoluble. Refer to [[Alkalis]] to find out the ones which are soluble in water. 
- Water soluble basic oxides (metal oxides) -> dissolve in water -> these oxides will react with water to form aqueous solution of corresponding metal hydroxides. 
- Good conductor of electricity when molten (properties of bases and alkalis)

**Chemical properties**
Since basic oxides are metal oxides, basic oxides are bases. Basic oxides and bases have similar chemical properties. 
Basic oxide + dilute acid-> salt + water (neutralization)
Basic oxides - solid at room temperature. They react with acids to form salt and water
