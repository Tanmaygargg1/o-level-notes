>[!definition]
>Compound of oxygen and a nonmetal. This compound does not show properties of acids and bases.
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

___  
**Physical properties**
Since neutral oxides are oxides of non metals - these compounds are covalent substances with simple covalent structure. 

- Low melting and boiling points. Gaseous at R.T.P
- Insoluble in water (except for water)
- Poor conductors of electricity when solid, liquid, gas. 
___
**Chemical properties**
Water reacts with basic oxides to form aqueous solutions of metal hydroxides (alkalis)
Water reacts with acidic oxides to form aqueous solutions of acids.

| --------------- | ------- |

| Carbon monoxide | CO      |
