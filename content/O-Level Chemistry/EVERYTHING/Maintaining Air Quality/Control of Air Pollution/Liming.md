effects of acid rain on forests and water bodies can also be lowered through application of calcium carbonate
carbonate is added to soil and water bodies to remove some of the excess acid contributed by acid rain through a process called liming

limitations of process:
- acid rain can affect vast areas -> there are limits to regions that can be protected -> such as territorial boundaries
- process also expensive - effects are only temporary
