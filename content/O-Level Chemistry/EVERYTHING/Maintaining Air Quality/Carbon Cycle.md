Element carbon essential to life.
- all organic compounds made of carbon skeletons that originate from carbon dioxide and water during photosynthesis.
- most of the organic carbon on Earth -> contained in cells of living organisms -> like fossil fuels and plastics we make from them

**Ocean Uptake**
- oceans and other large bodies of water absorb Carbon dioxide
- some marine plants use dissolved gas to photosynthesise. 
- it is also converted to carbonic acid and calcium carbonate -> that makes up shells of marine organisms + accumulate on seabed when they die

**Photosynthesis**
- plants take in carbon dioxide and water to make glucose and oxygen in the presence of sunlight
- $6CO_2 (g) + 6H_2O(l) \rightarrow C_6H_12O_6(g) + 6O_2(g)$ 
- glucose used in other processes that sustain plant life

**Combustion**
- most fossil fuels contain mainly methane. 
- they are burnt in presence of oxygen to generate energy. carbon dioxide is product of combustion
- $CH_4(g) + 2O_2(g) \rightarrow CO_2(g) + 2H_2O (g)$ 

other sources of carbon present in environment comes from inorganic origins.
- rocks, sediments, dissolved in water, carbon dioxide in air


>[!definition]
>Carbon cycle describes a set of processes that regulates the amount of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere
> #ChemistryDefinitions 

carbon cycle describes how carbon containing compounds are converted from 1 form to another. 
- involves land, oceans, lakes, and atmosphere
