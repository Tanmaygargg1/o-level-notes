ozone -> ozone layer -> absorb ultraviolet radiation -> reducing amt of UV radiation that reaches earths surface

ozone layer found in upper layer of atmosphere called stratosphere 

___
ozone molecules form when oxygen molecules interact with UV radiation from sun. 
ozone has formula $O_3$ and is allotrope of oxygen $O_2$. 
has pale blue colour and pungent smell -> electronics / photocopier smell

same ozone molecules - break up to reform oxygen when they absorb UV radiation.
cycle happens naturally in stratosphere and maintains ozone layer.
can be described using following equation

$\ce{3O_2(g) <=> 2O_3(g)}$

### Depletion of Ozone Layer
CFCs - found in aerosol propellants, refrigerants, certain kinds of plastics
- contain elements chlorine, fluorine, carbon

when they are released and rise up into stratosphere - they can interact with UV radiation to produce chlorine atoms which react with ozone
