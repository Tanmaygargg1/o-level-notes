### Volume of Gas Produced
- in some reactions -> gas is produced. 
- This method only works in reactions which *produce gaseous product*
- can measure ROR -> by observing production of gas in a given amount of time. 

Key Exam Phrasing: 
- <span style="background:rgba(240, 200, 0, 0.2)">Measure the volume of gas formed at regular time intervals for a fixed duration of time and plot a graph using the results.</span> [[Chemistry Practical Notes]]
- take readings until value becomes constant
- the graph must have a changing gradient*

### Change In Mass
-> as reactants get used up in reaction, mass decreases.
-> mass of products increases. 

- <span style="background:rgba(240, 200, 0, 0.2)">Measure change in mass at regular time intervals in a fixed duration of time and plot a graph using the results</span>
- take readings until value becomes constant
- graph must have changing gradient
- Method only works for reactions which *produce gaseous products*
- can determine amt of gaseous products -> from the loss of mass of reaction mixture -> due to the gas escaping from the reaction vessel.
![[Pasted image 20250403213414.png|400]]
![[Pasted image 20250403213443.png|400]]
1. Gradient of graph is steepest at start of reaction. Hence rate of reaction is fastest at beginning
2. As reaction progresses -> gradient becomes gentler -> rate of reaction decreases with time
3. Gradient approaches 0 -> mass of reaction mixture -> stopped decreasing. 

>[!note]
>always read axis of graph carefully -> to interpret rate of reaction -> than to memorise shape of graph

![[Pasted image 20250403213719.png|400]]
- shape of graph different -> because y axis -> shows *loss in mass* of reaction mixture + flask -> and not mass of reaction mixture and flask.

#### Calculating Rate of Reaction
>[!formulae]
>**Calculating Rate of Reaction**
>$Rate\ of \ Reaction\ = \frac{amount\ of\ product\ formed}{duration\ of\ time}$

**Must collect + measure quantity of the product formed using suitable apparatus**
