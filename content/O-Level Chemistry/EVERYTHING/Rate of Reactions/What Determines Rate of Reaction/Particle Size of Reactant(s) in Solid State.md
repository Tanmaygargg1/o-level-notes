- not referring to size of particles such as atoms, ions, molecules. particle size refers to the physical dimensions of a reactant in solid state.


- By breaking large particle into smaller pieces ->the surface area increases even though total mass and total volume remains the same.
- increased surface area -> allows for *more effective collisions* to occur.

![[Pasted image 20250403223257.png|400]]
More total exposed surface area in contact with other reactants.
___
>[!sample]
>Rate of Reaction increases with decrease in particle size
>- there is larger total exposed surface area of the reacting particles to collide into.
>- thus resulting in more collisions between reacting particles per unit time
>- thus frequency of effective collisions increases
>- hence rate of reaction increases

>[!sample]
>Rate of Reaction decreases with increase in particle size
>- there is smaller total exposed surface area of the reacting particles to collide into 
>- thus resulting in less collisions between the reacting particles per unit time
>- thus frequency of effective collisions decreases
>- hence rate of reaction decreases

-> please do not write surface area to volume ratio.
___
**Real Example**
Explain why marble chips instead of powdered marble slows down ROR between hydrochloric acid and calcium carbonate in the marble
- marble chips have larger particle size compared to powdered marble thus marble chips have smaller total exposed surface area for the reacting particles (of HCl or H+) ions to collide into
- this results in fewer collisions between reacting particles (of calcium carbonate and hydrochloric acid or reacting particles of calcium carbonate and H+ ions) per unit time
- thus frequency of effective collision decreases and the rate of reaction decreases
