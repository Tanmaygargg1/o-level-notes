When chemical reaction takes place -> reactants -> converted to products. 

Can measure rate of reaction in a given amt of time by observing:
- how much product appears
- how much reactant disappears

Can physically measure appearance / disappearance of substances by measuring: 
- Volume of gas produced in reaction
- Change in mass of reactant / product

R.S between reactants and products with time.

- mass of products increases with time

- mass of reactants is measured, gradient of graph is negative
- mass of reactants decreases with time.
