Industrial manufacture of ammonia by Haber process -> is catalysed by finely divided iron -> allowing good yields at lower pressure.
![[Pasted image 20250404115524.png|600]]

- in petroleum refining -> large hydrocarbon molecules in crude oil -> need to be broken down into smaller molecules.
- this is so they can be used as fuels + also as raw materials for the production of plastics
- the breaking of chemical bonds proceeds very slowly even with high heat and pressure. 
- by using catalysts -> less extreme physical conditions are required. this saves energy and also makes the refinery safer to operate. 

- similarly -> production of plastics benefit from use of catalysts. 
- this is bc formation of long chain of polymers -> would proceed very slowly without use of catalysts

>[!example]
>- Ethane ($C_2H_4$) and hydrogen react to give ethane ($C_2H_6$). 
>- this reaction occurs extremely slowly -> even at high temp. and pressure -> but when nickel is added as a catalyst -> the reaction occurs readily at 200 degrees
>- the speed of the above reaction increases in the presence of nickel -> as nickel provides an alternative pathway with lower activation energy for the reaction to proceed.
