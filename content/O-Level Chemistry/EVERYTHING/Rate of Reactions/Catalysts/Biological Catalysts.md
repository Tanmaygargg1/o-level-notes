>[!definition]
>Biological catalysts are known as enzymes
- all living things use to increase Rate of chemical reactions. 

Catalase -> converts hydrogen peroxide to hydrogen and oxygen
Many enzymes also use transition metals to facilitate catalysis. 

- aid in energy production and detoxification of cells
___
##### Characteristics

1. made of proteins

2. Temperature sensitive
	- operate best at body temp (35-40 degrees). 
	- below this temp -> can be dormant of denatured
	- reduced catalytic activity
	- at high temp -> denatured and unable to catalyse reactions

3. pH sensitive
	- mostly inactive above or below their optimum range. 
	- optimum pH range of enzymes varies greatly

4. Specific catalytic action
	- each catalyses a specific reaction
	- active site of each enzyme is specific.
