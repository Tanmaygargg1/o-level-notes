**Order of Reactivity of Metals -> can be deduced from a given set of experimental results. The result obtained by conducting experiments involving 3 types of reactions: **

###### I) Metals react with *cold* water
>[!equation]
>Metal + water -> metal hydroxide + hydrogen gas
(the Rule from [[Bases]] -> Grp 1 and Some Grp 2)
###### Ii) Metals react with steam
>[!equation]
>Metal + steam -> metal oxide + hydrogen gas

###### Iii) Metals react with dilute hydrochloric acid
>[!equation]
>Metal + dilute acid -> salt + hydrogen gas

{Order of reactivity -> of these metals -> can be deduced by comparing the observations experimental results) made when the common metals are mixed with steam, cold water, and dilute hydrochloric acid}

>- **Above** in the series means higher up (more reactive).
>- **Below** in the series means lower down (less reactive).
