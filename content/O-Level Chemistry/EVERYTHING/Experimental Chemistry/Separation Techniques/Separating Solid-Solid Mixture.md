**Magnetic Attraction**
Magnet can be used to separate magnetic solids from non magnetic solids
___
**Sieving**
Sieve can be used to separate solids with different particle size
___
**Using Suitable Solvents**
Ability of solute to dissolve in solvent -> known as solubility
solute is said to be very soluble is more of it can be dissolved in a solvent at a particular temperature

Solids usually have diff. Solubility's in different solvents. By using suitable solvent, we can separate solids of different solubility's.

Suitable solvents can be used to separate solid-solid mixtures, in which only one of the solids is soluble in the solvent.
___
**Sublimation**
Sublimation can be used to separate a substance that changes from solid to gaseous state directly.
*iodine and naphthalene used in mothballs under sublimation, as well as dry ice ofcourse*

Sublimation - can be used to separate a mixture of solids where one sublimes on heating while the others are stable at the sublimation temperature. 
Solid sublimes into gas and moves away from the mixture.
By providing a cool surface for gas to deposit on, gas will change state back into a solid.
Resulting solid deposit - known as sublimate
Pure sublimates can be easily collected by scraping them off the cool surface.
