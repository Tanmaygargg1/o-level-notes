- look for signs of emotion, attitude, bias
- look at diction
	- positive, negative, sarcastic, formal, emotional, neutral tone
- **Sentence Structure** and **Punctuation**
	- short, abrupt sentences - urgency / anger
	- long flowing sentences = calm / reflecting
- Imagery or descriptions
- Figurative language
	- similes, metaphors, irony [[Literary Devices]]
