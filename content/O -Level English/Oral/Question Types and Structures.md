## Meaningful Questions
1. Point (meaningful because)
2. Impact
	* cognitive
	* social
	* economic
	* familial (relationships)
	* etc
3. did it help others / did it shape you

-> gave me a new perspective on...
-> left deep impression on be because...
-> wasn't just about the task, but the connection i felt.
