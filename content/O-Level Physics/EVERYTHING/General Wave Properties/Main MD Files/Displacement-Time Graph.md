Imagine that bob is on the surface of the sea when water waves move towards him from the left to right
as the crest of the wave approaches bob => is he moving up or down?
![[Pasted image 20240827224347.png|600]]

As the wave moves towards the right => Bob will be moving up and down but not sideways.

after passing of each peak and as the bottom of the wave approaches bob => is he moving up or down
![[Pasted image 20240827224438.png]]

As the wave propagates => we track Bob's vertical displacement at a fixed horizontal position.
this allows us to describe how the wave's displacement varies with time.

from the moment bob first met the water wave until passing of 2 complete waves => his displacement time graph is shown.
![[Pasted image 20240827224550.png|500]]

Displacement time graph describes the displacement of Bob relative to his rest position over a time interval.
=> from his displacement time graph => we can able to *deduce the period, and hence the frequency.*
