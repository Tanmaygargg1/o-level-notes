>Explain why motor can be used as generator
>When a motor is rotated, there is a change in magnetic flux linked to
the coil. This induces an emf and a current in the coil. Therefore, the
motor can become a generator

why soft iron core? 
- To ensure maximum magnetic flux linkage of primary coil with secondary coil by concentrating magnetic field lines.

>[!sample]
>How does maglev train work 
>superconducting electromagnets produce magnetic field which changes as the train moves. 
>this changing magnetic flux linkages caused an induced current. 
>by Lenz's law, the direction of induced current is always opposed the change that produces it, so the train and track repel.
