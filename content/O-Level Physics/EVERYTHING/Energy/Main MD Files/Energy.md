### Principle of Conservation of Energy
___
>[!definition]
>Principle of conservation of energy states that energy cannot be created or destroyed. Energy can be transferred from 1 store to another. Total energy of an isolated system is constant. 
#PhysicsDefinitions

___
### Work Done and Power

**Work Done**
>[!definition]
>Work done by a constant force on an object is the product of the force and the distance moved by the object in the direction of the force applied. 
(Energy transferred mechanically by a force acting over a distance)
#PhysicsDefinitions 


*Calculating work done*
$$W = Fs$$
#physicsFormulae 
W = work done by constant force (j)
F = constant force (N) 
s = distance moved in the direction of the force applied (m)
Note that the constant force here means net force. 
-> if you have a frictional force acting force taken to calculate work done would be:
$$F_{net} = F_{forward} = F_{friction}$$
SI unit: Joule

**Power**
>[!definition]
>Power is defined as the work done or energy transferred per unit time
SI unit: Watt(W)
#PhysicsDefinitions 


*Calculating Power*
$$P = \frac{W}{t} = \frac{E}{t}$$
#physicsFormulae 
P = power (W)
W = Work done(j)
E = energy transferred (j)
t = time taken (s)

>[!formulae]
>$P = Fv$
>power in Watt
>v in m/s

### Efficiency (Pure Only)
Applying Principle of Conservation of Energy
$$Total\ energy\ input = useful\ energy\ output + non\ useful\ energy$$
in ideal machine -> total energy output = total energy input
*Calculating Efficiency*
	$$Efficiency = \frac{useful\ energy\ output}{Total\ energy\ input} \times 100\% $$

**Answering Technique for what happens to energy in kinetic store when rock hits the water upon impact:**
* Upon impact, some of the energy in the kinetic store of the rock is transferred to the internal store of water. water particles vibrate to and fro and energy is dissipated as sound waves to the surroundings. Energy in the kinetic store of the rock is also transferred to the internal store of the surroundings and the water. 

**Answering technique for why speed of the train is much lesser than value calculated:**
* work done by train against friction along the track, thus the gain in kinetic energy of the train does not equal work done by the train/ Energy transferred from kinetic store of train to internal energy of the train. 

**Answering Technique for why power of train must be increased to maintain the speed of 24m/s:**
* Trains engine needs to do extra work. this is to increase the gravitational potential store of the train as it travels up the slope while maintaining the same amount of energy in its kinetic store. Since more work is done in the same time interval, trains engine power must be increased.

**Answering Technique for why wind blowing in horizontal direction does not affect why water from fountain rises to same height**
* The wind in the horizontal direction has no effect on the gravitational force acting on the water (1). Thus, the same amount of work is needed to propel the water upwards to a height of 140 m. Since there is no change in the gravitational potential store of the water, the water still rises to the same height (1).

**Answering Technique for energy transferred to water from roller coaster:**
* some energy of the kinetic store of the car is transferred to the water. water particles vibrate to and for and energy is dissipated by sound waves to the surroundings. Energy in kinetic store of the car is also transferred to internal store of surroundings and water.
