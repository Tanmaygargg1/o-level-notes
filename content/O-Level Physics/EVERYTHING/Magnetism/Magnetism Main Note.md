## Properties of Magnets
[[Properties of Magnets]]
___
When 2 bar magnets are joined together -> the overall strength of the combined magnet:
- stronger than that of 1 bar magnet
- is weaker than the sum of the strength of both magnets

-> this is because when 2 magnets are stuck together -> the overall magnetic field is the vector sum of the magnetic field of each magnet
-> however edges of magnets that are in contact with each other are now one magnet away from the edge of the other magnet.
-> thus field strength is weaker than sum of field strength of both magnets.
