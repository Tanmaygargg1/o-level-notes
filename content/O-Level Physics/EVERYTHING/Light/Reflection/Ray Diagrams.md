#### Step 1
![[Pasted image 20241118160052.png|275]]
Locate the position of the image I behind the mirror.
Distance of mirror image behind mirror = distance of object in front of mirror
- draw a dashed line at $90\degree$ to the mirror, extending beyond the expected position of the image.
- Measure $d$ and use it to locate the position of image I. 

#### Step 2
![[Pasted image 20241118160415.png|275]]
- Draw lines to join I to the eye.
	- use dashed lines behind the mirror and solid lines for rays reflected into the eye
- Insert arrowheads to indicate the direction at which light travels

#### Step 3
![[Pasted image 20241118160513.png|275]]

- Draw solid lines from object O to the points of incidence on the mirror
	- These are the incident rays
- Insert arrowheads to indicate the direction at which the light travels
___
>[!Note]
> These steps ensure that the angle of incidence = angle of reflection
> (for an extended object, repeat the same steps for key object points)
