When Beam of light is reflected by smooth surface -> reflected rays remain parallel to each other + maintain their spacing.
-> thus reflection is described as regular (orderly) reflection

In contrast -> Rough surface gives rise to irregular (disorderly) reflection
-> irregular reflection is known as diffuse reflection -> because after reflection, rays spread out in different directions. 

![[Pasted image 20241116234431.png|600]]
___
In Both regular and diffuse reflection -> laws of reflection apply for each individual ray.
-> in case of regular reflection -> parallel incident rays -> reflected in same direction because surface is smooth.
-> all rays have same angle of incidence and reflection.

In case of diffuse reflection -> parallel incident rays are reflected in different directions -> because surface is uneven or rough.
-> normals at different points on surface are not parallel to one another.
-> thus angles of incidence and reflection of one ray are different from that of other rays.
