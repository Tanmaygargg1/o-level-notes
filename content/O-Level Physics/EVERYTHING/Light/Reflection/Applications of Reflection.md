- Cars side mirror helps driver to check approaching cars from behind before switching lanes. Is also useful when reversing a car
- Dentist uses small mirror to examine condition of inner surfaces of patients teeth
- Teleprompter -> 2 way mirror -> both for transmitting and reflecting light. 
	- allows light to reach recording devices (green arrow) .
	- also reflects words from news script at the same time (black arrow)
	- presenter is able to read news on the 2 way mirror and maintain eye contact with the video camera
