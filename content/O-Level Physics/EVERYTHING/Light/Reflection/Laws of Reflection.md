When light ray or light beam encounters smooth surface -> it is reflected as the above image.

- Light ray going towards surface is called the **incident ray**
- Light ray going away from the surface is called the **reflected ray**
- The Perpendicular line ($90 \degree$) to the surface at the point of incidence is called the **normal**
- Angle between the incident ray and normal ray is called the **angle of incidence ($\theta_i$)**. Angle between the reflected ray and the normal is called the **angle of reflection ($\theta_r$)
___
>[!law]
>First law of reflection states that the incident ray, reflected ray and the normal at the point of incidence lie in the same plane

>[!law]
>Second Law of Reflection states that the angle of incidence $\theta_{i}$, is equal to the angle of reflection $\theta_r$
