- In the first figure -> ray passes from A to material and to B. 
- In second figure -> light source is moved to B and a ray is sent in the opposite direction.
-> it is found that ray will reach A via the exact same path in the first figure. 
-> there is no change in angles x and y. 
<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">This is called the principle of reversibility of light.</mark>

>[!law]
>**Principle of Reversibility of Light rays** -> states that regardless of how many times a light ray has been reflected or refracted -> it will follow the same path when its direction is reversed

-> due to the principle of reversibility of rays -> refractive index of the material is always given by $n = \frac{sin\ x}{sin\ y}$ regardless of whether goes from A to B or B to A
