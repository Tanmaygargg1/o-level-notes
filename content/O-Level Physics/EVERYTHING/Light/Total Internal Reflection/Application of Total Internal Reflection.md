#### Glass Prisms in Binoculars
Glass Prisms -> used to reflect light in binoculars
Good quality binoculars -> use prisms instead of mirrors 
- Prisms are more durable + have better image quality

After 2 total internal reflections due to prisms -> resulting image is same orientation as object

#### Optical Fibres
- Made of glass or plastic
- Carry information in the form of coded light pulses. 
- Rely on total internal reflection of light to transmit signals
![[Screenshot 2025-01-21 at 10.13.00 AM.png|300]]
- Optical fibres are thin and flexible 
- Used in *telecommunication* and *medicine*
#### Telecommunications
For transmission of large amounts of data -> optical fibres -> can be bundled to form thicker cables. 
Larger + longer undersea cables -> span thousands of kilometres -> used as communication links across continents

Advantages of using optical fibres over copper wires in telecommunications:
- Data transfer rate -> tens to thousands of times faster -> compared to using copper wires, radio waves, microwaves
- There is less signal loss -> compared to copper cables
- Optical fibres are lighter + cheaper -> compared to copper cables of similar length
#### Medical use
Endoscope -> used to inspect insides of hollow organs -> such as digestive track. 
-> it has number of channels avaialble for surgical instruments -> such as laser and camera. 
Being flexible -> optical fibres are ideal to be used in endoscope to transmit light -> for illumination and to capture images inside organs

Optical fibres also play important role in laparoscopy. 

Laparoscope -> small tube -> with light source + camera. 
- Images captures are transmitted to monitor. 
- Small size and flexibility of optical fibres -> make it ideal -> for use in laparoscope -> to transmit light.
Laparoscopy -> greatly reduces pain, bleeding, recovery time of patients. 

Advantages of optical fibres in medicine:
- Thin and light -> endoscopes and laparoscopes -> kept small -> for minimally invasive examination and surgery
- Flexible -> endoscopes and laparoscopes -> can curve around obstacles -> when taking images inside body
#### Rain Sensor
Small device -> attached to inner side of glass windshield
When windshield is dry -> infrared light -> emitted diode (LED) -> is able to reach the photodiode detector. 
When there are raindrops on windshield -> less infrared light -> reflected to photodiode detector. 
Thus -> sensor connected to photodiode detector -> will turn on windshield wiper.
