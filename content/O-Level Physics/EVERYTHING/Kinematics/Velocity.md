>[!definition]
>velocity is the rate of change of displacement
#PhysicsDefinitions 

___
VECTOR quantity => can be negative
Velocity can be negative when the object is moving back to the original point from the positive side.
-> basically it is speed in a specified direction.
___
*Formulas*
$$Velocity = \frac{displacement}{time\ taken}$$
$$Average\ velocity = \frac{total displacement}{total\ time\ taken}$$
