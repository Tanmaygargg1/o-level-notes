nuclear decay is spontaneous -> we will not know exactly when particular nucleus will decay
- however every nuclide has fixed rate of decay

can make predictions abt decay of large numbers of a nuclei of a particular nuclide bc it has fixed half life value
>[!definition]
>- half life of a radioactive nuclide is the time taken for half the nuclei of that nuclide in any sample to decay
>-  #PhysicsDefinitions 

e.g. half life of iodine-131 is 8 days.
___
decay curve is graph of number of atoms or count rate against time.
