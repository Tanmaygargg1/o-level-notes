___
SI unit of amt of radioactivity: *Becquerel (Bq)*

1 becquerel = 1 disintegration per second
- refers to amt of ionising radiation released when a radioactive atom spontaneously emits electromagnetic radiation as a result of the radioactive decay
___
Measurement of radiation -> sometimes expressed as being a *rate of counts per unit time* -> known as *count rate*
- can be measured using GM counter

units: counts/min  or counts/s
____
Steps to measure background radiation. (require GM counter and stopwatch)
1. remove all known radioactive sources
2. start counter and stopwatch
3. stop counter after 10 mean and record number of counts
4. number of counts per minute = $\frac{number\ of\ counts\ in\ 10\ min}{10}$
5. repeat measurement at least 2 times and calculate mean
____
can also record number of counts for longer time interval - e.g. 20min

longer time - counts/min
shorter time - counts/s
___
when doing experiments with radioactive sources -> always measure background radiation. 
subtract this background count rate from measurements to obtain corrected count rate for radioactive source
