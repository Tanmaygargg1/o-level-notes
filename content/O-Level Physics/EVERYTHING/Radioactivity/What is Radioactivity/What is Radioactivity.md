### Nuclear Decay
>[!definition]
>Nuclear Decay is a random process by which an unstable atomic nucleus loses its energy by emission of electromagnetic radiation or particle(s)
> #PhysicsDefinitions 

Nuclear decay -> known as *radioactive decay / radioactivity*

- material containing unstable nuclei -> radioactive
- instability of atomic nuclei -> because the nuclear forces within nuclei are not enough to bind the nucleons together

- radiation emitted by radioactive nucleus is spontaneous and random in direction. 
- direction of emissions and the time between emissions cannot be predicted. 

insert image here

in other words, it is not possible:
- to make radioactive nucleus emit radiation by heating, cooling, chemical means or any other method
- predict when radioactive nucleus will emit radiation
- know the direction in which emitted radiation will leave nucleus
