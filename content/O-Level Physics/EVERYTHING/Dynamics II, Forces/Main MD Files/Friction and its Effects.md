>[!definition]
>Friction is the contact force that opposes or tends to oppose motion between surfaces in contact.
#PhysicsDefinitions 

=> result of irregularities of the surfaces.
![[Pasted image 20240908000640.png|560]]
Eg; if force is applied to move a book across table towards the right => friction will act towards the left.
if applied force is removed => friction will cause the book to slow down and come to a stop eventually.
___

| Positive effects of friction                     | Negative effects of friction                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| We can walk without slipping                     | Cars are less efficient by up to 20%                             |
| Moving objects are able to slow down when needed | Moving parts in engines, motors and machine suffer wear and tear |
___
#### Reducing Negative Effects of Friction

|                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Using Wheels**<br>Being circular in shape, wheels greatly reduce the friction between objects and the floor.<br>Smaller force can be applied to move the object around.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | **Using Ball Bearings**<br>Being spherical in shape - ball bearings are used to reduce friction between moving parts of machines, cars, and in line skates.<br><br>Ball bearings - placed between moving parts so that ball bearings can roll around.<br>Prevents the moving parts from rubbing against each other. |
| **Using Lubricants and polished surfaces**<br>Applying a layer of lubricant, such as oil or grease - between surfaces in contact can greatly reduce friction<br><br>  <br>Lubricants - frequently used between moving parts of an engine to reduce wear and tear.<br>Helps to prolong the life of the engine.<br>Polishing a surface removed surface irregularities.<br>Can also reduce friction between surfaces in contact | **Using air cushions**<br>Friction between two surfaces - reduced - when there is a thin layer of cushion between the 2 surfaces.                                                                                                                                                                                   |

#### Enhancing Positive Effects of Friction

| Using Threads                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Using Parachute                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Using Chalk                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Friction - important to motion of vehicles.<br>Without friction -> vehicle cannot move as its tires will just spin at the same spot<br>  <br>Friction - enables tires to grip the roads surface and roll without slipping<br><br>Rainy day - moving vehicles may skid on wet roads.<br>Tires need to have more grip on the road to prevent skidding.<br>Thus, tires are designed with threads - grooves that quickly channel water out from underneath the tires.<br>This improves grip of tires on wet roads, thus preventing skidding. | Air resistance (AR) -> type of friction in the air.<br><br>A skydiver in midair varies air resistance to change his speed.<br>- To speed up -> he reduces AR by reducing SA in contact with air (head first position)<br>- To slow down -> he increases AR by increasing SA in contact with air (spread-eagle position)<br>- To achieve a safe landing - he increases AR significantly -> making use of much larger SA of an open parachute.<br> | Rock climbers - need to have firm grip on rock surface with their hands and feet.<br>- Use chalk powder on their hands to absorb perspiration and improve their grip<br> |
