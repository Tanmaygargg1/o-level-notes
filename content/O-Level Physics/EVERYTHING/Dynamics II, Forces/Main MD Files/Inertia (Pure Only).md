___
>[!definition]
>Inertia of an object refers to the reluctance of the object to change its state of rest or motion, due to its mass.
#PhysicsDefinitions 

___
Mass => is the property that resists the change of motion (inertia). 
<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">* Objects with greater mass will have greater intertia</mark>

Larger the mass of the object, harder it will be for an object to:
- start moving
- slow down
- move faster
- change direction

<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Always state: due to body's inertia => it wants to continue moving forward at a constant velocity as before in a straight line</span>
