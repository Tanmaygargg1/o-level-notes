#### Moment
Turning effect of force => moment
Lever => rigid bar resting on pivot or fulcrum
-> used to move a load when a force is applied

When force is applied => load experiences a turning force. 
Rigid bar => is hinged at the pivot => can make a sweeping movement only.

Turning effect of a force is proportional to:
- Perpendicular distance form the pivot where the force is applied
- magnitude of force applied

Turning effect of a force => also known as *torque*
___
>[!definition]
>Moment of a force, M or torque, about a pivot is the product of the force F and the perpendicular distance d from the pivot to the line of action of the force
#PhysicsDefinitions 

$$M = Fd$$
where F = force applied (N)
d = perpendicular distance from the pivot to the line of action of the force (m)

SI unit N m
=> it is a *vector* quantity. Moment of force has direction
a clockwise rotation about pivot => is a clockwise moment. Anticlockwise rotation about pivot => is anticlockwise moment

Important => in order to create moment => line of action of force must not pass through pivot
