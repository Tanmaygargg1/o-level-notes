>[!definition]
>Principle of moments states that when a body is in equilibrium, sum of clockwise moments about a pivot is equal to sum of anticlockwise moments about the SAME pivot
#PhysicsDefinitions 

___
**Conditions for Equilibrium**
=> for body to be stationary or in equilibrium => must not have any rotational or translational motion
-> no resultant forces or resultant moments acting on the body

Conditions for equilibrium:
- resultant moment on body is 0
- resultant force on body is also 0
