- Energy + Nutrients -> transferred from producers to consumers to decomposers through feeding

| Producers                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Consumers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Decomposers                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Producer -> organism that *makes its own food*<br>- contains chlorophyll -> absorbs light to convert $CO_2$ and water into glucose -> photosynthesis<br>- Glucose -> chemical store of energy<br>- Oxygen is released in the process. | - not able to make their own food<br>- obtain energy + nutrients -> by feeding on other organisms. <br>- classified as -> primary, secondary, tertiary -> according to position in food chain. <br><br>**Primary Consumers:** feed on plants only<br>**Secondary Consumers:** feed on primary consumers<br>**Tertiary Consumers:** organisms that feed on secondary consumers | - organisms that get their energy by breaking down dead organisms, faeces, excretory products. <br><br>-> activities return mineral salts + nutrients -> back to env. <br> |

| Population                                                        | Community                                                                                  | Ecosystem                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| group of organisms -> of same species -> live together in habitat | - made up of all pop. of diff. species living + interacting with one another in a habitat. | Community of organisms -> interacting with one another + its non living env.  |
