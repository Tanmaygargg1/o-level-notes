Natural resources -> resources supplied by nature
- Air, water, soil, forest, wildlife -> renewable natural resources
	- Can be replaced in the ecosystem by natural cycles -> as long as humans do not overuse them

-> on other hand -> natural resources like fossil fuels -> take millions of years to form naturally
- Since formation takes millions of years -> cannot be replaced quickly enough
	- Called non renewable natural resources

Human beings -> continually using and depleting earths natural resources. 
- Upsets balance of nature + causes lasting damage to env. 
- Env. Damage caused -> largely irreversible. 
	- Thus human activities -> may be carefully planned + monitored -> preserve + conserve env for future

- These activities -> interrupt food chains and food webs in ecosystem
- Through altering food chains and food webs -> humans can have negative impact on habitats
