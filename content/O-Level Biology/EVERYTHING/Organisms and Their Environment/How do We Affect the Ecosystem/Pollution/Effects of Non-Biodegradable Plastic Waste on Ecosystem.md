- Burning of plastic waste -> and its effect on global warming -> already discussed [[Effect of Human Activities on Global Warming]]
- Plastic waste disposed in landfills:
	- Harmful chemicals from plastic waste -> buried in landfills -> may be leached -> into rivers and seas. They poison wildlife in sea or rivers
- Plastic waste -> created from disposables -> e.g. shopping bags -> may be harmful to marine animals when they are washed into sea
- Plastic waste can be broken down -> into microplastics -> can be ingested by marine organisms + accumulate in their bodies -> leads to biomagnification [[Insecticides|biomagnification]] of microplastics along food chain

Many animals -> die -> from feeding on plastic waste in landfills or in sea.
- Plastics are indigestable 
- Marine life like turtles and dolphins die - when plastics block up their stomachs and intestines
