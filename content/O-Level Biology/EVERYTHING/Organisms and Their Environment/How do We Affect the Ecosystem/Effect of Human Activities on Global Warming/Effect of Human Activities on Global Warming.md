##### Global Warming VS Climate Change
Global warming -> is the rise in global temperature
- Process is accelerated -> by human activities -> that cause -> increasing concentrations of greenhouse gasses -> such as $CO_2$ in the atmosphere.

Climate change -> refers to changes in temperature and weather patterns *over long period of time*
- Effects of climate change -> include rising sea levels, melting ice sheets in Greenland and Antarctica, shifts in flowering or plant blooming times and *unpredictable* weather changes. 
-> some countries -> more frequent and intense hurricanes and winter storms
