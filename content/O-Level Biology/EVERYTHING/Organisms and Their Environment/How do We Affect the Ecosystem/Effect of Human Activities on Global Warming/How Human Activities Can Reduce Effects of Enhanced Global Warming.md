- Reduce use of fossil fuels -> use renewable sources of energy -> like solar energy and wind
- Reduce energy consumption. Save electricity -> by using more efficient devices -> e.g. use of LED bulbs
- Replace petrol powered vehicles -> with hybrid / electric vehicles.
- Reduce daily consumption of electricity, water, paper and plastic. 

Forest Management:
- Conservation of forest -> disruption of natural cycles such a carbon cycle -> can be prevented, and this helps to prevent global warming
- Indiscriminate felling of trees should be prevented
- Reforestation: new seedlings - can be planted -> replace trees that have been removed or destroyed
- Control of forest fires: some forest fires are man made. People set fires - clear forests and provide land for agriculture. This must be prevented
- Education programs - conducted -> make people aware of the important of forests to our lives + methods by which forests can be conserved.

*Singapore green plan 2030 -> nationwide movement -> advance Singapore's national agenda -> on sustainable development:*
Aims to ensure -> SG - will remain a green and liveable home -> for current and future generations.
- Reduce carbon emissions; keep our env. Clean
- Save resources and energy
- Use cleaner energy and lower our carbon footprint
