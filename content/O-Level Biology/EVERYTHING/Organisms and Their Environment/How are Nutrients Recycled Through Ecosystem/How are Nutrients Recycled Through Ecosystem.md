### Nutrient Cycling in an Ecosystem
Carbon + Oxygen + Nitrogen + Water -> essential substances in nutrients -> needed for life.

Natural Ecosystems -> these substances -> released back into soil -> when organisms die.
- Decomposers (fungi and bacteria) -> break down dead organisms. 
- This way -> materials locked up in dead organisms -> returned to physical environment -> to be used again by green plants

Balanced ecosystem -> nutrients are *never lost* -> but are *continuously recycled*
- Cycling of these nutrients -> brought up by chemical, physical, biological processes
___
### Carbon Cycle
Carbon - constantly being removed from + released back into env. -> in form of $CO_2$
-> concentration of $CO_2$ in env. Remains relatively constant

> [!definition]
> Carbon Cycle: Various processes by which carbon, in form of carbon dioxide, is removed from and released into env. 
> #BiologyDefinitions 

![[Pasted image 20250123122100.png|300]]
1. Photosynthesis
	- Plants absorb carbon dioxide + use it to synthesise carbohydrates. 
	- Some of carbohydrates -> converted to proteins and fats -> carbon sinks

2. Feeding
	- Animals obtain *carbon compounds* -> by feeding on plants or other animals. (represented by arrow from tree to sheep)

3. Respiration
	- Plants and animals respire -> releasing $CO_2$ into atmosphere

4. Decoposition
	- Decomposers -> such as bacteria and fungi -> break down dead organic matter + release $CO_2$ 

5. Formation of fossil fuels and combustion
	- Dead bodies of plants and animals -> may be buried deep into earth
	- They are subjected to high pressure and temperature -> where they are converted to fossil fuels -> such as coal, natural gas, oil. 
	- Combustion of these fossil fuels -> releases $CO_2$ into env. 

> [!summary]
> 1 and 2 -> removal of carbon dioxide from env. 
> 3,4,5 -> return of carbon dioxide to env. 

___
##### Importance of Carbon Cycle
- Ensures that there is continuous supply of carbon dioxide for plants -> to carry out photosynthesis. 
	- Plants trap light during photosynthesis -> produce glucose, which is a store of chemical energy
- Enables energy to flow through an ecosystem. Carbon compounds -> carry stored energy form organism to organism in the food chains of an ecosystem
- Regulates amount of carbon dioxide in atmosphere
