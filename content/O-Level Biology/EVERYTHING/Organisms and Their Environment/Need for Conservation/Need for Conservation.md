##### Conservation of Mangrove Swamps
- some countries -> have mangrove restoration project
- volunteers -> plant mangrove seedlings -> over a portion of mangrove swamp.
- trees that develop -> will protect low lying areas against strong wind, waves, and tides

Adopt a mangrove -> program -> campaign initiated at Pulau Ubin -> raise awareness -> about conservation and restoration of degraded areas -> to reduce impact of global warming and climate change.

##### Conservation of Coral Reefs
People visiting coral reefs -> should practice -> safe and responsibly diving
Divers:
 -> should not tough reefs -> may damage coral animals
 -> should not anchor boats on reef as they may kill corals

**some guidelines for visitors to reefs**
- avoid using sunscreen -> some ingredients in sunscreen -> can be harmful to corals
- avoid purchasing living corals -> as this will encourage people -> to extract live corals for sale
- Avoid polluting coastline with trash -> especially non biodegradable plastic
	- such marine debris -> might be blown or washed away into the ocean.
	- can be harmful to coral reefs.
