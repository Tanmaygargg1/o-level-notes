#### Food Chains
> [!definition]
> Food chain is a series of organisms -> beginning with the producer, through which energy and nutrients are transferred
> 
> #BiologyDefinitions 
- Represent feeding RS -> between organisms in ecosystem using food chains

> [!note]
> Each arrow represents **Flow of energy**

Position an organism occupies in food chain -> is known as *trophic level*
- Generally food chains do not have more than 4 trophic levels
___
#### Food Webs
> [!definition]
> Food web consists of network of interconnected food chains
> #BiologyDefinitions 

Note* organism can be at more than one trophic level in food web
