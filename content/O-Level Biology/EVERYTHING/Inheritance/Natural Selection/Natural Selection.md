variations in organisms may arise due to mutation
competition for food and space occurs among these diff varieties of organisms.
those variations that are more competitive, more resistance to diseases and better adapted to changes in env can survive and reproduce better. 
other varieties that are susceptible to diseases or env changes may die. called *natural selection*
>[!definition]
>Natural selection refers to a process in which organisms with desirable genes and characteristics are 'selected' or 'favoured' to survive and pass on their genes to their offspring
> #BiologyDefinitions 

Mutation provides new genes or alleles (variations) for natural selection to operate on. 
After 1000s / millions of years -> more beneficial qualities (genes or alleles) may accumulate in a species. 
the new breed of organisms may be better adapted to their new env. 
they may even change so much that they become new species which differs from original species.
