>[!definition]
>Gene that exists in more than 2 different forms is said to have multiple alleles. 
> #BiologyDefinitions 

Individual can only have 2 alleles -> 1 allele from each parent. 
With multiple alleles -> can be diff. combinations of 2 alleles. 
- makes it possible to have more than 2 phenotypes expressed based on the dominant or recessive alleles available in the trait. 

Gene for human blood group -> is example of gene with multiple alleles. 

4 blood groups in human populations (A, B, AB, O) [[Human Transport System Main Note]]
Alleles for blood group A B O are: $I^A , I^B , I^O$. 
$I^A$ and $I^B$ are dominant over $I^O$ -> but $I^A$ and $I^B$ exhibit codominance. 

Therefore individuals with alleles $I^A$ and $I^B$ will have blood group AB.
