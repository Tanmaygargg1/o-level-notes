Human body cell -> there is 1 pair of sex chromosomes and 22 pairs of non sex chromosomes called *autosomes*
- means that each human body cell has one pair of each type of chromosome. 
- hence human body cells are diploid with 23 pairs of chromosomes. [[Meiosis]] [[Sexual Reproduction]]

In males -> pair of sex chromosomes consists of 1 X chromosome and 1 shorter Y chromosome.
Females -> have pair of X chromosomes in each normal body cell. 

During gamete formation -> each gamete produces can only have 1 chromosome from each pair of chromosomes. 
- in male -> sperm produced contains either Y or an X chromosome. 
- in female -> each egg will contain one X chromosome
- Y chromosome shorter than X. 
___
