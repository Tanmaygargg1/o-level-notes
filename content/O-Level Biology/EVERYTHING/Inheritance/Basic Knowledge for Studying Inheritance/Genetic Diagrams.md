- explain how alleles are passed on to an offspring + what traits will be expressed by offspring (genetic cross)

(Using plant experiment, Since $F_1$ offspring (heterozygous) were tall, it means the dominant phenotype is tall)

Let T be dominant allele for tall plants
Let t be recessive allele for dwarf plants


| Genetic Diagram                           | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| ![[Pasted image 20250519091637.png\|320]] | Top 2 big square boxes:<br>- organism that is pure bred for a trait is *homozygous* for that trait. <br>- Mendel's tall parent plant is *homozygous dominant (TT)* and dwarf plant is *homozygous recessive (tt)<br>*<br><br>Small t circle box: <br>- reduction cell division (meiosis) separates a pair of alleles by splitting up each pair of homologous chromosomes so that each gamete only receives 1 copy of each allele (either T or t)<br><br>Last square small box: <br>- $F_1$ generation offspring all have the (Tt) genotype |
| ![[Pasted image 20250519091716.png\|320]] | Top 2 big square boxes + second small circle boxes: <br>- $F_1$ generation offspring also produce gametes by meiosis. Each gamete contains either T or t allele. Random fertilisation gives rise to the $F_2$ generation offspring. <br><br>Last square small box: <br>- $F_1$ generation are self fertilised. The ratio of dominant to recessive phenotypes in $F_2$ generation is close to expected ratio 3:1. This is especially so when large number of plants is used in breeding experiment.                                         |
