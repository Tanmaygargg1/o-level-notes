#### Why do We Look Like Our Parents
- height, eye colour, skin colour -> referred to as *hereditary traits* -> can be *inherited* from one generation to another.

>[!definition]
>Inheritance refers to the transmission of traits from one generation to another
> #BiologyDefinitions 

example of inheritable traits:
- hair type (straight, wavy curly)
- earlobe (attached / detached)
- skin colour (fair / dark)
- eyelid (single / double)
- chin (with / without cleft)
