>[!definition]
>Mitosis is a type of cell division giving rise to genetically identical cells in which the chromosome number is maintained. 
> #BiologyDefinitions 

![[WhatsApp Image 2025-03-28 at 21.15.24_46b13337.jpg|350]]
Daughter cells: 
- identical to parent cell
- identical to each other
- same chromosomes
- same genes
___
### Importance of Mitosis
Other than asexual reproduction -> mitosis also occurs in other processes; 

Mitosis is important in: 
1. Asexual reproduction. 
	- Mitosis allows for asexual reproduction to occur

2. Growth in multicellular organisms. 
	- For multicellular organisms to grow -> new cells must be produced by mitosis

3. Repair of damaged tissues
	- New cells -> produced by mitosis to replace cells that are dead/damaged
___
>[!important]
>mitosis maintains the diploid number of chromosomes
