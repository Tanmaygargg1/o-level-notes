#### Plasma
->pale yellowish liquid in blood - 90% water
-> contains:
* mainly water
* glucose
* dissolved mineral salts <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">(sodium chloride, sodium bicarbonate, potassium chloride, existing as ions)</span>
* soluble proteins <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">(serum albumin, serum globulin, fibrinogen, prothrombin)</span>
* Calcium
* antibodies
* amino acids
* fats
* vitamins
* hormones
* excretory products like urea, uric acid, creatinine
-> also contains red and white blood cells. 
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">-> PH of 7.3</span>

**Function***:
<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">Transports:</span>
* <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">blood cells around the body</span>
* <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">nutrients from small intestines to other parts of the body</span>
* <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">excretory products from organs, where they are produced to excretory organs for removal</span>
* <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">hormones from endocrine glands to target organs. </span>
