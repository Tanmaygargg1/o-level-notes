tiny spaces between tissue cells - contain a colorless liquid - tissue fluid
-> transports dissolved substances between the tissue cells and the blood capillaries.

dissolved food substances and O2 - diffuse from the blood in the blood capillaries to the tissue fluid.
then they diffuse from tissue fluid to the cells.

Metabolic(or excretory) products -> diffuse from the cells -> into the tissue fluid.
they are then transferred through the blood capillary walls into the blood.
the blood then transports these waste products to excretory organs for removal.
