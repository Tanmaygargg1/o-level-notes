**Cell Wall**
* Rigid structure
* made out of cellulose
* encloses the entire PLANT cell => surrounding cell membrane
* protects cell from injury and gives the PLANT cell its fixed shape
* fully permeable => allows all substances to pass through => does not control type of substances that enter or exit the cell.
* absent in animal cells
