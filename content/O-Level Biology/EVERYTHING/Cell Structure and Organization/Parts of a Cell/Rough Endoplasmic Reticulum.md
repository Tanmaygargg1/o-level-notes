trans**Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum**
* consists of stacks of flattened spaces lined with a membrane

=> surface appears rough => when viewed under electron microscope => due to presence of small round structures called ribosomes attached on its outer surface.
=> outer surface of RER => is consistent with nuclear membrane.

Function:
=> transports proteins made by the Golgi body for secretion out of the cell.
