**Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum**
=> does not have ribosomes => attached to its outer surface
=> it is more tubular than the RER
-> connected to RER

Function:
* synthesises substances like fats and steroids (sex hormones in mammals are steroids')
* converts harmful substances into harmless substances => through process called detoxification
