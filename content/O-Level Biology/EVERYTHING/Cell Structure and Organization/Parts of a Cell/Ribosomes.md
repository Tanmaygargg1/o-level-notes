**Ribosomes**
=> small round structures
=> attached to the RER or lie freely in the cytoplasm

Function:
* needed to synthesise proteins in the cell
* those attached to RER => make proteins that are usually transported out of the cell
* those that lie freely in the cytoplasm => make proteins that are usually used within the cytoplasm of the cell.
