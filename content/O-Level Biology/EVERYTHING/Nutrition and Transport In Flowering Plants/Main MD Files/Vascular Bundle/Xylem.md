>[!definition]
>Xylem transports water and dissolved mineral salts from roots to the aerial parts of the plant. It also provides mechanical support to the plant
#BiologyDefinitions 

___
* structure made up of many dead cells without cytoplasm or cross walls. 
	* Each vessel is a long hollow tube stretching from root to the leaf
<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">=> This reduces resistance to water flowing through inner walls of xylem vessels</span>

* strengthened by deposits of a substance called lignin
	* Lignin may be deposited in the form of rings, spirals or the entire vessel may be lignified except for regions called the pits
<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">=> lignin on cell walls -> strengthen vessel walls so that xylem vessels may provide mechanical support to the plant and prevent collapse of the vessel.</span>
