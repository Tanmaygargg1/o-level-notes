1. In Daylight when photosynthesis occurs => Carbon dioxide in the leaf is rapidly used up
2. Carbon dioxide concentration in the leaf -> becomes lower than the atmospheric air => diffusion gradient exists
3. Thus, $CO_2$ diffuses from the surrounding air through the stomata into the intercellular air spaces
4. Surfaces of mesophyll cells are always covered with a thin film of moisture. this is so that $CO_2$ can dissolve into it
5. $CO_2$ dissolves into thin film of moisture on the mesophyll cells
6. Dissolves $CO_2$ then diffuses from cell to cell until it reaches inner cells
