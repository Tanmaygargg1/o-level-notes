1. Xylem transports water and dissolved mineral salts to the leaf from the roots
2. Once out of the veins, water and mineral salts => move from cell to cell right through the mesophyll of the leaf

- Veins in the leaf form branches -> which end among the mesophyll cells
- Veins contain xylem and phloem => which together make up a vascular bundle
