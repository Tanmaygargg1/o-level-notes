#### How to Prepare leaf for Presence of Starch* 
1. Remove green leaf from a plant that has been exposed to sunlight for a few hours
2. Immediately put the leaf in boiling water for 2 min to denature all enzymes in the leaf and stop chemical reactions in the leaf
3. Put boiled leaf in a boiling tube containing alcohol/ethanol => to decolorize the leaf. Place boiling tube => in the beaker of hot water. Leaf has to be decolorized so that color of iodine can be clearly observed. Alcohol with remove green pigment chlorophyll from leaf. Leaves will become colorless/white
4. Leaf is now very brittle. Gently remove leaf and put it back into hot water. This softens the leaf and makes it more permeable to iodine solution
5. Remove leaf and spread it out on a white tile. Add few drops of iodine solution to the leaf

#### How Can we Show that Photosynthesis has Taken Place
=> by testing for presence of starch in the leaves
Equation for photosynthesis  => shows that Glucose is the carbohydrate that is produced. 
However in experiments > we often test for starch to show photosynthesis has taken place
- this is because glucose is always present in all leaves
- however, excess glucose produced during P is converted to starch and stored in the leaves. 
- thus if starch is absent at the beginning of the experiment but present at the end, we can tell that photosynthesis has taken place

#### Essential Conditions for Photosynthesis
Light, $CO_2$ and chlorophyll
Suitable temperature and water is also needed
Photosynthesis => depends on enzyme reaction in chloroplasts => thus suitable (optimum) temperature is required for photosynthesis to take place

## What Happens during Photosynthesis
>[!definition]
>Photosynthesis is a process by which plants synthesize carbohydrates (eg: glucose) from water and carbon dioxide. Water and carbon dioxide are raw materials for photosynthesis. Oxygen is released during the process
#BiologyDefinitions 

During photosynthesis => chlorophyll absorbs light energy and transfers it to chemical stores of energy as carbohydrates (glucose) which are synthesized from water and carbon dioxide. 
Oxygen is released as a by product

Rate of Photosynthesis is affected by:
- light intensity
- concentration of $CO_2$ 
- Temperature
