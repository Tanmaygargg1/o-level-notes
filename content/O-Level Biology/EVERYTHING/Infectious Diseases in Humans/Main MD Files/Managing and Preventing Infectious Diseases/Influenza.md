Influenza, or flu -> disease that attacks respiratory system.
- most people recover from this disease within 2 weeks. 
- Sometimes influenza -> can lead to complications such as pneumonia (lung infection).
- Influenza -> caused by influenza virus
____
**Cause:**
- Caused by influenza virus

**How is it Transmitted:**
- Transmitted through -> respiratory droplets in air 
- When person touches an object or surface contaminated with virus and then touches his or her own mouth, nose, eyes

**Signs and Symptoms:**
- Produces signs and symptoms of high fever, headache, stuffy nose, cough, sore throat, muscle aches
___
#### Methods to Reduce Transmission of Influenza Virus
1. Get influenza vaccination. Annual Vaccination is recommended. 
2. Avoid coming into close contact -> with people who have the flu. If you are sick, keep your distance from others to prevent them from getting infected too. 
3. If you are sick -> cover mouth and nose with tissue when you cough/sneeze + dispose of the used tissue properly. 
	- Wear surgical mask if you are not feeling well. 
4. Wash hands with soap and water / rub with disinfectant -> if you think you have touched objects that are likely to be contaminated with virus.
5. avoid touching eyes, nose, mouth. Viruses -> often spread when person touches something that is contaminated with viruses that cause flu and then touches his/her eyes, nose, mouth. 
6. Take antiviral drugs -> prescribed by doctor -> treat the illness.
