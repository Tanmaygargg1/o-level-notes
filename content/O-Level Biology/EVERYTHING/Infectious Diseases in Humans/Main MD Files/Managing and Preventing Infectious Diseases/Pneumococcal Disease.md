- Caused by pneumococcal bacteria. 
	- May attack diff. parts of the body
		- E.g. -> may cause middle ear infection, lung infection (pneumonia), inflammation of membranes of brain and spinal cord, infection of the blood. 
___
**Cause:**
- pneumococcal disease -> caused by pneumococcus bacteria

**How is it Transmitted:**
* Mainly transmitted -> through respiratory droplets

**Signs and Symptoms:**
- Fever, headache, vomiting, cough, chest pain, rapid breathing
____
#### Methods to Reduce Transmission of Pneumococcus Bacteria
1. Get Pneumococcal vaccination
2. Take daily preventive actions (points 2-5 in [[Influenza]])
3. Take antibiotics -> prescribed by doctor to treat disease
