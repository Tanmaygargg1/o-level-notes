-> if the kidney is dissected longitudinally -> an outer dark red region and an inner pale region are visible

*2 Main sections of the kidney*
* cortex (outer region)
* medulla (inner region)

inside the kidney => there are numerous nephrons
Nephrons => are the basic functional units of the kidney
	=> there are tiny kidney tubules where urine is formed
	Kidney is connected to the ureter => which drains the urine into the urinary bladder for removal

| Functions of the Kidney                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Excretory Organ => removing mainly nitrogenous waste products (urea), excess water and mineral salts in the form of urine |
| Osmoregulators to maintain pH and composition of blood plasma by selectively reabsorbing useful substances along nephron  |
| Regulate salt and water balance of the body fluid depending on the various conditions the body is exposed to              |
