4 main parts of nephron are => Bowman's capsule, Proximal (first) convoluted tubule, loop of Henle, and distal (second) convoluted tubule.
Several Nephrons => open into a tube called collecting duct
* small independent processing unit
* located in renal cortex except for loops of Henle that descend into renal medulla
* has its own blood supply (arteriole, venule, network of capillaries)

| Structure                             | Elaboration                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Bowman's Capsule                   | Each nephron begins in the outer region of the kidney as a cup-like structure called the Bowmans capsule                                                                                                 |
| 2. Proximal (first) convoluted tubule | Proximal (first) convoluted tubule => which straightens out => as it passes into the inner region of the kidney<br><br>Convoluted part of the tubule => is called the proximal (first) convoluted tubule |
| 3. Loop of Henle                      | Inner region of the kidney => the tubule extends further in and makes a U-turn back into the outer region<br>=> U-shaped portion => of the tubule is known as the loop of Henle                          |
| 4. Distal (second) convoluted tubule  | When tubule => enters the inner region of the kidney again => it becomes convoluted again<br>=> this part of the tubule => is called the distal (second) convoluted tubule                               |
| 5. Collecting Duct                    | tubule => then opens into a collecting duct that runs straight through the inner region<br>=> eventually opens into a funnel like space that leads into the ureter                                       |

###### Blood Capillaries around the Nephron
Blood enters the kidney => through the renal artery => leaves kidney by renal vein
=> nephrons in the kidney are surrounded by blood vessels that are connected to the renal artery and renal vein
![[Pasted image 20240829185418.png|600]]
1. Small branch of renal artery => called the different arterioles carries blood inside the glomerulus
2. glomerulus is a knot of blood capillaries in the bowman's capsule
3. efferent arteriole => transports blood away from the glomerulus
4. Blood continues => into the blood capillaries => surrounding the nephron. these blood capillaries eventually lead into a branch of the renal vein
