Carbon Dioxide**<br>-> product of aerobic respiration<br>-> carbon dioxide level in our blood is usually not harmful<br>-> in certain cases where there is abnormally high level of $CO_2$ in our blood -> a condition called hypercarbia occurs.<br>-> person may suffer from headache, confusion, rapid breathing and premature heartbeats.<br>=> excretory organ is the lungs. and it is excreted as gas in expired air.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **2. Urea**<br>digestive system breaks down proteins into amino acids. Absorbed amino acids => transported to liver, distributed around the body and then assimilated.<br>Liver -> is involved in assimilation of amino acids. <br>-> uses amino acids to synthesise proteins found in blood plasma<br>these plasma proteins => include fibrinogen, which is essential for clotting of blood. [[Human Transport System Main Note#How does Blood Clot]]<br><br>if we consume more proteins than we need -> we will have excess amino acids in the blood<br>-> excess amino acids -> are transported to the liver where they are deaminated through a process called deamination. <br>During deamination -> part of the amino acid molecule is removed and converted to urea.<br>-> urea is then removed by kidneys<br><br>*Is urea in blood harmful?* <br>Urea -> is a waste product formed in the breakdown of proteins<br>Nomral concentration of urea in blood is nor harmful<br>=> abnormally high urea concentration in blood -> may cause abdominal pain, nausea, or vomiting<br>	-> rare cases -> can result in irregular heartbeat and muscle cramps<br> |
| **3. Mineral salts or ions**<br>Bodies -> need mineral salts<br>Deficiency of certain ions -> may result in deficiency diseases.<br>Example: lack of calcium results in weakening of bones or osteoporosis<br><br>Excess salts or ions in blood -> can be harmful. <br>=> may lower WP in blood plasma<br>Water may pass out of tissue cells into blood by osmosis.<br>Cells may become dehydrated (crenated) =  [[Movement of Substances Main Note]]<br>=> Kidneys help to remove excess salts from the body                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **4. Water**<br>Water -> essential to life<br>=> produced during aerobic respiration<br>hence it is also a metabolic product<br><br>However -> excess water in body is harmful<br>-> will increase WP in blood plasma<br>By Osmosis-> Water will enter tissue cells -> cells may swell and burst<br>Hence excess water also has to be removed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
