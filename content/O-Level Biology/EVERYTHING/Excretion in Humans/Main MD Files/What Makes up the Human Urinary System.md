| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Kidneys<br>[[Structure of the Kidney]] | => beaned shaped organs => embedded in a mass of fat in the abdominal cavity<br>Average size => of the human kidney is 10-13cm long and about 5-7cm wide<br><br>- attached to the dorsal body wall with one on each side of the vertebral column<br>- concave surface faces the vertebral column.<br>- depression at the centre of the concave surface (hilus)<br>- Renal artery, renal vein, and nerves are connected to the kidneys through the hilus<br>- made up of nephrons<br><br>=> lie just above the waistline<br>left kidney is slightly higher than the right kidney<br>main function of Kidneys: Excrete urea and excess salt and water as urine |
