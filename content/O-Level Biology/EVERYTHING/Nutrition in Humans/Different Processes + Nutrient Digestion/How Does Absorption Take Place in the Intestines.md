1. Glucose and amino acids -> absorbed by diffusion into blood capillaries of the villi
2. glucose and amino acids -> absorbed by active transport into blood capillaries of the villi. This happens when there is a lower concentration of these digested food substances in the lumen of the small intestine than in the blood capillaries. 
3. Glycerol and fatty acids -> diffuse into the epithelium. Here they combine to form minute fat globules that enter the lacteals.
