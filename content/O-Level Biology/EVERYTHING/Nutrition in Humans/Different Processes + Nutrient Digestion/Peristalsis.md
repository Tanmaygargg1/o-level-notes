>[!definition]
>peristalsis is the rhythmic, wave like muscular contractions in the walls of the alimentary canal.
#BiologyDefinitions 

Enables food to be mixed with digestive juices, and also pushes or propels food along the gut.
___
When circular muscles contract, they constrict the lumen
when longitudinal muscles contract, they shorten and widen the lumen
circular muscles and longitudinal muscles are antagonistic muscles.
-> means that one contracts, the other set relaxes.

* when circular muscles contract, longitudinal muscle relaxes. wall of the gut constricts, and the gut becomes narrower and longer. food is squeezed and pushed forward.
* when longitudinal muscles contract, the circular muscles relax. wall of the gut dilates, becomes wider and shorter. they widen the lumen for food to enter.
