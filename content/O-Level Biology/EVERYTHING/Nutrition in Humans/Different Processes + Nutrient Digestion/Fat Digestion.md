Bile - produced by liver and stored in the gall bladder - released into the duodenum - when gall bladder contracts. 
in small intestine - bile emulsify fats.
They lower surface tension of the fats -> they reduce attractive forces between fat molecules.
this causes large fat globules to break up into tiny fat droplets suspended in water - which forms an emulsion.
Note: this is just a physical break up, there is no chemical digestion of fats here.
emulsification - increases SA to V ratio of fats to enzyme action - speeding up their digestion by lipase.

Emulsified fats - are digested by lipases (pancreatic and intestinal) into fatty acids and glycerol
