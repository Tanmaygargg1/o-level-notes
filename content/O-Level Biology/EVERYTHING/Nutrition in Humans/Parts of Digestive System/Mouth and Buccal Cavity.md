[](Processes%20that%20take%20place%20in%20the%20mouth.md)food enters body through mouth which leads to buccal cavity
buccal cavity -> inside mouth and the beginning of the alimentary canal.

Adaptations of Mouth:
* Teeth: break UP large pieces of food into smaller pieces. this increases the SA to V ratio of food to enzymes so that enzymes can act on food more efficiently.
* salivary glands: secrete salivary amylase into mouth
* Tongue: mixes food with saliva and pushes food to the back of the mouth to be swallowed. also rolls into balls.

**Pharynx**:
part of alimentary canal that connects buccal cavity to esophagus and larynx(voice box)
also leads to trachea.(windpipe)
