-> narrow muscular tube
passes through thorax (chest) -> and connects mouth to the stomach

Walls of the oesophagus -> contains 2 layers of muscles.
-> muscles are present along the whole gut from the oesophagus to the rectum.

2 layers of muscles are:
* Longitudinal muscles on the outer side of the gut wall
* circular muscles on the inner side of the gut wall
-> both set of muscles produce long, slow contractions.
these contractions, move food along gut via peristalsis.
