Transgenic bacterium can be genetically engineered to express the gene for human insulin. 
___



| Step                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Visual                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 1 . Obtain the fragment (segment) of DNA in human chromosome that contains the insulin gene. (human insulin gene is isolated from human cell)<br> <br>Cut out the gene using restriction enzyme. This enzyme cuts the restriction site at the 2 ends of the gene to produce sticky ends (at both ends of gene)<br><br>Each sticky end is a single strand sequence of DNA bases. These bases can pair with complementary bases to form a double strand.<br> | ![[Pasted image 20250310114853.png\|150]] |
| 2. Plasmid is removed from Bacterium<br> <br>Plasmid is cut with same restriction enzyme.<br> <br>This produced sticky ends that are complementary to the ends of the insulin gene.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | ![[Pasted image 20250310115400.png\|150]] |
| 3. Mix the plasmid with the DNA fragment containing the human insulin gene.<br> <br>Human insulin gene will bind to the plasmid by complementary base pairing between their sticky ends.<br> <br>Add the enzyme DNA ligase -> to seal the human insulin gene to the plasmid.<br> <br>This plasmid containing DNA from 2 different organisms is called *recombinant plasmid*                                                                                | ![[Pasted image 20250310115929.png\|150]] |
| 4. Mix the recombinant plasmid with *Escherichia coli* bacterium<br><br>Apply heat or electric shock. <br>-> this opens up pores in the cell membrane of the bacterium for plasmid to enter<br><br>**Transgenic Bacterium is produced**                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | ![[Pasted image 20250310120209.png]]      |
| 5. This *transgenic bacterium* will use the new gene to make insulin<br> <br>Such bacteria can be isolated and grown in a fermenter for mass production of human insulin.<br> <br>Insulin protein has to be extracted and purified before it can be used.                                                                                                                                                                                                  | ![[Pasted image 20250310120349.png]]      |
___
>[!note]
>Any organism which acquired a foreign gene is called transgenic organism
>Bacterium carrying human insulin gene -> transgenic bacterium
