people suffering from diabetes - cannot control amt of sugar in their blood. 
Insulin is needed to treat such patients. 
Insulin obtained from pancreas of slaughtered animals. 
- However animal insulin is not same as human insulin. 
-> Many diabetics develop antibodies against animal insulin after prolonged treatment. 
such patients become *allergic* to animal insulin + can no longer use animal insulin to control their diabetes. 
- Diseases may also be transmitted from animals to humans who use animal insulin
___
##### Advantages of Genetically Engineered Insulin
- Insulin produced by genetic engineering is identical as ordinary human insulin. 

| Advantages of Genetically Engineered Insulin                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Does not induce allergic response in patient as insulin produced is identical to human insulin (unlike animal DNA)                                 |
| It is easier and cheaper to produce insulin in large quantities.                                                                                   |
| There is lesser risk of contamination by disease causing microorganisms like bacteria as compared to insulin obtained from the pancreas of animals |
| The ethical concerns of vegetarians or religious groups can be overcome. e.g. vegetarians will object to use of insulin obtained from animals.     |
Genes can be transferred from human cells to bacterial cells. 
-> enables us to transfer gene of interest -> which makes up human insulin -> to bacterial cells 
-> Bacteria acquire foreign gene. 
-> Foreign gene causes bacteria to produce insulin -> substance which is not normally produced by bacteria.
-> Org. that has foreign gene inserted into its *genome* or DNA is called **Transgenic Organism**
