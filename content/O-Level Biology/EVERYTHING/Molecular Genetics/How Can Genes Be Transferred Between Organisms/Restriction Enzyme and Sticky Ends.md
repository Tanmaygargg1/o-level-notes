1.  Gene is segment in the DNA molecule [[Relation Between DNA, Chromosomes, Genes]]. On each side of gene is a segment called *restriction site*
2. An enzyme called *restriction Enzyme* can <span style="background:#fff88f">recognise</span> and cut the restriction site to produce sticky ends. 
3. DNA strands from 2 diff. organisms are cut by the <span style="background:#fdbfff">same</span> restriction enzyme (same restriction enzyme is added to the *vector* and the DNA containing gene of interest) to produce *complementary sticky ends*
4. DNA fragments from 2 different sources are mixed together with an enzyme called *DNA ligase*
5. The 2 different DNA fragments will bind together by *complementary base pairing* [[Relation Between DNA, Chromosomes, Genes#Rule of Base Pairing]]. Often sealed together by DNA ligase to form *recombinant* DNA
