1. small segment of DNA -> carries gene -> that stores information used to make a single protein 
- proteins -> responsible for determining characteristics of org.

2. Each DNA molecule -> consists of 2 strands -> twisted around each other to form a *double helix* (i.e. 2 long thin strands twisted around each other like a spiral staircase)

3. Molecule of DNA is wrapped around proteins to form a single *chromosome* <span style="background:#ff4d4f">this is wrong this is chromatin thread. which then coils and condenses during cell division. refer to main note under rs of gene chromosome and DNA. </span>

4. Made up of 2 <span style="background:#d3f8b6">anti parallel</span> polynucleotide chains twisted into a double helix. (chains run in opp. directions) [[Relation Between DNA, Chromosomes, Genes#^7e422f]]

5. Each Polynucleotide chain is made up of nucleotide -> containing nitrogen containing base, deoxyribose sugar and phosphate group

6. Bases on one chain are bonded to those on the opposite chain according to the rule of base pairing
