>[!definition] 
>Proteins are organic molecules that are made up of the elements oxygen, hydrogen, carbon and nitrogen. Sulfur MAY also be present.
#BiologyDefinitions 

- Always present in all cells
- Molecules are the largest and most complicated of all food substances
- commonly found in meat of animals

**Amino Acids - Building Blocks of Proteins*
- protein molecule => built up from simpler molecules called amino acids
*Amino acid is made up of:*
- amino group($NH_2$)
- side chain (denoted by R, which may sometimes contain sulfur)
- acidic group (COOH)
![[Pasted image 20240817144148.png]]

20 naturally occurring amino acids
-> differ because of different R groups
-> Amino acids combined in various ways to form millions of diff proteins molecules
___
**Long Chains of Amino Acids fold to give proteins a 3D Shape**
-> many amino acids -> joined in linear manner to form polypeptide
-> polypeptides may be linked to form even longer chains of amino acids
-> Protein molecule -> made up of 1 or more such chains folded together
