#### Insect Pollinated Vs Wind Pollinated

| Feature      | Insect Pollinated Flowers                                                                                                              | Wind Pollinated Flowers                                                                                                            |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|              | ![[Pasted image 20250329195145.png\|250]]                                                                                              | ![[Pasted image 20250329195159.png\|250]]                                                                                          |
| Petals       | Flowers are usually large with brightly coloured petals to attract insects                                                             | Flowers are usually small and dull coloured, without petals                                                                        |
| Nectar       | Nectar present                                                                                                                         | Nectar absent                                                                                                                      |
| Scent        | Flowers are fragrant or sweet smelling                                                                                                 | Flowers are odourless                                                                                                              |
| Stigmas      | Stigmas are usually small, compact, and do not protrude out of flower                                                                  | Stigmas are large, feathery, and usually protrude out of flower -> provide large surface area to trap pollen                       |
| Pollen       | Pollen is fairly abundant<br>- pollen grains usually larger with rough surfaces -> so that they can readily cling onto body of insects | - Pollen is more abundant<br>- Pollen grains have smooth surfaces and are tiny and light -> sot they are easily blown away by wind |
| Nectar guide | Nectar guide may be present on petals to guide insects toward nectar                                                                   | Nectar guide absent                                                                                                                |
| Stamens      | Stamens are not pendulous and usually do not protrude out of flower.                                                                   | Stamens have long pendulous filaments and protruding anthers. <br>pollen grains are hence easily shaken out from anthers.          |
___
>[!important]
>for pollen grains: 
>
>- hooks / rough and hairy bodies
