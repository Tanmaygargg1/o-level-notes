Male part of flower
Consists of anther and a filament
___
1. Filament
- stalk -> that holds the anther in suitable position to disperse the pollen

1. Anther
- <font color="#b2a2c7">consists of 2 lobes each containing 2 pollen secs</font>
- <font color="#b2a2c7">pollen gains are found in these sacs</font>
- produces pollen grains. 
- when anther matures -> it splits open to release pollen grains
- anther contains vascular bundle: Phloem ->  translocate sucrose and amino acids to developing anther. Xylem -> transport water and mineral salts. 

3. Pollen grains
- *CONTAIN* male gamete. 
- male games -> produced by meiosis -> are haploid
- have 1/2 number of chromosomes as a normal cell
- if normal cell of plant has 20 chromosomes, then gamete will have 10 chromosomes
