Complete flower consists of:
- Flower Stalk
- Receptacle
- Sepals
- Petals
- Stamens
- Carpels (Pistils)
![[Pasted image 20250329190804.png|400]]

| Part            | Features                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Petal        | Petals = modified leaves -> forming conspicuous (most obvious) part of flower. <br><br>In insect pollinated flowers petals:<br>- brightly coloured -> attract insects for pollination<br>- Provide platform for insects to land on<br>- have lines -> guide insects to base of petals to obtain nectar<br><br>- all petals form *corolla* |
| 2. Sepal        | - modified leaves -> enclose + protect -> other parts of flower in the *bud* stage. <br>- usually form outermost layer of floral leaves<br>- all sepals make up *calyx*                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 3. Flower Stalk | - attaches flower to the stem. <br>- some flowers have no stalks -> and are attached to plan directly at base                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 4. Receptacle   | - swollen end of flower stalk                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
