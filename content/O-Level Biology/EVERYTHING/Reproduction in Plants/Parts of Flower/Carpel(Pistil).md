female part of flower
- consists of ovary, style above the ovary, and stigma

1. Stigma
- Swollen structure at the end of the style
- receives pollen grains
- mature stigma secretes sugary fluid -> stimulates pollen grains to germinate

2. Style
- Stalk that connects stigma to the ovary
- holds the stigma in a suitable position -> to trap pollen grains

3. Ovary
- Contains one or more ovules
- within each mature ovule -> is the female gamete or egg cell -> called ovum
- ovum is produced by meiosis. thus is haploid
