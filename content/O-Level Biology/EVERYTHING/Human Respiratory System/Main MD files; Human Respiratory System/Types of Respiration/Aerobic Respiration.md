>[!definition]
>Aerobic respiration is the release of energy by the breakdown of glucose 
>in the presence of oxygen. Carbon dioxide and water are released as by products. Aerobic respiration releases a large amount of energy

mitochondria in cells ([[cell structure and organization]]) - contain enzymes that aid in respiration (series of steps that are catalyzed by enzymes)

Complex Organisms (trees/humans) -> respire aerobically as they need large amounts of energy to survive. 
Example of activities that require large amounts of energy
* synthesis of new protoplasm for growth and repair
* active transport in the absorption of food substances in the small intestine
* transmission of nerve impulses
* muscle contractions such as heartbeats and respiratory movements
* cell division
* synthesis of proteins from amino acids*
