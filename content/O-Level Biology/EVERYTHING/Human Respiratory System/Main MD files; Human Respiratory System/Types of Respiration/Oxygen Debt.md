>[!definition]
>Oxygen debt is the amount of oxygen required to remove lactic acid
> #BiologyDefinitions 

how can oxygen debt or lactic acid be removed after a race.

<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">These 2 processes happen at the same time:</span>
1. Continuation of fast heart rate
	* results in continued and faster transport of 
		* Lactic acid from muscles to the liver
		* oxygen from the lungs to the liver
		* 
2. continuation of deeper and faster breathing
	* results in continued and faster intake of oxygen by the lungs*
	* -> sufficient oxygen is required to remove lactic acid from blood
	* once lactic acid is removed - oxygen debt is paid. 

during a race - athlete obtains some of his energy from anaerobic respiration
this is because a lot of energy is needed <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">during a short period of time.</mark> (important to say this in exam)
oxygen supply to muscles is insufficient for aerobic respiration to meet the energy demand.
