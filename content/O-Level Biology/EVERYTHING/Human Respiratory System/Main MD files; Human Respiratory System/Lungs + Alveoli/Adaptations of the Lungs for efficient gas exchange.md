* Numerous alveoli in the lungs -> provide a very large surface area
* walls of the alveolus -> is only 1 cell thick -> which provides a short diffusion distance -> ensuring higher rate of diffusion
* thin film of moisture covers the inner wall of the alveolus -> which allows O2 to dissolve in it.
* walls of alveoli -> richly supplied with blood capillaries -> which ensure continuous flow of blood -> which maintain the concentration gradient of gasses

diffusion gradient is maintained by:
- continuous flow of blood through blood capillaries
- continuous breathing of air in and out
