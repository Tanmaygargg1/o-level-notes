Lung Capacity:
* about 500cm3 of air enters the lungs under each normal and quiet breathing cycle
* same amount of air lungs while breathing out
* this volume is known as tidal air
* additional volume of air taken in on top of tidal air - during deep breath - is known as complemental air. (1500cm3)
* supplemental air (1500cm3) is the amount of air forced out after normal expiration
* vital capacity of lung -> 3500-4000 cm3 -> is the amount of air one can take by taking in the deepest breath
* residual air the amount of air left in the lungs no matter how hard one tries to force out (1500cm3)

vital air = tidal air + complemental air + supplemental air.
