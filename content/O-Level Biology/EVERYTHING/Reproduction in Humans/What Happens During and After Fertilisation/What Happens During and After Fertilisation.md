### Mating/Sexual Intercourse
- when male -> sexually stimulated/aroused -> blood is pumped into penis faster than it can be returned into bloodstream via veins. 
- Hence -> blood fills spaces in the *spongy tissue* of the penis. 
- causes penis -> to become stiff + erect -> to allow it to enter the vagina of the female. 
- known as mating, copulation, sexual intercourse

- semen containing sperm -> *ejaculated* -> into vagina, along with *slippery liquid* -> made by prostate gland. 
- sperm -> able to swim up oviducts -> of female in this liquid
- chemicals in this liquid -> also make the sperm tails (*flagellum*) to beat/propel sperm along oviducts. 
- if sperm meets egg in oviduct -> fertilisation may occur. 
- millions of sperm -> released into vagina during 1 ejaculation. 
- only 1 will fertilise egg.
