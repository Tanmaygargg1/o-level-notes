>[!definition]
>Fertilisation is the process by which the nucleus of male gamete fuses with the nucleus of the female gamete to produce a zygote
> #BiologyDefinitions 


| Step                                                                                                                                                                       | Image                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 1. Only 1 sperm nucleus -> enters egg.<br>- sperm nucleus fuses with egg nucleus -> and fertilised egg or zygote is formed. <br>- this process is known as *fertilisation* | ![[Pasted image 20250410095150.png\|200]] |
| 2. As soon as sperm has entered egg -> no other sperm can enter. <br>- remaining sperm, which do not fertilise egg -> eventually die                                       | ![[Pasted image 20250410095217.png\|200]] |
___
##### Further Complex Explanation

1. in oviduct -> acrosome of sperm releases enzyme to break down corona radiate (layer of follicle cells surrounding the eggs)

2. follicle cells are dispersed

3. only 1 sperm enters the egg. membrane of egg changes so that no other sperm can enter

4. haploid sperm nucleus fuses with haploid egg nucleus

5. diploid zygote (fertilised egg) formed
