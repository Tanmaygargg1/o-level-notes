- first sign of puberty in females-> monthly discharge of blood of menses from the uterus via the vagina -> *menstruation*
- menstrual period -> about 5 days. 
	- length of menstrual period and amount of blood lost -> varies from female to female

- Cycle of events that take place in female reproductive organs -> *menstrual cycle*
- average menstrual cycle for an adult female -> 28 days. 
	- menstrual cycles ranging from 21 to 33 days is not abnormal
	- there is natural variation in length of menstrual cycle
		- stress, tiredness, illnesses, unbalanced diets, malnutrition -> may alter or stop menstrual cycle and change interval between period

As females age -> ovaries will eventually stop releasing eggs -> menstrual cycle will stop. 
- known as *menopause*
- takes place between 45-55 years of age

___
#### What Happens if No Fertilisation Occurs
- egg breaks down
- blood progesterone level decreases
- on 28th day -> without progesterone -> uterine lining begins to break down. menstruation begins again
- whole cycle repeats
___
#### What Happens if Fertilisation Occurs
- Fertilised egg is called a zygote. Zygote develops into embryo through process of [[Mitosis]]
- ovary secretes progesterone and oestrogen. 
	- causes maintenance + thickening of uterine lining for implantation of embryo. 
- embryo -> embeds itself in the uterine lining -> until a structure called *placenta* is formed. 
- ovary continues to secrete -> progesterone + oestrogen -> until placenta is able to take over production of 2 hormones.
___
#### Fertile and Infertile Periods of Menstrual Cycle
- egg can survive in oviduct for up to 24 hours . 
	- if ovulation occurs on day 14th in a 28 day menstrual cycle -> can survive from 14 to 15
- Sperm can survive in female reproductive system for about 3-5 days. Means sperm deposited into vagina around day 10 can fertilise egg and bring about pregnancy. 
- Hence fertilisation is possible -> if sexual intercourse occurs during day 10-15 of menstrual cycle. This period is known as *fertile period*. 
- rest of the days -> make up the *infertile periods* of the menstrual cycle

>[!note]
>we are assuming that menstrual cycle is highly regular. we learnt earlier than there are various factors that may affect and alter menstrual cycle
