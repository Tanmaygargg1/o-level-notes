| Stages of Menstrual Cycle                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1. **Menstruation**<br>- uterine lining + unfertilised egg -> break down and are shed with some blood                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 2. **After Menstruation**<br>- ovary produce hormone called *oestrogen*<br>- oestrogen -> causes uterine lining to repair and thicken<br>- high oestrogen concentration -> leads to ovulation                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 3. **Ovulation**<br>- on 14th day -> *an* ovary -> releases an egg into the oviduct -> through process known as ovulation<br>- 2 ovaries take turns to release an egg                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 4. **After Ovulation**<br>- ovaries produce hormone -> *progesterone*<br><br>Functions of progesterone:<br>-> prevents ovulation + further development of eggs<br>-> causes uterine lining to thicken further, preparing it for implantation and growth of embryo. <br><br>- if no fertilisation occurs -> progesterone production decreases. <br>- uterine lining breaks down and whole cycle repeats |

>[!note]
>- since implantation (if fertilisation occurs) happens 5 days after fertilisation, the level of progesterone peaks at around day 22 -> refer to the function of progesterone which is to thicken uterine lining further which prepares uterus for implantation. 
>- must come down at around day 27 -> prepare for menstruation again.

>- when answering question -> assume menstrual cycle of same female remains same every month
