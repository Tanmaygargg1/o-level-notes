- female gamete
- female -> born with all potential eggs she will ever have. 
	- at birth -> about 70000 potential eggs already present in ovaries
	- however only abt 500 will ever become mature within the 2 ovaries. 
- usually only 1 mature egg -> released every month from time female becomes physically mature -> until abt 45-55 years of age. 

- egg is spherical and about $120 -150 \mu\text{m}$ wide

**Egg:**
- abundant cytoplasm which may contain small amount of yolk
- surrounded by cell membrane which in turn -> surrounded by outer membrane (or jelly coat)
- outer membrane -> changes during fertilisation to prevent other sperm from entering the egg
___
- large nucleus containing haploid number of chromosomes. 
- presence of plasma membrane surrounded by outer membrane
