>[!definition]
>Definition: Also called accommodation, is the adjustment of the lens of the eye so that clear images of objects at difference distances are formed on the retina. 
#BiologyDefinitions 
___

___
Focusing -> necessary -> so that objects at different distances can be seen clearly
- in focusing -> the thickness or curvature of the lens is adjusted. 
	- This allows light rays to be focused on the retina.
