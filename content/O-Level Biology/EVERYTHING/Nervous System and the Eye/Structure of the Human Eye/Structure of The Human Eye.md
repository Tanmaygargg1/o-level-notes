- Sense organ containing receptors for detecting light stimulus

in Humans -> each eyeball lies in a hollow in the skull called the *orbit*
Each eyeball is attached to the skull by the *rectus muscles*
the rectus muscles -> control eye movement.

#### What Forms the External Structure of the Eye
Front part of the eyeball is covered by eyelids.
-> only part of the eyeball can be seen.
![[Pasted image 20241113220145.png|400]]

| Part                       | Function                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sclera (or sclerotic coat) | -> Tough, white outer covering of the eyeball. <br>-> It is continuous with the cornea -> which is a dome shaped transparent layer at the front of the eye. It protects the eyeball from mechanical damage<br>-> Optic nerve is attached to the sclera at the very back of the eye                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Conjuctiva                 | Thin transparent membrane covering the sclera in front. <br>It is a mucous membrane. <br>It secretes mucous, thus helping to keep the front of the eyeball moist                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Eyelashes                  | Help to shield the eye from dust particles                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Tear Gland                 | Lies at the corner of the upper eyelid. It Secretes tears that:<br>- wash away dust particles<br>- Keep the cornea moist for atmospheric oxygen to dissolve so that dissolved oxygen can diffuse into the cornea<br>- Lubricate the conjunctiva, helping to reduce friction when the eyelids move                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Eyelids                    | Protect cornea from mechanical damage. <br>They can be partly closed -> known as squinting<br>-> squinting prevents too much light from entering eyelid and damaging the retina<br>Blinking spreads tears over the cornea and conjunctiva + wipes dust particles off the cornea                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Iris                       | Iris is a circular sheet of muscles. <br>-> controls size of the pupils and therefore the amount of light entering the eye.<br>-> round opening in centre of iris is called the pupil<br>-> embedded with tiny muscles that dilate (widen) and constrict (narrow) the pupil size. <br>-> flat + divides front of the eye (anterior chamber) from back of the eye (posterior Chamber)<br>-> colour comes from microscopic pigment cells -> called *melanin*. Colour, texture, patterns of each persons iris is as unique as fingerprint. |
| Pupil                      | Pupil is a hole in the centre of the iris. Allows light to enter the eye<br>- size of pupil determines amount of light entering eye. <br>-> size controlled by dilator and sphincter muscles of iris.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
