>[!definition]
>Main Function of Nervous System
>- Coordinates and regulates body functions and activities and how we react to surroundings (respond to stimuli)

- Regulates bodily functions and activities and how we react to surroundings (respond to stimuli)
- Nervous system consists of well developed brain, spinal cord, spinal nerves and *sense organs.*
	- these sense organs detect changes in environment (detect stimuli)

Humans -> many activities -> heartbeat, peristalsis, breathing -> automatic and not controlled consciously. they are **involuntary actions**

activities that are controlled consciously are called **voluntary actions**

Both involuntary and voluntary actions are controlled by human nervous system
