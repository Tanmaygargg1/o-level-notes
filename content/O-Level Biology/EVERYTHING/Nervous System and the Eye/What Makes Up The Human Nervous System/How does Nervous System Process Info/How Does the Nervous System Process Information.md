#### Sensation
<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Example: When you hold a piece of ice -> you can feel its coldness.</span>
<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">-> this is because thermal receptors in your skin are stimulated. </span>
<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Nerve impulses are produced and transmitted to the brain.</span>
<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Brain interprets the nerve impulses -> and you have a sensation of coldness. </span>
