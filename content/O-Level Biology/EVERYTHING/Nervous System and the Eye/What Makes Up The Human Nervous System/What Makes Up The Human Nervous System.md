>[!definition]
>Main Function of Nervous System
>- Coordinates and regulates body functions and activities and how we react to surroundings (respond to stimuli)
### Sensitivity
Living organism is able to react to changes in its surroundings.
*Stimulus*: Any change in environment -> causing an organism to react. 
*Response*: Organisms reaction to stimulus 

**Examples of Organism Responding to a Stimulus**
- plants need light for Photosynthesis -> so they grow towards light
- Euglena (single celled organism) makes food by Photosynthesis -> swims towards light
- Cockroaches -> move away from light + hide in dark areas during day

Ability to respond to stimulus -> *sensitivity* or *irritability*
