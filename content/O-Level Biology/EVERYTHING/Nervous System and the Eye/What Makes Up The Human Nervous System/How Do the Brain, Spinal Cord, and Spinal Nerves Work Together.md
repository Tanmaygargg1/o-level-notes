(Recall: Nerve impulses are transmitted by neurones to and from the central nervous system)

Brain:
- Contained within the skull which protects it. 
-> From brain -> Spinal Chord runs down the back of the body. The Spinal cord passes through the vertebral column (backbone) which protects it.

Spinal nerves:
- connect spinal chord to various parts of the body.
- like all nerves -> they are composed of bundles of nerve fibres. 
- divided into 2 roots just before joining spinal cord

![[Pasted image 20241113135243.png|600]]
Spinal nerves -> emerge at intervals along the length of the spinal chord.
-> as the spinal nerve leaves the spinal chord -> it progressively subdivides into branches -> supplying nerve fibres to various parts of the body.
