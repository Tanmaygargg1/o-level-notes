**Cell Body**
- Cell Body contains a nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane and organelles
- Cell Body of motor neurone is irregular in shape

**Nerve Fibre**
- Nerve Fibre is a strand of cytoplasm extending from the cell body.
___
**Function**
- transport nerve impulses from the relay neurone in the CNS to the effector
