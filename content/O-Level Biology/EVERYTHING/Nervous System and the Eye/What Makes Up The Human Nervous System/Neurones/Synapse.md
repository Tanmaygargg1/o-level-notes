There is a tiny space at the junction between 2 neurones. 
- junction between the 2 neurones is called a synapse.
Nerve impulses cannot directly cross from 1 neurone to the next due to the tiny space between them.
Nerve impulses are transmitted across a synapse by chemicals released by the neurones.

___
>[!important]
>- Impulses are transmitted from axon of one neurone to dendron of another neurone across a minute space -> called synapse

**Alternative?**
- Electrical impulses -> cannot jump across this gap
- Production of chemical substance is stimulated (WHERE)
- Chemical substance (neurotransmitter) -> flows across synapse and stimulates next nerve-> to start electrical impulse
____
##### Advantages of Having Synapses rather than one long continuous nerve fibre
- synapse -> makes it possible for 1 nerve -> to stimulate impulses in several other nerves at same time
- Presence of neurotransmitters -> can allow us -> to administer  certain drugs -> to prevent transmission of chemical signals -> thus blocking nerve impulses
