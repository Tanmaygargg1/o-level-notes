Sensory Neurone has circular cell body.
It has:
- One long nerve fibre extending towards the receptor 
- Short nerve fibre extending towards the relay neurone in the central nervous system.
![[Screenshot 2024-11-06 at 11.03.56 AM.png|600]]

___
**Function**
- transmit nerve impulses from the receptor or the sense organ to the relay neurone in the CNS
