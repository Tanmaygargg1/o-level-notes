>[!note]
>- Axon -> transmits impulses **away** from cell body
>- Dendron -> *conducts* impulses **towards** cell body
>- Dendrites -> terminal branches of both dendrons and axons

Nervous system is made up of nervous tissue
Nervous tissue consists of nerve cells called neurones.
![[Screenshot 2024-11-06 at 10.47.18 AM.png|500]]
<mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Note the direction of travel of nerve impulses*</mark>

1. Sensory Neurone (receptor Neurone)
- Transmits nerve impulses from the sense organs or receptors to the <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">relay neurone</mark>in the central nervous system

2. Relay Neurone (intermediate Neurone)
- Transmits nerve impulses from the sensory neurone to the motor neurone. 
- Found within central nervous system

3. Motor Neurone (effector Neurone)
- transmits nerve impulses from the <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">relay neurone</mark> in the central nervous system to the effectors 

>[!note]
>- Must Say "Relay Neurone IN THE central nervous system"
>- if you are sure where it is then you can say whether it is in brain or spinal cord, depending on whether it is spinal reflex or cranial reflex

>[!important]
>There are no relay neurones in the PNS, they are only found in CNS. 
>PNS contains only sensory and motor neurones*

___
Although size and shape of neurones differ in diff. parts of nervous system, they are basically similar.
- each neurone consists of a cell body containing nucleus and cytoplasm, and a number of nerve fibres.
- nerve fibre is a strand of cytoplasm extending from the cell body.
