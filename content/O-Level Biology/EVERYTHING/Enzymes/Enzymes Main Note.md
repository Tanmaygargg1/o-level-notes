>[!definition] 
>Catalyst is a substance that can speed up the rate of chemical reaction, without itself being chemically changed at the end of the reaction. 
#BiologyDefinitions 

___
>[!definition] 
>Enzymes are proteins that function as biological catalysts. They catalyse or speed up the rate of chemical reactions. They remain unchanged at the end of the reaction
#BiologyDefinitions 

___
*Enzymes are made up of protein molecules, that are folded to take on 3D globular shapes.*
