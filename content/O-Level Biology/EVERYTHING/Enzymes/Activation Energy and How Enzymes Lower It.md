Activation Energy: Refers to the energy required to start a chemical reaction.
Enzymes -> provide alternative pathway with lower activation energy required to start the chemical reaction.
-> Enzymes catalyze chemical reactions, and they speed up the rate of chemical reactions by lowering the activation energy.
