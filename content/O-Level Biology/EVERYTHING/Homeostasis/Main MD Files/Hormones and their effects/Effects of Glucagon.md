Islets of Langerhans => increase secretion of hormone glucagon when concentration of blood glucose decreases below normal levels.
=> main target of glucagon is liver
Glucagon increases blood glucose concentration by stimulating:
* conversion of glycogen into glucose
* conversion of fats and amino acids into glucose

Insulin and glucagon together regulate blood glucose concentration.
This is an example of homeostatic mechanism
