### Heat Gained by Body
Heat is released within the body => result of metabolic activities. => such as cellular respiration
High levels of cellular respiration => takes place in muscles and liver.
=> thus large amount of heat is released in these organs. 
=> heat is distributed to rest of the body via bloodstream.

Body can also gain extra heat through:
* vigorous muscular excersise
* consumption of hot food
* Being in warm env => eg being outdoors

Excess heat needs to be removed from the body => if not one could die of overheating.

### Heat Lost by the Body
heat is lost:
* through the skin
* by evaporation of water in sweat from the surface of the skin
* in the feces and urine
* in the air that is exhaled

amount of blood flowing through skin capillaries *per unit time* affects heat loss through skin surface.
